# 财务报告模型改进备考核心：GASB 103 的五节 MD&A、不寻常或不频繁项目与自营基金小计及加州口径

> 个人学习笔记，不构成税务或会计建议。规则以 GASB Statement No. 103, Financial Reporting Model Improvements（2024 年 4 月发布）公布文本与 AICPA CPA Exam Blueprint（BAR 政府会计）现行口径为准。选题说明：经 ls／grep 实查，仓内 GASB 103 全仓零专篇（仅在 GASB 96 篇里被一句带过、没有本体专篇），GASB 68／75、96、101、102 均已有专篇，本篇补上财务报告模型改进这一格，与 102 号的风险披露、104 号的资本资产披露分工对照读。

## 一、定位：改的是报告模型，不是确认计量

### 中文

GASB 第 103 号公告《Financial Reporting Model Improvements》于 2024 年 4 月发布，对 2025 年 6 月 15 日之后开始的财政年度生效，鼓励提前适用；若基本政府提前适用，其组成部分单位应在同一年度一起执行。先把定位说清：103 号动的是第 34 号公告立起来的财务报告模型的五个部件——管理层讨论与分析（MD&A）、不寻常或不频繁项目、自营基金（proprietary fund）收益费用表的列报、主要组成部分单位信息、预算比较信息。它基本不创设新的确认与计量规则，改的是「怎么摆、怎么分、怎么讲」。过渡处理上，为与新规衔接而作的变更按第 100 号公告《会计变更与差错更正》作会计原则变更处理，含相应的列报与披露。考试抓手：看到 103 号，先问它改了哪一处列报，不要往确认计量上套；加州以 6 月 30 日为年结日的城市、县与学区，第一个适用年度就是截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日的财年。

### English

GASB Statement No. 103, Financial Reporting Model Improvements, was issued in April 2024 and is effective for fiscal years beginning after June 15, 2025, with earlier application encouraged; a primary government that applies early should have its component units implement in the same year. Its character: Statement 103 works on five components of the reporting model built by Statement 34 — management's discussion and analysis (MD&A), unusual or infrequent items, presentation of the proprietary fund statement of revenues, expenses, and changes in fund net position, major component unit information, and budgetary comparison information. It creates essentially no new recognition or measurement rules; it changes how things are displayed, classified, and explained. Changes adopted at transition to conform are reported as a change in accounting principle under Statement 100, Accounting Changes and Error Corrections, including its display and disclosure requirements. Exam shortcut: when Statement 103 appears, ask which presentation it changed, and do not force it into recognition and measurement. For California cities, counties, and school districts with a June 30 year-end, the first required year is the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026.

Su carácter: trabaja sobre cinco piezas del modelo de reporte de la Declaración 34 — el análisis y discusión de la gerencia (MD&A), las partidas inusuales o infrecuentes, la presentación del estado de ingresos, gastos y cambios en la posición neta de los fondos empresariales (proprietary), la información de unidades componentes principales y la información de comparación presupuestaria. Regla de examen: ante la n.º 103 pregunte qué presentación cambió, sin forzarla al reconocimiento. Para ciudades, condados y distritos escolares de California con cierre al 30 de junio, el primer año obligatorio es el ejercicio terminado el 30 de junio de 2026. (初译待校)

(初译待校)

## 二、MD&A：只准写五节，还要讲清为什么变

### 中文

MD&A 仍是基本财务报表之前的要求补充信息（RSI），仍要把基本政府与离散列报的组成部分单位区分开写。103 号最大的动作是把内容锁死在五个题目里：财务报表概览、财务摘要、详细分析、重大资本资产与长期融资活动、目前已知的事实、决定或条件。不属于这五节的信息不要往 MD&A 里塞，跨节重复的解释也应避免，套话式的样板文字明确不再被接受。变化分析的要求也抬高了：不能只报余额或经营结果变了多少、变了百分之几，必须详细解释为什么变。考试里最容易设的坑，就是给一段只列百分比、把明年的预算展望和与五节无关的宣传内容一并写进 MD&A 的文字——前者缺「为什么」，后者越界。记住分工：MD&A 讲主要政府的相关信息，细到附注里已有的明细不必在这里重抄一遍。

### English

MD&A remains required supplementary information (RSI) presented before the basic financial statements, and it still distinguishes the primary government from its discretely presented component units. The big move in Statement 103 is to confine the content to five topics: overview of the financial statements, financial summary, detailed analyses, significant capital asset and long-term financing activity, and currently known facts, decisions, or conditions. Information outside those five topics does not belong in MD&A, explanations repeated across sections should be avoided, and boilerplate language is expressly no longer acceptable. The analysis bar also rises: reporting by how much or by what percentage balances and results changed is not enough — the MD&A must explain in detail why they changed. The classic exam trap is an MD&A that lists only percentages while stuffing in next year's budget outlook and promotional material unrelated to the five topics: the first defect is the missing "why," the second is scope creep. Keep the division of labor in mind: MD&A covers relevant information about the primary government, and detail already in the notes need not be copied again here.

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

El MD&A sigue siendo información suplementaria requerida (RSI) antes de los estados básicos y sigue distinguiendo al gobierno primario de sus unidades componentes de presentación discreta. El gran movimiento de la n.º 103 es encerrar el contenido en cinco temas: panorama de los estados financieros, resumen financiero, análisis detallados, actividad significativa de activos de capital y financiamiento a largo plazo, y hechos, decisiones o condiciones actualmente conocidos. Lo que no pertenezca a esos cinco temas no va en el MD&A, deben evitarse explicaciones repetidas entre secciones y el lenguaje de plantilla (boilerplate) ya no se acepta expresamente. También sube la vara del análisis: no basta decir cuánto o qué porcentaje cambiaron los saldos y resultados; hay que explicar en detalle por qué cambiaron. La trampa clásica de examen es un MD&A que solo lista porcentajes y mete la perspectiva presupuestaria del año siguiente y material promocional ajeno a los cinco temas: el primer defecto es el «por qué» ausente, el segundo es salirse del alcance. División de trabajo: el MD&A trata la información relevante del gobierno primario y no necesita copiar el detalle que ya está en las notas. (初译待校)

### 한국어 (초역·初译待校)

MD&A는 여전히 기본 재무제표 앞에 놓이는 필수 보충정보(RSI)이며 본정부와 분리 표시 구성단위를 구분해 써야 합니다. 제103호의 가장 큰 움직임은 내용을 다섯 주제로 가두는 것입니다. 재무제표 개관, 재무 요약, 상세 분석, 중대한 자본자산 및 장기 자금조달 활동, 현재 알려진 사실·결정·조건이 그것입니다. 이 다섯 주제에 속하지 않는 정보는 MD&A에 넣지 말고, 섹션을 넘나드는 중복 설명은 피해야 하며, 상투적인 틀 문구(boilerplate)는 명시적으로 더 이상 허용되지 않습니다. 분석의 기준도 높아졌습니다. 잔액과 운영 결과가 얼마, 몇 퍼센트 변했는지만 적어서는 안 되고 왜 변했는지를 상세히 설명해야 합니다. 시험에서 가장 흔한 함정은 퍼센트만 나열하면서 다음 해 예산 전망과 다섯 주제와 무관한 홍보 내용을 함께 집어넣은 MD&A입니다. 앞부분은 '왜'가 빠진 결함이고 뒷부분은 범위 이탈입니다. 분업도 기억하십시오. MD&A는 본정부에 관한 관련 정보를 다루며 주석에 이미 있는 명세를 다시 베껴 올 필요는 없습니다. (初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ·初译待校)

MD&A vẫn là thông tin bổ sung bắt buộc (RSI) đặt trước các báo cáo tài chính cơ bản, và vẫn phân biệt chính quyền chính với các đơn vị thành phần trình bày riêng. Động thái lớn của số 103 là khóa nội dung trong năm chủ đề: tổng quan về báo cáo tài chính, tóm tắt tài chính, phân tích chi tiết, hoạt động tài sản vốn và tài trợ dài hạn đáng kể, và các sự kiện, quyết định hoặc điều kiện hiện đã biết. Thông tin ngoài năm chủ đề này không thuộc về MD&A, cần tránh giải thích lặp lại giữa các phần, và văn mẫu rập khuôn (boilerplate) bị nói rõ là không còn chấp nhận được. Thanh phân tích cũng được nâng lên: chỉ nói số dư và kết quả thay đổi bao nhiêu, bao nhiêu phần trăm là chưa đủ — phải giải thích chi tiết vì sao chúng thay đổi. Bẫy thi kinh điển là một MD&A chỉ liệt kê phần trăm, đồng thời nhồi triển vọng ngân sách năm sau và tài liệu quảng bá không liên quan đến năm chủ đề: lỗi đầu là thiếu cái «vì sao», lỗi sau là vượt phạm vi. Hãy nhớ phân công: MD&A lo thông tin liên quan của chính quyền chính, không cần chép lại chi tiết đã có trong thuyết minh. (初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·初译待校)

MD&A अब भी मूल वित्तीय विवरणों से पहले रखी जाने वाली आवश्यक अनुपूरक जानकारी (RSI) है, और अब भी प्राथमिक सरकार को उसकी पृथक्‌-प्रस्तुत घटक इकाइयों से अलग रखता है। कथन 103 का बड़ा कदम है विषय-वस्तु को पाँच विषयों में बंद करना: वित्तीय विवरणों का अवलोकन, वित्तीय सारांश, विस्तृत विश्लेषण, महत्वपूर्ण पूंजी संपत्ति व दीर्घकालिक वित्तपोषण गतिविधि, और वर्तमान में ज्ञात तथ्य, निर्णय या स्थितियाँ। इन पाँच विषयों से बाहर की जानकारी MD&A में नहीं आती, खंडों में दोहराई गई व्याख्या से बचना चाहिए, और प्रारूपीय (boilerplate) भाषा अब स्पष्ट रूप से स्वीकार्य नहीं है। विश्लेषण का स्तर भी ऊँचा हुआ है: केवल यह बताना कि शेष या परिणाम कितने या कितने प्रतिशत बदले, काफ़ी नहीं — विस्तार से समझाना होगा कि वे क्यों बदले। परीक्षा का क्लासिक जाल है ऐसा MD&A जो केवल प्रतिशत गिनाए और साथ में अगले वर्ष के बजट का परिदृश्य व पाँच विषयों से असंबंध प्रचार सामग्री ठूँस दे: पहली खामी गायब «क्यों» है, दूसरी दायरे से बाहर जाना। श्रम-विभाजन याद रखें: MD&A प्राथमिक सरकार की प्रासंगिक जानकारी संभालता है, और टिप्पणियों में पहले से मौजूद ब्योरा यहाँ फिर से नकल करने की ज़रूरत नहीं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)

## 三、不寻常或不频繁项目：二选一即可，单独摆在最后

### 中文

103 号用「不寻常或不频繁项目」（unusual or infrequent items）取代了旧的非常项目（extraordinary items）与特殊项目（special items）两套标签。定义沿用第 62 号公告的底盘：性质不寻常，指高度异常、与政府的通常活动明显无关或仅附带相关；发生不频繁，指按政府所处环境判断、未来可预见期间内合理预期不会再发生。关键在连接词——满足其中一项即可，不再要求同时满足两项，也不再单设一套「管理层控制之内」的特殊项目口径。列报要求很硬：每一项不寻常或不频繁项目相关的流入与流出，要在政府层面报表、政府基金报表与自营基金报表的资源流量表里单独列示，位置是资源净变动之前的最后一笔（或最后几笔）流量。做题时先分清性质题与列报价：定义问「或」，列报问「单独、最后」。

### English

Statement 103 replaces the old pair of labels — extraordinary items and special items — with a single category, unusual or infrequent items. The definitions carry over from Statement 62: unusual in nature means highly abnormal and clearly unrelated, or only incidentally related, to the government's ordinary activities; infrequent in occurrence means not reasonably expected to recur in the foreseeable future, judged in the government's environment. The operative word is the connector: meeting either test is enough. There is no longer a requirement to meet both, and no separate special-item track keyed to management control. The display rule is strict: the inflows and outflows related to each unusual or infrequent item are displayed separately as the last presented flow, or flows, of resources before the net change in resource flows, in the government-wide, governmental fund, and proprietary fund statements of resource flows. On the exam, separate the two question types: definitions ask "either/or," display asks "separately, and last."

## 四、自营基金报表：非经营先定义，补助单列，新小计必考

### 中文

自营基金的收益、费用与净资产变动表仍要区分经营与非经营，但 103 号不再让各政府自定口径，而是先把非经营定义出来，经营就是剩下的部分。非经营收入与费用包括五类：收受与提供的补助（subsidies）、对永久与定期捐赠基金的捐款、与融资相关的收入与费用、处置资本资产与存货取得的资源、投资收益与费用。补助的定义抓三点：从另一方或另一基金收到资源、且本基金不向对方提供商品或服务，资源直接或间接使本基金当前或未来的收费低于原本水平；向另一方或基金提供资源、对方不向本基金提供商品或服务、且可通过本基金当前或未来的定价政策收回；以及其他一切转让。新增的列报小计是硬考点：必须在列报其他非经营收入费用之前，先给出「经营损益与非资本性补助」的小计。顺序记成：经营收入减经营费用得经营损益，加非资本性补助得新小计，再列其他非经营项目，最后接不寻常或不频繁项目与净变动。

### English

The proprietary fund statement still distinguishes operating from nonoperating revenues and expenses, but Statement 103 stops letting each government define the split itself: nonoperating is defined first, and operating is what remains. Nonoperating revenues and expenses comprise five classes: subsidies received and provided, contributions to permanent and term endowments, revenues and expenses related to financing, resources from the disposal of capital assets and inventory, and investment income and expenses. A subsidy, in substance, is a resource received from another party or fund without the fund providing goods or services in return, which directly or indirectly keeps the fund's current or future fees lower than they otherwise would be; a resource provided to another party or fund on the same no-exchange basis that is recoverable through the fund's current or future pricing; and all other transfers. The new subtotal is a prime exam target: a subtotal for operating income (loss) and noncapital subsidies must be presented before other nonoperating revenues and expenses are reported. Memorize the order: operating revenues minus operating expenses gives operating income (loss); add noncapital subsidies to reach the new subtotal; then other nonoperating items; then unusual or infrequent items and the net change.

## 五、主要组成部分单位与预算比较：分开摆、只走 RSI、两列差异

### English

For major component units, Statement 103 requires each major discretely presented component unit to be presented separately in the reporting entity's statement of net position and statement of activities, as long as doing so does not reduce the readability of the statements; if separate columns would make the statements unreadable, combining statements of major component units are presented after the fund financial statements. The test is not "important enough to separate" — separate presentation is the default and readability is the only escape. Budgetary comparison information converges on a single channel: it is presented only as required supplementary information (RSI). Two variance columns are required: the variance between original and final budget amounts, and the variance between final budget and actual amounts, with explanations of significant variances in the notes to RSI. The standard combined exam trap puts the budgetary comparison back into the basic statements, shows only one variance column, or moves the variance explanations into the MD&A narrative — all three placements are wrong.

## 六、算例与收束：一条自营基金链算到底，加州口径

### 中文

教学算例（虚构数字）：某市自营水务基金本年经营收入 12,000,000、经营费用 10,500,000，经营损益为 12,000,000 − 10,500,000 ＝ 1,500,000；收到非资本性补助 800,000，新小计为 1,500,000 ＋ 800,000 ＝ 2,300,000；其他非经营项目为投资收益 200,000、利息费用 500,000、处置资本资产损失 100,000，净额 200,000 − 500,000 − 100,000 ＝ −400,000，小计推进到 2,300,000 − 400,000 ＝ 1,900,000；本年另有一笔不寻常或不频繁流出 600,000，单独摆在最后，净变动为 1,900,000 − 600,000 ＝ 1,300,000。预算差异算例：一般基金原始预算 50,000,000、最终预算 52,000,000，第一列差异 52,000,000 − 50,000,000 ＝ 2,000,000；实际 51,200,000，第二列差异 51,200,000 − 52,000,000 ＝ −800,000。两组数字本地复算勾稽一致。收束五句：MD&A 只写五节且要讲为什么变；不寻常或不频繁是「或」不是「且」、单独列在最后；非经营先定义、经营是剩余；新小计是经营损益加非资本性补助；预算比较只走 RSI 且两列差异、重大差异在 RSI 附注解释。加州口径：州与地方政府同样执行 GASB 准则，加州主计长办公室的 GASB 更新指引是地方政府落地的实务入口，6 月 30 日年结的政府自截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日的财年起按 103 号改版报表与 MD&A。

### English

Teaching example (fictional figures). A city's proprietary water fund reports operating revenues of 12,000,000 and operating expenses of 10,500,000, so operating income is 12,000,000 − 10,500,000 = 1,500,000. Noncapital subsidies received are 800,000, so the new subtotal is 1,500,000 + 800,000 = 2,300,000. Other nonoperating items are investment income of 200,000, interest expense of 500,000, and a loss on disposal of capital assets of 100,000, a net of 200,000 − 500,000 − 100,000 = −400,000, bringing the running total to 2,300,000 − 400,000 = 1,900,000. An unusual or infrequent outflow of 600,000 is displayed separately, last, so the net change is 1,900,000 − 600,000 = 1,300,000. Budget variance example: a general fund's original budget is 50,000,000 and its final budget is 52,000,000, so the first variance column is 52,000,000 − 50,000,000 = 2,000,000; actual results are 51,200,000, so the second variance column is 51,200,000 − 52,000,000 = −800,000. Both chains were recomputed locally and tie out. Five closing lines: MD&A is five topics only and must explain why things changed; unusual or infrequent is "or," not "and," and is displayed separately, last; nonoperating is defined first and operating is the remainder; the new subtotal is operating income (loss) plus noncapital subsidies; budgetary comparisons live only in RSI with two variance columns and significant-variance explanations in the RSI notes. California follows the same GASB standards; the State Controller's Office GASB update guidance is the practical entry point for local governments, and June 30 year-end governments restyle their statements and MD&A under Statement 103 beginning with the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026.

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·初译待校)

शिक्षण उदाहरण (काल्पनिक आँकड़े)। किसी शहर की स्वामित्व जल निधि का परिचालन राजस्व 12,000,000 और परिचालन व्यय 10,500,000 है, इसलिए परिचालन आय 12,000,000 − 10,500,000 = 1,500,000 है। प्राप्त ग़ैर-पूंजी सब्सिडी 800,000 है, इसलिए नया उपयोग 1,500,000 + 800,000 = 2,300,000 है। अन्य गैर-परिचालन मदें हैं निवेश आय 200,000, ब्याज व्यय 500,000 और पूंजी संपत्ति निपटान हानि 100,000, जिनका निवल 200,000 − 500,000 − 100,000 = −400,000 है, जिससे चलता योग 2,300,000 − 400,000 = 1,900,000 होता है। 600,000 का असामान्य या अनियमित बहिर्गम अलग से, अंत में दिखाया जाता है, इसलिए निवल परिवर्तन 1,900,000 − 600,000 = 1,300,000 है। बजट-अंतर उदाहरण: किसी सामान्य निधि का मूल बजट 50,000,000 और अंतिम बजट 52,000,000 है, तो पहला अंतर स्तंभ 52,000,000 − 50,000,000 = 2,000,000 है; वास्तविक परिणाम 51,200,000 है, तो दूसरा अंतर स्तंभ 51,200,000 − 52,000,000 = −800,000 है। दोनों शृंखलाएँ स्थानीय रूप से पुनः गिनी गईं और मेल खाती हैं। पाँच समापन पंक्तियाँ: MD&A केवल पाँच विषयों का है और क्यों बदला यह समझाना होगा; असामान्य या अनियमित «या» है, «और» नहीं, और अलग से अंत में दिखता है; गैर-परिचालन पहले परिभाषित होता है और परिचालन शेष है; नया उपयोग परिचालन आय (हानि) जमा ग़ैर-पूंजी सब्सिडी है; बजटीय तुलना केवल RSI में रहती है, अंतर के दो स्तंभों के साथ और महत्वपूर्ण अंतरों की व्याख्या RSI टिप्पणियों में होती है। कैलिफ़ोर्निया भी वही GASB मानक लागू करता है; राज्य नियंत्रक कार्यालय का GASB अद्यतन मार्गदर्शन स्थानीय सरकारों के लिए व्यावहारिक प्रवेश है, और 30 जून वर्षांत वाली सरकारें 30 जून 2026 को समाप्त वित्तीय वर्ष से कथन 103 के अनुसार अपने विवरण और MD&A नए रूप में बनाती हैं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)

*来源口径：GASB Statement No. 103, Financial Reporting Model Improvements（2024 年 4 月发布），范围涵盖 MD&A 五个题目、不寻常或不频繁项目的定义与单独列示、自营基金非经营定义与补助定义及「经营损益与非资本性补助」小计、主要组成部分单位单独列示与预算比较 RSI 及两列差异；生效日为 2025 年 6 月 15 日后开始的财政年度、鼓励提前适用，过渡按 Statement No. 100 作会计原则变更。定义底盘参 GASB Statement No. 62；加州落地参 California State Controller's Office 的 GASB 更新指引。算例为教学虚构数字，非引自任何真实政府报表。*
