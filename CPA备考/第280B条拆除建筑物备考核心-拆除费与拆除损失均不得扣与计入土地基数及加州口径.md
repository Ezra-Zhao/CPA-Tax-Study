# 第 280B 条拆除建筑物备考核心：拆除费与拆除损失双否认、计入土地基数与加州口径

导语：买下一块带旧楼的地、把楼拆掉重建，是开发和自用扩建里最常见的动作。账务直觉会说：拆楼花的钱是费用、旧楼没提完的基数是损失。但第 280B 条把这两笔一起堵死——拆除支出不得扣、拆除损失不得扣，统统计入土地的资本账户，等将来卖地时才回收。本选题经 ls 文件名＋grep 正文双查：第 280B 条在四板块文件名与正文、README 全零命中、无本体专篇；第 280A、280C、280E、280F、280G 各条在仓内已有专篇或本体篇，本篇补上第 280 系列最后一块条文本体。

## 中文主文

### 一、条文本体：两笔否认、一处去向

第 280B 条正文只有一款两项，结构干净。开头句是「在拆除任何建筑物（structure）的情况下」；第 (1) 项规定：本章下本可扣除的金额，不得由该建筑物的所有人或承租人（owner or lessee）就下列两笔扣除——(A) 为拆除所支出的任何金额，(B) 因拆除而遭受的任何损失；第 (2) 项定去向：第 (1) 项所述金额，视为可适当计入（properly chargeable to capital account）该建筑物所坐落土地的资本账户。三个考点一次说清：主体不限所有人、承租人拆自己承租的建筑物同样中招；否认的是两笔——拆的钱与被拆物的剩余基数损失；去向只有一个——土地基数。土地本身不可折旧，所以这两笔钱不是换个科目慢慢提，而是冻结在土地基数里，直到土地出售或交换时才经由利得计算回收。答题时把「计入土地基数」写成「资本化后折旧」就是送分给阅卷人。

### 二、「建筑物」看条例：Reg. 第 1.280B-1 条

条文用词是 structure，射程由 Reg. 第 1.280B-1 条 (b) 款锚定：structure 指建筑物（building），按第 1.48-1 条 (e)(1) 的定义，并包括该建筑物的结构组成部分（structural components，第 1.48-1 条 (e)(2) 口径）。该条例由 T.D. 8745 于 1997 年 12 月 30 日定版，(c) 款定其适用于 1997 年 12 月 30 日或其后开始的拆除。实务含义：拆的是整栋建筑物及其结构组成部分才进本条；拆非结构性的内部装修、设备，另按第 162、263 条 (a) 款与有形财产条例判断，不自动套第 280B 条。

### 三、沿革：1976 年只管历史建筑，1984 年扩到一切建筑物

第 280B 条由 1976 年《税收改革法》（Pub. L. 94-455）第 2124 节 (b)(1) 加入法典，最初只管经认证的历史建筑物（certified historic structure）——立法目的是不让税法补贴拆历史建筑。1984 年《赤字削减法》（Pub. L. 98-369）第 1063 节把射程扩到「任何建筑物」，标题里的 historic 字样一并删去。生效口径写在该法第 1063 节 (c) 款：修订适用于 1983 年 12 月 31 日之后结束的纳税年度，但不适用于 1984 年 7 月 19 日之前开始的拆除（经认证的历史建筑物除外）。考试若给 1984 年前后的拆除日期，先分这一刀：扩围前的普通建筑物拆除不归本条、回旧口径；扩围后开始的拆除，一律先进本条。

### 四、意图不再相关：与 Reg. 第 1.165-3 条旧口径的分工

本条扩围之前，拆除的税务处理看意图，口径在 Reg. 第 1.165-3 条：(a) 款——买房时就打算拆的，价款全额算土地成本，拆除不产生可扣损失；(b) 款——买下之后才决定拆的，拆除损失与拆除费用原本可按第 165 条等扣除。第 280B 条扩到一切建筑物之后，这套意图检验在拆除问题上退场：只要拆除落在条文射程内，不问意图是买前就有还是买后才生，拆除费与拆除损失两笔都不得扣、都进土地基数。Reg. 第 1.165-3 条 (a) 款的结论与本条恰好一致（都进土地），真正被本条改掉的是 (b) 款那一路。做题顺序因此固定：先问是不是「拆除建筑物」，是就直接套第 280B 条，不必再去查纳税人何时起意。

### 五、算例：两笔合计进土地，卖地时才回收

某公司以 500,000 美元买入一处物业，价款分摊为土地 200,000 美元、建筑物 300,000 美元。建筑物使用多年，累计折旧 120,000 美元，拆除时调整基数为 180,000 美元（300,000 − 120,000 ＝ 180,000）。公司花 40,000 美元把建筑物拆除、平整场地准备自建新楼。按第 280B 条：拆除造成的 180,000 美元损失不得扣，40,000 美元拆除费不得扣，两笔合计 220,000 美元（180,000 ＋ 40,000 ＝ 220,000）计入土地资本账户，土地基数由 200,000 美元抬到 420,000 美元（200,000 ＋ 220,000 ＝ 420,000）。若干年后公司以 700,000 美元卖掉这块地（未建新楼），利得是 700,000 − 420,000 ＝ 280,000 美元——当年被堵住的 220,000 美元，是在这里以少认利得的方式回收的，不是凭空消失。对照旧口径 (b) 款（买后起意、本条扩围前）：同样的事实当年可扣 220,000 美元。一正一反，正是本条的立法效果：把拆除的税务好处从「当年扣除」推迟到「卖地减利得」。

### 六、三条边界，别把本条用过头

第一，灾害例外。Notice 90-21（1990-1 C.B. 332）明确：第 280B 条的资本化规则不适用于因灾害（casualty）受损或被毁建筑物的拆除支出、以及因该拆除遭受的损失——那条线回第 165 条灾害损失本轨处理，先分清楼是自己拆的还是灾毁后清的。第二，部分拆除与改造。只拆一部分、或拆除是整体翻修计划一环的，落点是第 263 条 (a) 款与有形财产条例的资本化分析（翻修计划口径），不是把整笔都按第 280B 条塞进土地；新建筑物本身的建造成本另按第 263、168 条资本化折旧，与本条进土地的两笔分开记账。第三，弃置不等于拆除。建筑物永久弃置、退出使用但不拆的，按第 165 条弃置口径另行判断，不进本条；反过来，只要动工拆了，就回本条，不许改名弃置绕行。

### 七、加州口径

加州没有另设一套州版拆除扣除规则，实务上随联邦口径把拆除费与拆除损失计入土地资本账户，土地基数抬高后，将来卖地的州侧利得也随之变小；具体申报以 FTB 当期说明为准（证据等级 B；联邦条文与条例本体 A 级）。

个人学习笔记，不构成税务建议。条文以 26 U.S.C. 第 280B 条（govinfo 2024 年版法典文本）与 Reg. 第 1.280B-1 条（T.D. 8745）现行文本为准，沿革据 Pub. L. 94-455 第 2124 节与 Pub. L. 98-369 第 1063 节，灾害例外据 Notice 90-21。算例本地复算两遍一致：300,000 − 120,000 ＝ 180,000；180,000 ＋ 40,000 ＝ 220,000；200,000 ＋ 220,000 ＝ 420,000；700,000 − 420,000 ＝ 280,000。

## English

Section 280B is the last untested corner of the section 280 family in this repository, and it is short enough to memorize whole. When any structure is demolished, section 280B(1) denies the owner or lessee of the structure any deduction otherwise allowable under chapter 1 for two amounts: (A) any amount expended for the demolition, and (B) any loss sustained on account of the demolition. Section 280B(2) then sends both amounts to one place only — they are treated as properly chargeable to the capital account with respect to the land on which the demolished structure was located. Because land is not depreciable, the two amounts are frozen in the land basis and recovered only when the land is sold or exchanged, as a smaller gain; writing "capitalized and depreciated" on an exam answer gives the point away. Reg. section 1.280B-1(b) (T.D. 8745, December 30, 1997; effective for demolitions commencing on or after that date) defines "structure" as a building under Reg. section 1.48-1(e)(1), including its structural components under section 1.48-1(e)(2). The section began narrow: added by section 2124(b)(1) of the Tax Reform Act of 1976 (Pub. L. 94-455), it originally reached only certified historic structures; section 1063 of the Deficit Reduction Act of 1984 (Pub. L. 98-369) extended it to any structure, applicable to taxable years ending after December 31, 1983 but not to demolitions (other than of certified historic structures) commencing before July 19, 1984. Before that expansion, Reg. section 1.165-3 asked about intent: under paragraph (a), property bought with the intention to demolish took a full land basis with no demolition loss; under paragraph (b), an intent formed after acquisition once allowed the loss and the demolition cost. Section 280B retires the intent inquiry for demolitions within its scope — whenever the intent arose, both amounts are denied and added to land basis. Worked example, verified twice locally: a company buys a property for $500,000, allocated $200,000 to land and $300,000 to the building; accumulated depreciation of $120,000 leaves an adjusted basis of $180,000 ($300,000 − $120,000 = $180,000); demolition costs $40,000. Neither the $180,000 loss nor the $40,000 cost is deductible; the combined $220,000 ($180,000 + $40,000) raises the land basis to $420,000 ($200,000 + $220,000), and a later sale of the bare land for $700,000 produces a gain of only $280,000 ($700,000 − $420,000) — the denied amounts are recovered there, not lost. Three boundaries keep the section from being overused. First, Notice 90-21 (1990-1 C.B. 332) takes demolition of a structure damaged or destroyed by casualty outside section 280B's capitalization rule, sending that line back to section 165 casualty treatment. Second, partial demolition or demolition as one step of a renovation plan is analyzed under section 263(a) and the tangible property regulations, not swept into land under section 280B, and the cost of the new building is separately capitalized and depreciated under sections 263 and 168. Third, permanent abandonment without demolition is a section 165 question, not a section 280B question — but once demolition actually happens, the section applies and relabeling does not help. California has no separate state demolition-deduction regime and in practice follows the federal capitalization into land basis, which also shrinks the state-side gain on a later land sale; check current FTB guidance for any specific filing (evidence grade B; the federal statute and regulation are grade A). *Personal study notes; not tax advice.*

## Español (初译待校)

El artículo 280B es el último rincón de la familia del artículo 280 en este repositorio y es lo bastante breve para memorizarlo entero. Cuando se demuele cualquier estructura, el § 280B(1) niega al propietario o arrendatario de la estructura toda deducción por dos montos: (A) cualquier cantidad gastada en la demolición y (B) cualquier pérdida sufrida a causa de la demolición. El § 280B(2) envía ambos montos a un solo lugar: se tratan como cargables a la cuenta de capital respecto del terreno sobre el que se alzaba la estructura. Como el terreno no se deprecia, ambos montos quedan congelados en la base del terreno y solo se recuperan al venderlo o intercambiarlo, como una ganancia menor. El Reg. § 1.280B-1(b) (T.D. 8745, 30 de diciembre de 1997) define «structure» como un edificio según el § 1.48-1(e)(1), incluidos sus componentes estructurales del § 1.48-1(e)(2). La norma nació estrecha: la añadió el § 2124(b)(1) de la Ley de Reforma Fiscal de 1976 (Pub. L. 94-455) solo para estructuras históricas certificadas; el § 1063 de la Ley de Reducción del Déficit de 1984 (Pub. L. 98-369) la extendió a cualquier estructura, aplicable a años fiscales terminados después del 31 de diciembre de 1983 pero no a demoliciones (salvo de estructuras históricas certificadas) comenzadas antes del 19 de julio de 1984. Antes de esa ampliación, el Reg. § 1.165-3 preguntaba por la intención: comprado con intención de demoler, todo el precio era base del terreno sin pérdida deducible (apartado (a)); con intención posterior, la pérdida y el costo eran deducibles (apartado (b)). El § 280B retira esa indagación para las demoliciones en su ámbito. Ejemplo verificado dos veces: compra por 500.000 dólares (terreno 200.000, edificio 300.000); depreciación acumulada de 120.000 deja base ajustada de 180.000; la demolición cuesta 40.000. Ni la pérdida de 180.000 ni el costo de 40.000 son deducibles; los 220.000 combinados elevan la base del terreno a 420.000, y una venta posterior del terreno por 700.000 produce una ganancia de solo 280.000. Tres límites: el Notice 90-21 (1990-1 C.B. 332) excluye de la capitalización la demolición de estructuras dañadas o destruidas por siniestro (vuelve al § 165); la demolición parcial o integrada en un plan de renovación se analiza bajo el § 263(a) y el reglamento de propiedad tangible; y el abandono permanente sin demolición es cuestión del § 165, no del § 280B. California no tiene un régimen estatal propio y en la práctica sigue la capitalización federal en la base del terreno (grado de evidencia B; el texto federal es grado A). *Notas personales de estudio; no constituyen asesoría fiscal.* (初译待校)

## Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Điều 280B là góc cuối cùng của họ Điều 280 trong kho này và đủ ngắn để học thuộc toàn văn. Khi bất kỳ công trình (structure) nào bị phá dỡ, § 280B(1) từ chối cho chủ sở hữu hoặc người thuê (owner or lessee) khấu trừ hai khoản: (A) mọi số tiền chi cho việc phá dỡ và (B) mọi khoản lỗ do phá dỡ gây ra. § 280B(2) đưa cả hai khoản về một nơi duy nhất: tính vào tài khoản vốn (capital account) của thửa đất nơi công trình tọa lạc. Vì đất không khấu hao được, hai khoản bị đóng băng trong cơ sở tính giá của đất và chỉ thu hồi khi bán hoặc trao đổi đất, dưới dạng lãi nhỏ hơn. Reg. § 1.280B-1(b) (T.D. 8745, 30 tháng 12 năm 1997) định nghĩa structure là tòa nhà (building) theo § 1.48-1(e)(1), bao gồm các thành phần kết cấu theo § 1.48-1(e)(2). Điều này khởi đầu hẹp: § 2124(b)(1) của Đạo luật Cải cách Thuế 1976 (Pub. L. 94-455) chỉ áp cho công trình lịch sử được chứng nhận; § 1063 của Đạo luật Giảm Thâm hụt 1984 (Pub. L. 98-369) mở rộng ra mọi công trình, áp dụng cho năm tính thuế kết thúc sau 31 tháng 12 năm 1983 nhưng không áp cho việc phá dỡ (trừ công trình lịch sử được chứng nhận) bắt đầu trước 19 tháng 7 năm 1984. Trước khi mở rộng, Reg. § 1.165-3 hỏi về ý định: mua với ý định phá dỡ thì toàn bộ giá là cơ sở của đất, không có lỗ khấu trừ (khoản (a)); ý định hình thành sau khi mua từng cho khấu trừ lỗ và chi phí phá dỡ (khoản (b)). Điều 280B bãi bỏ việc xét ý định đối với phá dỡ trong phạm vi của nó. Ví dụ đã kiểm hai lần tại chỗ: mua 500.000 đô la, phân bổ đất 200.000 và tòa nhà 300.000; khấu hao lũy kế 120.000 để lại cơ sở đã điều chỉnh 180.000; phá dỡ tốn 40.000. Cả khoản lỗ 180.000 lẫn chi phí 40.000 đều không được khấu trừ; tổng 220.000 nâng cơ sở của đất lên 420.000, và bán thửa đất trống sau đó với 700.000 chỉ tạo lãi 280.000. Ba ranh giới: Notice 90-21 (1990-1 C.B. 332) loại việc phá dỡ công trình bị hư hại hoặc phá hủy do tai họa (casualty) khỏi quy tắc vốn hóa, đưa về Điều 165; phá dỡ một phần hoặc phá dỡ trong kế hoạch cải tạo được phân tích theo § 263(a) và quy định về tài sản hữu hình; bỏ hoang vĩnh viễn mà không phá dỡ là vấn đề của Điều 165 chứ không phải Điều 280B. California không có chế độ khấu trừ phá dỡ riêng của bang và trong thực hành theo cách vốn hóa liên bang vào cơ sở của đất (cấp bằng chứng B; văn bản liên bang là cấp A). ## हिन्दी (初译待校)

धारा 280B इस भंडार में धारा 280 परिवार का अंतिम अछूता कोना है और इतनी छोटी है कि पूरा पाठ याद किया जा सकता है। किसी भी structure के demolition पर § 280B(1) उस structure के owner या lessee को दो राशियों की कटौती से वंचित करता है: (A) demolition पर ख़र्च की गई कोई भी राशि, और (B) demolition के कारण उठाया गया कोई भी नुक़सान। § 280B(2) दोनों राशियों को केवल एक जगह भेजता है — वे उस land के संबंध में capital account में chargeable मानी जाती हैं जिस पर demolished structure स्थित था। Land depreciable नहीं है, इसलिए दोनों राशियाँ land basis में जम जाती हैं और केवल land की बिक्री या exchange पर, कम gain के रूप में, वसूल होती हैं। Reg. § 1.280B-1(b) (T.D. 8745, 30 दिसंबर 1997) structure को § 1.48-1(e)(1) के अंतर्गत building के रूप में, उसके structural components (§ 1.48-1(e)(2)) सहित, परिभाषित करता है। यह धारा संकरी शुरुआत से आई: Tax Reform Act 1976 (Pub. L. 94-455) के § 2124(b)(1) ने इसे केवल certified historic structures तक रखा था; Deficit Reduction Act 1984 (Pub. L. 98-369) के § 1063 ने इसे किसी भी structure तक बढ़ाया, जो 31 दिसंबर 1983 के बाद समाप्त होने वाले कर-वर्षों पर लागू है किंतु 19 जुलाई 1984 से पहले शुरू हुए demolitions (certified historic structures को छोड़कर) पर नहीं। उस विस्तार से पहले Reg. § 1.165-3 इरादा पूछता था: paragraph (a) के तहत demolition के इरादे से ख़रीदी गई संपत्ति का पूरा मूल्य land cost होता था और कोई loss नहीं मिलता था; paragraph (b) के तहत ख़रीद के बाद बना इरादा कभी loss और demolition cost की कटौती देता था। § 280B अपने दायरे के demolitions के लिए इरादे की जाँच समाप्त कर देता है। उदाहरण, स्थानीय रूप से दो बार सत्यापित: $500,000 में ख़रीद, land $200,000 और building $300,000 में बँटवारा; $120,000 accumulated depreciation के बाद adjusted basis $180,000; demolition लागत $40,000। न $180,000 का loss कटेगा, न $40,000 की लागत; संयुक्त $220,000 land basis को $420,000 तक बढ़ाते हैं, और बाद में ख़ाली land की $700,000 में बिक्री पर gain केवल $280,000 बनता है। तीन सीमाएँ: Notice 90-21 (1990-1 C.B. 332) casualty से क्षतिग्रस्त या नष्ट structure के demolition को capitalization नियम से बाहर रखकर § 165 की ओर भेजता है; आंशिक demolition या renovation plan का हिस्सा demolition § 263(a) और tangible property regulations के तहत परखा जाता है; और बिना demolition के स्थायी abandonment § 165 का प्रश्न है, § 280B का नहीं। California का अपना राज्य-स्तरीय demolition deduction regime नहीं है और व्यवहार में वह federal capitalization को land basis में अपनाता है (साक्ष्य ग्रेड B; federal पाठ ग्रेड A)। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा प्रतीक्षित)
