# 开车办事能按多少扣：2026 年标准里程率上半年 72.5 美分、7 月 1 日起 76 美分，四种用途四把尺与记录要求及加州口径

标准里程率是 IRS 每年公布的一把简易尺：不算油费、保险、折旧的实际账，直接拿合格里程乘一个官方单价，就得到可扣的车辆费用。2026 年特殊在年中改了一次价——上半年一个价、7 月 1 日起另一个价，全年记账要分两段。本篇管这把尺的本体：四个单价、四种用途、选用规则与记录要求；仓内《网约车与外卖骑手怎么报税》篇管骑手一侧的记账实务、《医疗费用能不能扣税》篇管第 213 条门槛、《搬家费用还能扣税吗》篇管第 217 条暂停线，三篇以指针分工。

依据：IRC § 62(a)(2)、§ 67、§ 162、§ 170(i)、§ 213、§ 217(g)、§ 274(d)｜Rev. Proc. 2019-46｜IRS Notice 2026-10（IR-2025-128，2025-12-29 发布）｜IRS Announcement 2026-11（I.R.B. 2026-29，2026-07-13 发布，修订 Notice 2026-10）｜Reg. § 1.62-2

## 一、2026 年的价目表：一年两段价，年中涨过一次

### 中文

自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起（Notice 2026-10）：经营用途每英里 72.5 美分，比 2025 年涨 2.5 美分；医疗用途 20.5 美分，降 0.5 美分；搬家用途 20.5 美分，降 0.5 美分；慈善用途 14 美分，与 2025 年持平。自 2026 年 7 月 1 日起，Announcement 2026-11 修订上半年口径：经营升到 76 美分，医疗与搬家升到 23.5 美分，公告写明原因是油价上涨；慈善率由第 170(i) 条法定钉死在 14 美分，不在这次修订的射程内。切段口径按费用发生日走：7 月 1 日之前发生的运输费用用旧率、7 月 1 日起发生的用新率；里程报销想用新率，还要报销本身在 7 月 1 日之后支付、且对应费用在 7 月 1 日之后发生，两头都占才行——3 月开的车、8 月才报销，仍按 72.5 美分算。适用车辆是轿车、厢型车、皮卡与面板卡车，纯电、混动与燃油车同价。两个价位差的来历也说清：经营率按固定成本加变动成本的年度研究定，医疗与搬家率只按其中的变动成本定，所以低一截，不是同一把尺打折。

### English

From January 1, 2026 (Notice 2026-10): 72.5 cents per mile for business use, up 2.5 cents from 2025; 20.5 cents for medical purposes, down half a cent; 20.5 cents for moving purposes, down half a cent; and 14 cents for charitable use, unchanged from 2025. From July 1, 2026, Announcement 2026-11 revised the first-half figures: business rose to 76 cents, and medical and moving rose to 23.5 cents, with the announcement citing higher fuel prices as the reason; the charitable rate is fixed by § 170(i) at 14 cents and sits outside the revision. The split follows the date the expense is incurred: transportation expenses incurred before July 1 take the old rate, those incurred on or after July 1 take the new one. A mileage allowance gets the new rate only if it is both paid on or after July 1 and paid for expenses incurred on or after July 1 — driving in March and being reimbursed in August still prices at 72.5 cents. The rates cover cars, vans, pickups, and panel trucks, and apply equally to fully electric, hybrid, gasoline, and diesel vehicles. The gap between the price tiers has a simple origin: the business rate is built from an annual study of fixed plus variable costs, while the medical and moving rates reflect only the variable costs in that study — they are lower by construction, not a discount on the same ruler.

## 二、四种用途四把尺：同一英里，四条扣除线

### 中文

经营用途走第 162 条：自雇者的经营里程在 Schedule C（或 E、F）上乘经营率扣除，是这把尺用得最多的地方。医疗用途走第 213 条：为就医开车的里程乘医疗率，进 Schedule A 与其他医疗费用合算、一起过 7.5% AGI 门槛。慈善用途走第 170 条：为合格慈善组织无偿服务开的车按法定 14 美分，只有走逐项扣除的人用得上。搬家用途如今只剩第 217(g) 条一小扇门：现役军人因长期换防军令搬家，外加 OBBBA（P.L. 119-21）第 70113(b) 节新增的部分情报界成员；其余人的搬家扣除已被同法第 70113(a) 节永久取消，这把尺对普通人不存在。雇员未报销的工作里程在联邦层面已经关门：OBBBA 第 70110 节把第 67 条杂项逐项扣除的取消永久化，普通雇员不能再在联邦税表上扣自掏腰包的工作里程；只剩第 62(a)(2) 条线上调整的人群（预备役军人、按费收费的州与地方官员、特定表演艺术家）与教育者费用通道还能用上经营率。最后一刀是通勤：家到固定工作地点的往返，任何用途下都不算合格里程，这是所有里程账的第一道剔除线。

### English

Business miles run through § 162: a self-employed taxpayer multiplies business mileage by the business rate on Schedule C (or E or F), which is where this ruler gets most of its use. Medical miles run through § 213: miles driven for medical care are priced at the medical rate and pooled on Schedule A with other medical expenses, together clearing the 7.5%-of-AGI floor. Charitable miles run through § 170: driving in gratuitous service to a qualified charitable organization is priced at the statutory 14 cents, usable only by taxpayers who itemize. Moving miles now survive only through the small door of § 217(g): active-duty service members relocating under permanent-change-of-station orders, plus certain members of the intelligence community added by § 70113(b) of the OBBBA (P.L. 119-21); for everyone else the moving deduction was made permanently disallowed by § 70113(a) of the same act, so that ruler no longer exists for ordinary taxpayers. Unreimbursed employee driving is closed at the federal level: § 70110 of the OBBBA made permanent the disallowance of § 67 miscellaneous itemized deductions, so an ordinary employee can no longer deduct out-of-pocket work mileage on a federal return; the business rate remains reachable only by the above-the-line groups in § 62(a)(2) (reservists, fee-basis state and local officials, and certain performing artists) and through the educator-expense channel. The final cut is commuting: travel between home and a regular workplace is never a qualified mile under any purpose — the first exclusion line in every mileage log.

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

Las millas de negocios pasan por el § 162: el trabajador por cuenta propia multiplica su millaje de negocios por la tasa de negocios en el Anexo C (o E o F), que es donde esta regla más se usa. Las millas médicas pasan por el § 213: conducir para recibir atención médica se valora a la tasa médica y se suma en el Anexo A con los demás gastos médicos, superando en conjunto el piso del 7,5 % del AGI. Las millas benéficas pasan por el § 170: conducir prestando servicios gratuitos a una organización benéfica calificada se valora a los 14 centavos legales, y solo sirve a quien detalla deducciones. Las millas de mudanza hoy sobreviven solo por la puerta pequeña del § 217(g): militares en servicio activo que se trasladan por órdenes de cambio permanente de destino, más ciertos miembros de la comunidad de inteligencia añadidos por el § 70113(b) de la OBBBA (P.L. 119-21); para todos los demás, la deducción por mudanza quedó eliminada de forma permanente por el § 70113(a) de la misma ley, así que esa regla ya no existe para el contribuyente común. El millaje laboral no reembolsado del empleado está cerrado a nivel federal: el § 70110 de la OBBBA hizo permanente la denegación de las deducciones detalladas misceláneas del § 67, de modo que un empleado común ya no puede deducir en su declaración federal las millas de trabajo pagadas de su bolsillo; la tasa de negocios solo sigue al alcance de los grupos con ajuste sobre la línea del § 62(a)(2) (reservistas, funcionarios estatales y locales pagados por honorarios y ciertos artistas intérpretes) y del canal de gastos del educador. El último corte es el desplazamiento diario: ir y volver entre la casa y el lugar de trabajo habitual nunca es milla calificada bajo ningún propósito; es la primera línea de exclusión de todo registro de millas.

### 한국어 (초역·初译待校)

사업 마일은 제162조를 통합니다. 자영업자는 Schedule C(또는 E, F)에서 사업 마일에 사업율을 곱해 공제하며, 이 자가 가장 많이 쓰이는 곳입니다. 의료 마일은 제213조를 통합니다. 진료를 받으러 운전한 마일은 의료율로 계산해 Schedule A에서 다른 의료비와 합산하고, 함께 AGI 7.5% 문턱을 넘겨야 합니다. 자선 마일은 제170조를 통합니다. 자격 있는 자선 단체에 무상으로 봉사하며 운전한 마일은 법정 14센트로 계산하며, 항목별 공제를 선택한 사람만 쓸 수 있습니다. 이사 마일은 이제 제217(g)조의 작은 문으로만 남았습니다. 영구 근무지 변경 명령으로 이사하는 현역 군인과, OBBBA(P.L. 119-21) 제70113(b)절이 더한 일부 정보기관 구성원이 대상이고, 그 밖의 사람의 이사 공제는 같은 법 제70113(a)절로 영구히 폐지되어 일반 납세자에게 이 자는 더 이상 존재하지 않습니다. 직원의 미상환 업무 마일은 연방 차원에서 문이 닫혔습니다. OBBBA 제70110절이 제67조 잡다한 항목별 공제의 배제를 영구화했기 때문에, 일반 직원은 자기 돈으로 낸 업무 마일을 연방 신고서에서 공제할 수 없습니다. 사업율을 계속 쓸 수 있는 것은 제62(a)(2)조의 조정 대상 집단(예비군, 수수료 기준으로 보수를 받는 주·지방 공무원, 특정 공연 예술가)과 교육자 비용 통로뿐입니다. 마지막 한 칼은 통근입니다. 집과 고정 근무지 사이의 왕복은 어떤 용도로도 자격 있는 마일이 아니며, 모든 마일 기록의 첫 번째 제외선입니다.

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ·初译待校)

Dặm kinh doanh đi qua § 162: người tự kinh doanh nhân số dặm kinh doanh với mức kinh doanh trên Schedule C (hoặc E, F), và đây là nơi thước đo này được dùng nhiều nhất. Dặm từ thiện đi qua § 170: lái xe phục vụ miễn phí cho một tổ chức từ thiện đủ điều kiện được tính theo mức luật định 14 xu, và chỉ người khấu trừ theo từng khoản mới dùng được. Dặm chuyển nhà nay chỉ còn qua cánh cửa nhỏ của § 217(g): quân nhân tại ngũ chuyển chỗ theo lệnh thay đổi nơi đóng quân lâu dài, cộng thêm một số thành viên cộng đồng tình báo do § 70113(b) của OBBBA (P.L. 119-21) bổ sung; với mọi người khác, khoản khấu trừ chuyển nhà đã bị § 70113(a) của cùng đạo luật bãi bỏ vĩnh viễn, nên thước đo đó không còn tồn tại với người nộp thuế thông thường. Dặm làm việc không được hoàn trả của người lao động đã đóng ở cấp liên bang: § 70110 của OBBBA biến việc bãi bỏ các khoản khấu trừ từng khoản linh tinh theo § 67 thành vĩnh viễn, nên nhân viên thông thường không còn khấu trừ dặm làm việc tự bỏ tiền túi trên tờ khai liên bang; mức kinh doanh chỉ còn tới được với các nhóm điều chỉnh trên dòng theo § 62(a)(2) (quân dự bị, công chức tiểu bang và địa phương hưởng phí theo vụ việc, một số nghệ sĩ biểu diễn) và qua kênh chi phí của nhà giáo dục. Nhát cắt cuối cùng là đi làm hằng ngày: quãng đường giữa nhà và nơi làm việc cố định không bao giờ là dặm đủ điều kiện dưới bất kỳ mục đích nào — đó là dòng loại trừ đầu tiên trong mọi sổ ghi dặm.

व्यावसायिक मील § 162 से गुज़रते हैं: स्व-नियोजित करदाता Schedule C (या E या F) पर व्यावसायिक माइलेज को व्यावसायिक दर से गुणा करके कटौती करता है, और यही इस पैमाने का सबसे बड़ा उपयोग है। चिकित्सा मील § 213 से गुज़रते हैं: इलाज के लिए चलाई गई दूरी चिकित्सा दर से गिनी जाती है और Schedule A पर अन्य चिकित्सा ख़र्चों के साथ मिलकर AGI की 7.5% दहलीज़ पार करती है। धर्मार्थ मील § 170 से गुज़रते हैं: किसी योग्य धर्मार्थ संगठन की निःशुल्क सेवा में चलाई गई दूरी विधि-निर्धारित 14 सेंट से गिनी जाती है, और यह केवल मदवार कटौती लेने वालों के काम की है। स्थानांतरण मील अब केवल § 217(g) के छोटे दरवाज़े से बचे हैं: स्थायी तैनाती-परिवर्तन आदेशों पर स्थानांतरित होने वाले सक्रिय सैनिक, और OBBBA (P.L. 119-21) की धारा 70113(b) द्वारा जोड़े गए ख़ुफ़िया समुदाय के कुछ सदस्य; बाक़ी सबके लिए स्थानांतरण कटौती उसी अधिनियम की धारा 70113(a) से स्थायी रूप से समाप्त हो गई है, इसलिए आम करदाता के लिए वह पैमाना अब मौजूद नहीं। कर्मचारी की अप्रतिपूर्त कार्य-दूरी संघीय स्तर पर बंद हो चुकी है: OBBBA की धारा 70110 ने § 67 की विविध मदवार कटौतियों के अस्वीकरण को स्थायी बना दिया, इसलिए साधारण कर्मचारी अब जेब से चुकाई कार्य-दूरी संघीय रिटर्न पर नहीं काट सकता; व्यावसायिक दर केवल § 62(a)(2) के ऊपर-रेखा समूहों (रिज़र्व सैनिक, शुल्क-आधार पर वेतन पाने वाले राज्य व स्थानीय अधिकारी, कुछ प्रदर्शन कलाकार) और शिक्षक-ख़र्च मार्ग से ही उपलब्ध रहती है। अंतिम काट आवागमन है: घर और नियमित कार्यस्थल के बीच का आना-जाना किसी भी उद्देश्य में योग्य मील नहीं है — यह हर माइलेज लॉग की पहली अपवर्जन रेखा है।

## 三、怎么用：二选一的规矩、折旧成分与记录

### English

The standard rate is an optional shortcut; a taxpayer may instead track actual costs, but choosing it comes with gates. For an owned car, the standard mileage rate must be chosen in the first year the car is available for business use; in later years the taxpayer may switch between the standard rate and actual expenses. For a leased car, choosing the standard rate locks it in for the entire lease period, including renewals. The standard rate is unavailable if the taxpayer has claimed a § 179 deduction or depreciation other than straight-line on the same car, or operates five or more automobiles at the same time in a fleet-style operation (section 4 of Rev. Proc. 2019-46 and Publication 463). On top of the standard amount, parking fees and tolls may be added separately. One easily missed entry: the business rate embeds a depreciation component — 35 cents per mile for 2026 (section 4 of Notice 2026-10) — so for each year the standard rate is used, the car's basis must be reduced by business miles times 35 cents, and that slice of basis cannot be recovered a second time when the car is sold. Records follow the § 274(d) substantiation standard: date, mileage, destination, and business purpose, written down as you go rather than reconstructed from memory at year-end. Employer reimbursement runs on a separate track: under an accountable plan, a mileage allowance paid at or below the federal standard rate is deemed substantiated and stays out of wages, while any excess over the standard rate goes into wages (Reg. § 1.62-2 and the substantiation rules of Rev. Proc. 2019-46). An employer using a fixed-and-variable-rate (FAVR) plan works from a maximum standard automobile cost of $61,700 for 2026 (section 5 of Notice 2026-10).

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

## 四、算例：分两段乘，别拿一个价乘全年

### 中文

自雇司机全年经营里程 16,000 英里，上半年 8,000 英里、下半年 8,000 英里：上半年 8,000 × 0.725 ＝ 5,800 美元，下半年 8,000 × 0.76 ＝ 6,080 美元，全年可扣 11,880 美元；同年基数减额为 16,000 × 0.35 ＝ 5,600 美元。医疗里程上半年 600 英里、下半年 400 英里：600 × 0.205 ＝ 123 美元，400 × 0.235 ＝ 94 美元，合计 217 美元，再进 Schedule A 与其他医疗费用合并过 7.5% 门槛。慈善里程 1,200 英里 × 0.14 ＝ 168 美元。报销一笔：雇主按下半年每英里 80 美分给 1,000 英里付了 800 美元，其中按标准率 76 美分对应的 760 美元在责任制计划下免税，超出的 40 美元进工资。四个算例的共同点是先切段、再乘率——2026 年谁把 76 美分乘满全年，谁的账就是错的。

### English

A self-employed driver logs 16,000 business miles in the year, 8,000 in the first half and 8,000 in the second: the first half gives 8,000 × 0.725 = $5,800, the second half 8,000 × 0.76 = $6,080, for a total deduction of $11,880; the same year's basis reduction is 16,000 × 0.35 = $5,600. Medical miles of 600 in the first half and 400 in the second give 600 × 0.205 = $123 and 400 × 0.235 = $94, totaling $217, which then goes onto Schedule A to clear the 7.5% floor together with other medical expenses. Charitable miles of 1,200 price at 1,200 × 0.14 = $168. ### English

California publishes no mileage rate of its own; state agencies and reimbursement practice anchor on the federal standard rates (evidence grade B). But California differs from the federal rules in two visible places. On the employer side, California Labor Code § 2802 requires an employer to indemnify employees for necessary expenditures incurred in discharging their duties, so mileage reimbursement in California is a statutory right of the employee rather than a company perk; the method need not be per-mile, but it must fully cover the actual costs (the Gattuso case, California Supreme Court, 2007; evidence grade B). On the employee-deduction side, the door the federal system closed is still open in California: the state does not conform to the § 67 disallowance, so unreimbursed employee expenses can still be figured on Form 2106 using California amounts and carried onto Schedule CA (540), line 19, into California itemized deductions, pooled under the 2%-of-federal-AGI floor (per the FTB's Schedule CA instructions; evidence grade B). A closing checklist: keep the mileage log split between the first and second halves of the year; strip out commuting miles before recording purposes; keep reimbursement slips and the log cross-referenced so the amounts tie; and before selling a car, total the depreciation-component reductions accumulated over every standard-rate year.
