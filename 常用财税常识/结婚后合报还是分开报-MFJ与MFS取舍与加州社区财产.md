# 结婚后合报还是分开报：MFJ 与 MFS 的取舍与加州社区财产

这篇讲什么：
- 结婚之后联邦税表只有两个主选项：合报（Married Filing Jointly，简称 MFJ）和分开报（Married Filing Separately，简称 MFS）。默认答案是合报，绝大多数夫妻合报更省税；分开报不是错，而是一个代价很贵、只在少数情形下才划算的例外。
- 先定身份再谈税率：能不能用某个身份报税，看的是 12 月 31 日那一天的婚姻状态，不看结婚了几个月，也不看年底有没有分居。
- 分开报会被关掉一串门：多项抵免和扣除对 MFS 直接不可用，税率档和门槛通常只有合报的一半；再加上一方列举、另一方标准扣除归零这类联动规则，很多人一算才发现分开报贵得多。
- 加州还要多算一层：加州是社区财产州，夫妻分开报时一般要先把社区收入对半分，用 Form 8958 分配，这一步不做，分开报的数字从根上就是错的。

## 一、先定身份：12 月 31 日决定全年婚姻状态

### 中文
报税身份先于一切计算。第 7703(a) 条定的规则很直白：纳税人在课税年度最后一天的婚姻状态，决定他这一整年算已婚还是未婚。也就是说，12 月 31 日那天是已婚，全年按已婚处理；12 月 31 日之前离婚判决已经生效，全年按未婚处理，哪怕这一年大部分时间还是夫妻。结婚日期只差几天，结果可能完全不同：12 月 30 日结婚，当年就能选合报或已婚分开报；1 月 2 日结婚，当年仍按未婚身份报上一年的税。已婚纳税人报联邦税时，身份选项主要是三种：合报 MFJ、已婚分开报 MFS，以及在符合条件时用单亲户主身份（Head of Household），后者有单独的居住和扶养条件，不是夫妻随便就能改用的。先把 12 月 31 日这一天的状态定死，再往下比较税额，这一步错了，后面算得再细也没有用。

### English
Filing status comes before any calculation. Section 7703(a) states the rule plainly: a taxpayer's marital status on the last day of the tax year determines whether the taxpayer is treated as married or unmarried for the entire year. If you are married on December 31, you are treated as married for the whole year; if a divorce decree is already final before December 31, you are treated as unmarried for the whole year, even if you were married for most of it. A difference of a few days in the wedding date can change everything: marry on December 30 and you may choose joint filing or married filing separately for that year; marry on January 2 and you still file the prior year as unmarried. A married taxpayer's main federal options are married filing jointly, married filing separately, and, when separate conditions on living arrangements and support are met, head of household — that third option has its own tests and is not a label a couple can simply elect. Fix the December 31 status first, then compare tax; if this step is wrong, no amount of careful arithmetic below it will save the return.

### Español (初译待校)
El estado civil para declarar va antes que cualquier cálculo. La sección 7703(a) lo dice sin rodeos: el estado civil del contribuyente en el último día del año fiscal determina si se le trata como casado o soltero durante todo el año. Si el 31 de diciembre está casado, se le trata como casado todo el año; si la sentencia de divorcio ya es firme antes del 31 de diciembre, se le trata como soltero todo el año, aunque haya estado casado la mayor parte de ese tiempo. Unos pocos días de diferencia en la fecha de boda lo cambian todo: casarse el 30 de diciembre permite elegir entre declaración conjunta y declaración separada de casados para ese año; casarse el 2 de enero significa que el año anterior aún se declara como soltero. Las opciones federales principales de una persona casada son la declaración conjunta, la declaración separada de casados y, cuando se cumplen condiciones aparte sobre convivencia y manutención, la de cabeza de familia, que tiene sus propios requisitos y no es una etiqueta que la pareja pueda elegir sin más. Primero se fija el estado del 31 de diciembre y después se compara el impuesto; si este paso falla, ningún cálculo posterior, por fino que sea, salva la declaración.

신고 신분은 모든 계산에 앞선다. 제7703(a)조는 규칙을 단도직입적으로 정한다. 과세연도 마지막 날의 혼인 상태가 그 해 전체를 기혼으로 볼지 미혼으로 볼지를 결정한다. 12월 31일에 기혼이면 1년 내내 기혼으로 취급되고, 12월 31일 전에 이혼 판결이 이미 확정됐으면 그 해 대부분을 부부로 지냈더라도 1년 내내 미혼으로 취급된다. 결혼 날짜가 며칠 차이 나는 것만으로 결과가 완전히 달라진다. 12월 30일에 결혼하면 그 해에 부부합산 신고와 기혼자 개별 신고 중에서 고를 수 있지만, 1월 2일에 결혼하면 전년도 세금은 여전히 미혼으로 신고한다. 기혼 납세자의 연방 신고 선택지는 주로 부부합산, 기혼자 개별, 그리고 거주와 부양에 관한 별도 요건을 충족할 때 선택할 수 있는 세대주 신분이다. 세대주는 부부가 임의로 골라 붙일 수 있는 이름표가 아니다. 먼저 12월 31일 상태를 확정하고 그다음 세액을 비교해야 한다. 이 단계가 틀리면 아래 계산을 아무리 정교하게 해도 신고서는 구제되지 않는다.

## 二、合报的好处与连带责任

### 中文
合报是默认选项，原因不只是税率档更宽。第 1 条下，已婚分开报的多数税率档宽度和门槛大致只有合报的一半，合报等于把两人的收入放进一套更宽的档里算，收入差距越大的夫妻，合报的合并效应通常越明显。许多抵免和扣除本来就是按合报设计的，合报时能完整使用，分开报时则被减半、设限或直接取消，下一节会逐项列。合报的代价写在第 6013 条里：联合申报表需要夫妻双方签名，签名意味着双方对整张表承担连带责任，第 6013(d)(3) 条说得很清楚，每个人对全部税款负责，不按谁赚的钱、谁的扣缴来分。配偶少报了收入，另一方即使毫不知情，税务局也可以向任何一方追讨全额。唯一的制度出口是第 6015 条的无辜配偶救济，符合条件时可以申请免除因配偶少报而产生的那部分责任，但那是事后救济，要举证、要等审核，绝不是签名前的一句口头保证能替代的。所以合报的正确打开方式是：省税先算清，连带责任也要在签字前谈清。

### English
Joint filing is the default for more than the wider brackets. Under Section 1, most rate brackets and thresholds for married filing separately are roughly half the joint amounts, so a joint return runs the couple's combined income through one wider set of brackets; the larger the gap between the two incomes, the more this blending usually helps. Many credits and deductions were designed around joint filing and are fully available on a joint return, while separate filing cuts them in half, caps them, or removes them outright, as the next section lists item by item. The price of joint filing is written into Section 6013: a joint return requires both spouses' signatures, and signing creates joint and several liability — Section 6013(d)(3) makes each spouse liable for the entire tax, not for a share apportioned by who earned what or whose withholding it was. If one spouse understated income, the IRS may collect the full amount from either spouse, even from the one who knew nothing about it. The statutory exit is innocent-spouse relief under Section 6015, which can, when its conditions are met, relieve a spouse of liability attributable to the other spouse's understatement — but that is after-the-fact relief requiring proof and review, not a substitute for a conversation before signing. The right way to file jointly is to count the tax saving first and to face the joint liability just as clearly before either signature goes on the return.

### Español (初译待校)
La declaración conjunta es la opción por defecto por algo más que unos tramos más anchos. Según la sección 1, la mayoría de los tramos y umbrales de la declaración separada de casados son aproximadamente la mitad de los de la conjunta, de modo que la declaración conjunta hace pasar el ingreso combinado de la pareja por un conjunto de tramos más amplio; cuanto mayor es la diferencia entre los dos ingresos, más suele ayudar esa combinación. Muchos créditos y deducciones fueron diseñados pensando en la declaración conjunta y están plenamente disponibles en ella, mientras que al declarar por separado se reducen a la mitad, se limitan o desaparecen, como enumera la sección siguiente punto por punto. El precio de la conjunta está escrito en la sección 6013: la declaración conjunta exige la firma de ambos cónyuges, y firmar crea responsabilidad solidaria — la sección 6013(d)(3) hace a cada cónyuge responsable de la totalidad del impuesto, no de una parte repartida según quién ganó qué o de quién era la retención. Si un cónyuge declaró menos ingreso, el IRS puede cobrar el importe completo a cualquiera de los dos, incluso al que no sabía nada. La salida legal es el alivio del cónyuge inocente de la sección 6015, que cuando se cumplen sus condiciones puede liberar a un cónyuge de la responsabilidad atribuible a la subdeclaración del otro, pero es un remedio posterior, con carga de prueba y revisión, no un sustituto de hablarlo antes de firmar. La forma correcta de declarar en conjunto es calcular primero el ahorro y mirar con la misma claridad la responsabilidad solidaria antes de que cualquiera de los dos firme.

### 한국어 (初译待校)
부부합산 신고가 기본값인 이유는 세율 구간이 더 넓어서만이 아니다. 제1조 아래에서 기혼자 개별 신고의 대부분 세율 구간과 기준액은 합산 신고의 대략 절반이다. 따라서 합산 신고는 부부의 합친 소득을 더 넓은 구간 체계 하나로 계산하게 하고, 두 사람의 소득 차이가 클수록 이 혼합 효과가 보통 더 크게 나타난다. 많은 공제와 감면은 애초에 합산 신고를 전제로 설계되어 합산하면 온전히 쓸 수 있지만, 개별 신고에서는 절반으로 줄거나 한도가 걸리거나 아예 사라지는데, 다음 절에서 항목별로 정리한다. 합산 신고의 대가는 제6013조에 적혀 있다. 공동 신고서에는 부부 양쪽의 서명이 필요하고, 서명하면 연대 책임이 생긴다. 제6013(d)(3)조는 각 배우자가 세금 전액에 책임을 진다고 정하며, 누가 얼마를 벌었는지, 누구의 원천징수였는지로 몫을 나누지 않는다. 한 배우자가 소득을 적게 신고하면, 전혀 몰랐던 다른 배우자에게도 국세청이 전액을 징수할 수 있다. 법이 마련한 출구는 제6015조의 무고한 배우자 구제이다. 요건을 충족하면 상대 배우자의 과소신고에서 비롯된 책임을 면할 수 있지만, 이는 입증과 심사를 거쳐야 하는 사후 구제일 뿐 서명 전에 나눈 말 한마디로 대신할 수 없다. 합산 신고의 올바른 방식은 절세액을 먼저 계산하고, 서명하기 전에 연대 책임 역시 똑같이 분명히 마주하는 것이다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Khai chung là lựa chọn mặc định không chỉ vì các khung thuế rộng hơn. Theo Điều 1, phần lớn các khung thuế suất và ngưỡng của khai riêng đối với người đã kết hôn chỉ bằng khoảng một nửa so với khai chung, nên tờ khai chung cho thu nhập gộp của hai vợ chồng đi qua một bộ khung rộng hơn; chênh lệch thu nhập giữa hai người càng lớn, hiệu ứng gộp này thường càng có lợi. Nhiều khoản tín dụng và khấu trừ vốn được thiết kế cho khai chung và dùng được trọn vẹn khi khai chung, trong khi khai riêng thì bị cắt một nửa, bị giới hạn hoặc mất hẳn, như mục tiếp theo sẽ liệt kê từng khoản. Cái giá của khai chung nằm ở Điều 6013: tờ khai chung cần chữ ký của cả hai vợ chồng, và ký tên tạo ra trách nhiệm liên đới — Điều 6013(d)(3) quy định mỗi người chịu trách nhiệm cho toàn bộ số thuế, chứ không chia theo ai kiếm được bao nhiêu hay khoản khấu lưu là của ai. Nếu một người khai thiếu thu nhập, IRS có thể thu toàn bộ số tiền từ bất kỳ người nào trong hai vợ chồng, kể cả người hoàn toàn không biết chuyện. Lối thoát theo luật là cơ chế giảm trách nhiệm cho người phối ngẫu vô tội tại Điều 6015, khi đáp ứng điều kiện có thể miễn phần trách nhiệm do người kia khai thiếu gây ra, nhưng đó là cứu trợ sau sự việc, phải chứng minh và chờ xét duyệt, không thay thế được việc nói rõ trước khi ký. ## 三、分开报被关掉的门：逐项列

### 中文

### English
Separate filing is expensive less because of its rates than because of the long list of doors it closes. First, the earned income credit is generally unavailable: Section 32(d) restricts it for married taxpayers filing separately, with room for exceptions only in specific circumstances such as living apart under the statutory conditions. Second, education credits are generally unavailable: Section 25A(g)(6) expressly excludes married filing separately taxpayers from the American Opportunity and Lifetime Learning credits. Third, the student loan interest deduction cannot be used; the Section 221 deduction is not open to separate filers. Fourth, the premium tax credit is generally unavailable: Section 36B(c)(1)(C) and related rules leave only narrow exceptions, such as for victims of domestic abuse or spouses who have been abandoned, so the accurate statement is generally barred, with limited exceptions — not absolutely barred in every case. Fifth is a linkage trap: under Section 63(c)(6)(A), if one spouse itemizes deductions, the other spouse's standard deduction is zero, so the plan of one itemizing while the other takes the standard deduction is not permitted. Sixth, the annual capital loss allowance is halved: Section 1211(b) allows $3,000 generally but only $1,500 for married filing separately. Seventh, Roth IRA room is essentially squeezed away: for a separate filer who lived with the spouse, the contribution phase-out range is only $0 to $10,000, so even modest income can eliminate direct contributions entirely. On top of that, Section 1 already gives separate filers brackets and many thresholds at half the joint amounts. Added together, item by item, these closed doors are the real price of filing separately.

## 四、什么时候分开反而划算

### 中文
把上一节的门都关上之后，分开报居然还能赢，只剩下几类有明确机制的情形。第一类是按收入计算的学生贷款还款：如果还款额按借款人本人的收入算，而合报会把配偶的收入也算进家庭收入，那么分开报可能把月供压下来；代价是总税额通常更高，所以要把多交的税和少付的月供放在一起算，不能只看一边。第二类是一方有大额医疗费用：第 213 条的医疗费用扣除有调整后总收入 7.5% 的起扣线，费用只有超过本人 AGI 的 7.5% 的部分才能扣；收入较低的那一方单独报税时，自己的 AGI 更低，同一笔医疗费更容易越过起扣线，扣到的部分可能多到足以抵消分开报的其他损失。第三类是责任隔离：一方有欠税、少报风险或经营风险，另一方不想签连带责任，分开报可以把各自的税表和责任切开，这买的是安全，不是省税。第四类是州层面的原因：有些州的税制、抵免或社区财产规则会让联邦分开、州再按各自规则处理的组合更合适，必须联邦和州一起建模。除此之外，凭感觉分开报、为了吵架时赌气分开报，算完账几乎总是更贵。结论很朴素：分开报是一个要先算出具体受益机制、再决定用的工具，不是默认选项。

### English
After all the doors in the previous section close, separate filing can still win, but only in a few situations with an identifiable mechanism. The first is income-driven student loan repayment: if the payment is calculated on the borrower's own income while a joint return would pull the spouse's income into the household figure, filing separately may lower the monthly payment; the cost is usually a higher total tax bill, so the extra tax and the reduced payment must be weighed together, never one side alone. The second is large medical expenses for one spouse: the Section 213 medical expense deduction has a floor of 7.5% of adjusted gross income, and only expenses above 7.5% of the taxpayer's own AGI are deductible; when the lower-income spouse files separately, that spouse's own AGI is lower, so the same medical bills clear the floor more easily, and the additional deduction can occasionally outweigh the other losses from filing separately. The third is liability separation: where one spouse has back taxes, underreporting risk, or business exposure, the other spouse may refuse to sign for joint and several liability, and separate returns cut each person's return and liability apart — this buys safety, not tax savings. The fourth is state-level reasons: some states' tax systems, credits, or community property rules make a federal-separate combination work better once the state side is modeled as well, so federal and state must be computed together. Beyond these, filing separately on a hunch, or to make a point during an argument, almost always costs more once the arithmetic is done. The conclusion is plain: separate filing is a tool to use only after a specific benefit mechanism has been identified and measured, not a default.

Después de que se cierran todas las puertas de la sección anterior, declarar por separado aún puede ganar, pero solo en unas pocas situaciones con un mecanismo identificable. La primera es el pago de préstamos estudiantiles basado en ingresos: si la cuota se calcula sobre el ingreso del propio prestatario, mientras que una declaración conjunta incorporaría el ingreso del cónyuge a la cifra familiar, declarar por separado puede bajar la cuota mensual; el costo suele ser un impuesto total mayor, así que el impuesto extra y la cuota reducida deben pesarse juntos, nunca un solo lado. La segunda son los gastos médicos grandes de un cónyuge: la deducción por gastos médicos de la sección 213 tiene un piso del 7,5% del ingreso bruto ajustado, y solo es deducible lo que supere el 7,5% del AGI propio del contribuyente; cuando el cónyuge de menor ingreso declara por separado, su AGI es más bajo, las mismas facturas médicas superan el piso con más facilidad, y la deducción adicional a veces puede compensar las demás pérdidas de declarar por separado. La tercera es la separación de responsabilidad: cuando un cónyuge tiene impuestos atrasados, riesgo de subdeclaración o exposición empresarial, el otro puede negarse a firmar una responsabilidad solidaria, y las declaraciones separadas separan la declaración y la responsabilidad de cada uno; esto compra seguridad, no ahorro fiscal. La cuarta son razones estatales: el sistema tributario, los créditos o las reglas de bienes gananciales de algunos estados pueden hacer que la combinación con declaración federal separada funcione mejor cuando el lado estatal también se modela, de modo que lo federal y lo estatal deben calcularse juntos. Fuera de estos casos, declarar por separado por corazonada, o para ganar una discusión, casi siempre sale más caro cuando se hacen las cuentas. La conclusión es sencilla: declarar por separado es una herramienta que se usa solo después de identificar y medir un mecanismo concreto de beneficio, no una opción por defecto.

## 五、加州社区财产：分开报为什么先对半分

### 中文
住在加州的夫妻分开报，还要先过社区财产这一关。加州是美国九个社区财产州之一。社区财产的基本想法是：婚姻期间夫妻各自赚的工资等社区收入，法律上视为两人各占一半，不按钱实际进了谁的账户算。最高法院在 Poe v. Seaborn, 282 U.S. 101 (1930) 中确认了这一处理在联邦税上的效力，第 879 条等社区财产规则也延续同一原则：夫妻分开申报时，每人一般报告社区收入的一半，加上各自的个人财产收入。操作工具是 Form 8958（Allocation of Tax Amounts Between Certain Individuals），用它把工资、利息等社区项目在两张分开的税表之间分配，并让两张表对得上。跳过这一步、谁赚的谁全报，是加州分开报最常见的错误。第 66 条留了例外和救济：比如夫妻全年分居、未共同申报等符合条件时，可以不按社区财产对半分处理某些收入；不符合严格例外时，还有衡平救济的空间，由税务局按公平原则决定是否免除按社区规则多算出的责任。所以在加州谈分开报，顺序永远是先分社区财产、填 Form 8958、再查第 66 条有没有例外可用，最后才轮到比较税额。

### English
A couple in California who files separately must first clear the community property hurdle. California is one of nine community property states in the United States. The basic idea is that community income earned during the marriage, such as each spouse's wages, is treated in law as owned one-half by each spouse, regardless of whose account the money actually landed in. The Supreme Court confirmed the federal tax effect of this treatment in Poe v. Seaborn, 282 U.S. 101 (1930), and the community property rules reflected in Section 879 carry the same principle forward: when spouses file separately, each generally reports one-half of the community income, plus that spouse's own separate property income. The working tool is Form 8958, Allocation of Tax Amounts Between Certain Individuals, which allocates community items such as wages and interest between the two separate returns so the two returns tie to each other. Skipping this step and reporting all of one's own earnings on one's own return is the most common California separate-filing mistake. Section 66 leaves exceptions and relief: where conditions such as spouses living apart for the entire year without filing jointly are met, certain income may be treated as the earning spouse's alone rather than split; and where the strict exceptions are not met, equitable relief may still be available, with the IRS deciding on fairness grounds whether to relieve the extra liability the community rules would otherwise create. So in California the order is always the same: split the community property first, complete Form 8958, check whether a Section 66 exception or relief applies, and only then compare the tax.

Vì vậy, ở California thứ tự luôn là: chia tài sản chung trước, điền Mẫu 8958, kiểm tra xem có ngoại lệ hay cứu trợ theo Điều 66 không, và cuối cùng mới so sánh số thuế.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)
California में अलग दाखिल करने वाले दंपति को पहले सामुदायिक संपत्ति की बाधा पार करनी होती है। California संयुक्त राज्य के नौ सामुदायिक संपत्ति राज्यों में से एक है। मूल विचार यह है कि विवाह के दौरान अर्जित सामुदायिक आय, जैसे प्रत्येक जीवनसाथी का वेतन, कानून में दोनों की आधी-आधी मानी जाती है, चाहे पैसा वास्तव में किसी के भी खाते में गया हो। सर्वोच्च न्यायालय ने Poe v. Seaborn, 282 U.S. 101 (1930) में इस व्यवहार की संघीय कर-प्रभावशीलता की पुष्टि की, और धारा 879 में प्रतिबिंबित सामुदायिक संपत्ति नियम उसी सिद्धांत को आगे बढ़ाते हैं: जब पति-पत्नी अलग दाखिल करते हैं, तो प्रत्येक सामान्यतः सामुदायिक आय का आधा हिस्सा, अपनी अलग संपत्ति की आय के साथ, 신고 करता है। व्यावहारिक साधन Form 8958, Allocation of Tax Amounts Between Certain Individuals है, जो वेतन और ब्याज जैसी सामुदायिक मदों को दो अलग रिटर्न के बीच इस तरह बाँटता है कि दोनों रिटर्न आपस में मेल खाएँ। इस कदम को छोड़कर अपनी कमाई पूरी अपनी रिटर्न में डालना California में अलग दाखिल की सबसे आम ग़लती है। धारा 66 अपवाद और राहत छोड़ती है: जहाँ शर्तें पूरी हों, जैसे जीवनसाथी पूरे वर्ष अलग रहे हों और संयुक्त दाखिल न किया हो, वहाँ कुछ आय को बाँटने के बजाय कमाने वाले जीवनसाथी की अकेले की आय माना जा सकता है; और जहाँ कड़े अपवाद लागू न हों, वहाँ भी समानता-आधारित राहत संभव है, जिसमें IRS निष्पक्षता के आधार पर तय करता है कि सामुदायिक नियमों से बनने वाला अतिरिक्त दायित्व माफ़ किया जाए या नहीं। इसलिए California में क्रम हमेशा यही है: पहले सामुदायिक संपत्ति बाँटिए, Form 8958 भरिए, देखिए कि धारा 66 का कोई अपवाद या राहत लागू होता है या नहीं, और उसके बाद ही कर की तुलना कीजिए।
