# 学生贷款被免除要交税吗：第 108 条 f 款排除、PSLF 与 2021 至 2025 免税窗口及加州口径

这篇讲什么：
- 学生贷款被免除，默认先按第 61 条 a 款第 11 项的债务免除收入看，再逐条找第 108 条的排除；学贷专属的排除在第 108 条 f 款。
- 第 108 条 f 款第 1 项的老通道至今有效：因死亡或完全永久伤残免除、以及依特定联邦计划（公共服务贷款免除 PSLF 是最常见的一条）免除的学贷，不计入总收入。
- 《美国救援计划法》（ARPA）新增的第 108 条 f 款第 5 项曾开过一扇大窗：2020 年 12 月 31 日之后、2026 年 1 月 1 日之前发生的学贷免除，联邦层面一律排除——这扇窗已经关上，2026 年起一般性的收入驱动还款（IDR）免除恢复应税。
- 加州不跟随 ARPA 那扇窗：加州援用第 108 条的跟随日期早于 ARPA，联邦免税的那几年，加州一侧可能照样要报。

## 一、先定性：免除先是收入，排除要另找条文

### 中文
债务被免除，省下的那笔钱在税法上先被当作收入：第 61 条 a 款第 11 项把债务免除收入（income from discharge of indebtedness）明文列进总收入，本仓《债务免除怎么计税》篇管的是这条总线。第 108 条是排除的总仓库，其中 a 款列破产、资不抵债等一般排除，f 款专管学生贷款。看学贷免除的顺序是固定的：先确认这笔免除落进第 61 条，再到第 108 条 f 款找专属排除，f 款找不到再看 a 款的资不抵债排除等一般通道，顺序不能反过来先假定免税。还要分清两个常被混在一起的数：免除的是本金加应计利息的总额，申报与排除都按免除总额算，不只算本金。

### English
A discharged debt is income first: section 61(a)(11) expressly lists income from discharge of indebtedness in gross income, and this repository's general article on discharge of indebtedness covers that main line. Section 108 is the warehouse of exclusions — subsection (a) holds the general ones such as bankruptcy and insolvency, and subsection (f) is the student-loan-specific one. The order of analysis is fixed: confirm the discharge falls under section 61, then look for the specific exclusion in section 108(f); only if (f) does not reach it do the general routes in subsection (a), such as insolvency, come into play. Do not start by assuming tax-free treatment. Also keep the amount straight: the figure is the total discharged — principal plus accrued interest — and both reporting and any exclusion are measured on that total, not on principal alone.

La sección 108 es el almacén de exclusiones: el apartado (a) guarda las generales, como quiebra e insolvencia, y el apartado (f) es el específico para préstamos estudiantiles. El orden de análisis es fijo: primero se confirma que la condonación cae bajo la sección 61, luego se busca la exclusión específica en la sección 108(f); solo si (f) no la alcanza entran las vías generales del apartado (a), como la insolvencia. No se debe partir de suponer que es libre de impuestos. Y la cifra es el total condonado — principal más intereses devengados —: tanto la declaración como cualquier exclusión se miden sobre ese total, no solo sobre el principal.

## 二、老通道：第 108 条 f 款第 1 项与 PSLF

### 中文
第 108 条 f 款第 1 项是长期有效的学贷排除，覆盖三类情形：一是贷款依联邦法律的相关计划条款被免除，其中最有名的就是《高等教育法》下的公共服务贷款免除（Public Service Loan Forgiveness, PSLF，《美国法典》第 20 编第 1087e 条 m 款）——在合格公共服务雇主处全职工作、按收入驱动或标准计划还满 120 次合格月供后，剩余余额被免除，这笔免除不计入联邦总收入；二是借款人死亡；三是借款人完全且永久伤残（total and permanent disability）。这三类的共同点是：免除的依据本身写在联邦计划或身份事实里，不依赖年份窗口，今天仍然有效。实务提醒一句：PSLF 的免除走教育部与贷款服务机构的核定，不经第 108 条 f 款第 5 项那扇已经关闭的窗口，所以 2026 年之后拿到 PSLF 免除的人，联邦一侧依旧排除，不要被「窗口关了」吓住。

### English
Section 108(f)(1) is the long-standing student-loan exclusion, covering three situations. First, loans discharged under the relevant provisions of federal law — most famously Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) under the Higher Education Act, 20 U.S.C. section 1087e(m): after full-time work for a qualifying public-service employer and 120 qualifying monthly payments under an income-driven or standard plan, the remaining balance is discharged and stays out of federal gross income. Second, discharge on the borrower's death. Third, discharge on total and permanent disability. What these share is that the basis for discharge is written into a federal program or a status fact, not into a calendar window — they remain in force today. A practical note: a PSLF discharge runs through the Department of Education and the loan servicer, not through the now-closed window of section 108(f)(5), so a borrower who receives PSLF forgiveness after 2025 still excludes it federally. Do not let the closed window scare the wrong people.

### Español (初译待校)

Primero, préstamos condonados bajo las disposiciones pertinentes de la ley federal — el más conocido es la condonación por servicio público (Public Service Loan Forgiveness, PSLF) de la Ley de Educación Superior, 20 U.S.C. § 1087e(m): tras trabajar a tiempo completo para un empleador de servicio público calificado y hacer 120 pagos mensuales calificados bajo un plan según ingresos o el plan estándar, el saldo restante se condona y queda fuera del ingreso bruto federal. Segundo, la condonación por fallecimiento del prestatario. Tercero, la condonación por discapacidad total y permanente (total and permanent disability). Lo común es que la base de la condonación está escrita en un programa federal o en un hecho de estatus, no en una ventana de calendario: siguen vigentes hoy. Nota práctica: la condonación PSLF pasa por el Departamento de Educación y el administrador del préstamo, no por la ventana ya cerrada de la sección 108(f)(5), de modo que quien reciba PSLF después de 2025 sigue excluyéndolo a nivel federal.

## 三、大窗与关窗：第 108 条 f 款第 5 项的 2021 至 2025 免税窗口

### 中文
2021 年《美国救援计划法》（American Rescue Plan Act, P.L. 117-2）第 9675 节增订第 108 条 f 款第 5 项，把排除一下子放宽：凡在 2020 年 12 月 31 日之后、2026 年 1 月 1 日之前发生的学生贷款免除——不问是 IDR 还满年限的免除、私立学贷的和解免除，还是学校关门等情形——联邦层面一律不计入总收入。这就是常说的「2021 至 2025 免税窗口」。窗口的边界是免除发生的日期，不是申请日期，也不是还款开始的年份。窗口关上之后，自 2026 年 1 月 1 日起发生的免除回到老框架：先过 f 款第 1 项（PSLF、死亡、伤残仍排除），过不了的再看第 108 条 a 款的资不抵债排除等一般通道，都过不了就按第 61 条计入收入。受影响最大的是收入驱动还款计划（IDR，包括 SAVE、PAYE、IBR 等）还满 20 或 25 年后的余额免除——它从来不在 f 款第 1 项里，2021 至 2025 年靠窗口免税，2026 年起在联邦一侧恢复应税。具体年份的执行细则以 IRS 当期公告为准。

### English
Section 9675 of the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (P.L. 117-2) added section 108(f)(5) and widened the exclusion dramatically: any student loan discharge after December 31, 2020 and before January 1, 2026 — whether an income-driven repayment (IDR) term-end discharge, a private-loan settlement, or a school-closure discharge — was excluded from federal gross income. That is the so-called 2021–2025 tax-free window. The boundary is the date of discharge, not the date of application or the year repayment began. Once the window closed, discharges on or after January 1, 2026 returned to the old framework: test section 108(f)(1) first (PSLF, death, and disability still excluded); if that fails, test the general routes in section 108(a), such as insolvency; if nothing applies, the amount is income under section 61. The group hit hardest is borrowers whose IDR balances (under SAVE, PAYE, IBR, and similar plans) are discharged after 20 or 25 years of payments — that discharge was never inside (f)(1), was sheltered by the window in 2021–2025, and is federally taxable again from 2026. For year-specific mechanics, follow current IRS guidance.

### Español (初译待校)
La sección 9675 de la Ley del Plan de Rescate Estadounidense de 2021 (American Rescue Plan Act, P.L. 117-2) añadió la sección 108(f)(5) y amplió la exclusión de golpe: toda condonación de préstamo estudiantil posterior al 31 de diciembre de 2020 y anterior al 1 de enero de 2026 — fuera condonación al final de un plan según ingresos (IDR), un acuerdo sobre un préstamo privado o una condonación por cierre de la escuela — quedó excluida del ingreso bruto federal. Es la llamada «ventana libre de impuestos 2021–2025». La frontera es la fecha de la condonación, no la de la solicitud ni el año en que empezó el reembolso. Cerrada la ventana, las condonaciones desde el 1 de enero de 2026 vuelven al marco antiguo: primero la sección 108(f)(1) (PSLF, fallecimiento y discapacidad siguen excluidos); si no alcanza, las vías generales de la sección 108(a), como la insolvencia; si nada aplica, el importe es ingreso bajo la sección 61. El grupo más afectado es el de los saldos IDR (SAVE, PAYE, IBR y similares) condonados tras 20 o 25 años de pagos: esa condonación nunca estuvo en (f)(1), la ventana la protegió en 2021–2025 y desde 2026 vuelve a tributar a nivel federal. ## 五、算例与加州口径

### 中文

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Ví dụ một (PSLF, xóa 30.000 đô la): một y tá làm toàn thời gian tại bệnh viện công, hoàn thành 120 khoản trả đủ điều kiện và nhận xóa PSLF 30.000 đô la vào năm 2026. Ở cấp liên bang, khoản này rơi vào Điều 108(f)(1) và số chịu thuế bằng không; nếu bị xử lý nhầm là chịu thuế, 30.000 nhân thuế suất biên minh họa 22% sẽ là 6.600 đô la — đúng quy mô mà loại trừ tiết kiệm được, và là khoản tiền vốn không hề phải nộp. Ví dụ hai (IDR, xóa 25.000 đô la): cùng một khoản xóa IDR 25.000 đô la khi hết hạn, nếu xảy ra năm 2025 thì rơi vào cửa sổ (f)(5) và được loại trừ liên bang; nếu xảy ra năm 2026, cửa sổ đã đóng và (f)(1) không áp dụng, nên về nguyên tắc tính toàn bộ vào thu nhập — ở mức minh họa 22% là 25.000 nhân 22% bằng 5.500 đô la về quy mô (thuế thực tế tùy khung thuế của năm và khả năng loại trừ mất khả năng thanh toán). Kiểm tra lại: 30.000 × 22% = 6.600 và 25.000 × 22% = 5.500, đã tính hai lần khớp nhau. California xét riêng: California tiếp nhận Điều 108 qua Điều 17131 của Bộ luật Doanh thu và Thuế và các quy định liên quan, nhưng ngày tuân thủ của bang cố định trước ARPA, nên California không hề tiếp nhận cửa sổ (f)(5) 2021–2025 — các khoản xóa kiểu IDR được miễn thuế liên bang trong những năm cửa sổ về nguyên tắc vẫn là thu nhập tại California, điều chỉnh trên Schedule CA. Các loại trừ cũ của (f)(1), gồm PSLF, có trước ngày tuân thủ và nhìn chung được theo. Trường hợp cụ thể lấy hướng dẫn hiện hành của FTB làm chuẩn.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)
उदाहरण एक (PSLF, $30,000 माफ़): एक नर्स सार्वजनिक अस्पताल में पूर्णकालिक काम करती है, 120 योग्य भुगतान पूरे करती है और 2026 में $30,000 की PSLF मुक्ति पाती है। संघीय स्तर पर यह धारा 108(f)(1) में पड़ती है और करयोग्य राशि शून्य है; यदि गलती से इसे करयोग्य मानें तो $30,000 पर उदाहरणार्थ 22% सीमांत दर से $6,600 बनते हैं — यही वह पैमाना है जो छूट बचाती है, और वह धन कभी देय था ही नहीं। उदाहरण दो (IDR, $25,000 माफ़): वही $25,000 की IDR अवधि-समाप्ति मुक्ति यदि 2025 में हो तो (f)(5) खिड़की में पड़कर संघीय स्तर पर बाहर रहती है; 2026 में हो तो खिड़की बंद है और (f)(1) लागू नहीं, इसलिए सिद्धांततः पूरी राशि आय में जाती है — उदाहरणार्थ 22% पर 25,000 गुणा 22% अर्थात $5,500 पैमाने की (वास्तविक कर उस वर्ष के स्लैब और शोधन-अक्षमता छूट पर निर्भर है)। पुनः जाँच: 30,000 × 22% = 6,600 और 25,000 × 22% = 5,500, दो बार मिलान किया गया। California अलग से: California Revenue and Taxation Code धारा 17131 व संबंधित प्रावधानों से धारा 108 को अपनाता है, किंतु उसकी अनुरूपता तिथि ARPA से पहले ठहरी है, इसलिए California ने (f)(5) की 2021–2025 खिड़की कभी नहीं अपनाई — खिड़की के वर्षों में संघीय स्तर पर कर-मुक्त रही IDR-प्रकार की मुक्तियाँ सिद्धांततः California में अब भी आय हैं, Schedule CA पर समायोजन के साथ। PSLF सहित (f)(1) की पुरानी छूटें अनुरूपता तिथि से पहले की हैं और मोटे तौर पर अपनाई जाती हैं। विशिष्ट मामले में FTB के वर्तमान मार्गदर्शन को देखें।
