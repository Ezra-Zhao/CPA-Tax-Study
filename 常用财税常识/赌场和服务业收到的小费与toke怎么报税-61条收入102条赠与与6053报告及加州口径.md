# 赌场和服务业收到的小费与 toke 怎么报税：第 61 条收入、第 102 条赠与与第 6053 条报告及加州口径

## 1. 先定性：小费与 toke 是第 61 条收入，不是第 102 条赠与

### 中文主文

餐厅服务员的小费、赌场发牌员的 toke、理发师和代客泊车收到的现金，定性只有一条主线：《国内收入法典》第 61 条 (a) 款的总收入包括服务报酬，顾客自愿给的小费正是服务报酬的一种。赌场 toke 的底案是 Olk v. United States, 536 F.2d 876 (9th Cir. 1976)：第九巡回按 Commissioner v. Duberstein, 363 U.S. 278 (1960) 的判准——赠与须出于「超然无私的慷慨」——判 toke 是收入、不是第 102 条 (a) 款赠与；赌客出于冲动慷慨或迷信给钱也不行，因为那是对命运之神「深度投入、利害攸关」的献礼。落到日常：钱是冲着服务给的，就先按收入记账，不要因为「客人自愿」就当礼物放过。

第 102 条不是完全用不上，只是门在另一边：与服务无关的亲友馈赠才走赠与排除；同一笔钱不能既是服务对价又是赠与。雇主以奖金、红包名义发的与工作挂钩的钱，同样是收入。先把定性钉死，后面的报告与代扣才有地方落。

来源：《国内收入法典》第 61 条、第 102 条 (a) 款、第 6053 条 (a) 款；Olk v. United States, 536 F.2d 876 (9th Cir. 1976)；Commissioner v. Duberstein, 363 U.S. 278 (1960)；IRS 小费收入专题页（雇员小费报告口径）。

声明：个人学习笔记，不构成法律、税务或医疗建议。

### English

Tips for servers, tokes for casino dealers, and cash handed to barbers and valets follow one main line: gross income under I.R.C. § 61(a) includes compensation for services, and a customer's voluntary tip is a form of service compensation. The foundational case for casino tokes is Olk v. United States, 536 F.2d 876 (9th Cir. 1976): applying Commissioner v. Duberstein, 363 U.S. 278 (1960) — a gift must proceed from "detached and disinterested generosity" — the Ninth Circuit held tokes are income, not § 102(a) gifts; impulsive generosity or superstition does not help, because a tribute to the gods of fortune is a deeply involved act. In daily practice: money given for service is booked as income first; "the customer gave it voluntarily" does not make it a gift.

Section 102 is not useless — its door is just elsewhere: only transfers unrelated to services, such as gifts among family and friends, take the gift exclusion. The same money cannot be both service pay and a gift, and employer payments tied to work, whatever they are called, are likewise income. Settle the characterization first; reporting and withholding then have somewhere to land.

### Español（初译待校）

Las propinas de camareros, los tokes de crupieres y el efectivo para peluqueros y aparcacoches siguen una línea principal: el ingreso bruto del § 61(a) incluye la retribución por servicios, y la propina voluntaria del cliente es una forma de esa retribución. El caso base de los tokes es Olk v. United States, 536 F.2d 876 (9th Cir. 1976): con el criterio de Commissioner v. Duberstein, 363 U.S. 278 (1960) — la donación exige «generosidad desprendida y desinteresada» — el Noveno Circuito declaró los tokes ingreso y no donación del § 102(a); la generosidad impulsiva o la superstición no sirven, porque el tributo a los dioses de la fortuna es un acto profundamente interesado. En la práctica: el dinero dado por un servicio se registra primero como ingreso.

El § 102 no desaparece: su puerta está en otro lado. Solo las entregas sin relación con servicios, como las de familiares y amigos, usan la exclusión por donación. El mismo dinero no puede ser a la vez pago por servicios y regalo, y lo que paga el empleador ligado al trabajo también es ingreso, se llame como se llame. Primero fije la calificación; luego el reporte y la retención tienen dónde apoyarse.

## 2. 报告与代扣：第 6053 条每月报告、W-2 三栏与池分小费

### 中文主文

动作在第 6053 条 (a) 款：直接收小费的雇员，一个月内收到的小费达到 20 美元，就要在次月 10 日前向雇主书面报告，实务上一线工具是 Form 4070（Employee's Report of Tips to Employer）；现金小费、刷卡小费、池分分到的份额都算。报告不是走形式——雇主据此对小费代扣所得税与社保医保税，并把全年小费汇进 W-2：第 1 栏工资小费总报酬、第 5 栏社保医保工资、第 7 栏社保小费三栏都含小费；雇主另在第 8 栏填的 allocated tips 是分配小费，与雇员自报数分开看。

池分（tip pool）按实际所得报，不按桌边总数报。算例与案例篇对读：四名发牌员一班合并 toke 800 美元、平分，每人实际所得 200 美元（800÷4＝200），每人就按 200 美元计入当月报告与全年收入，而不是按 800 美元。自己留一本小费日记（日期、金额、池分规则）最稳：数字与雇主记录对不上时，日记就是解释的底稿。漏报的代价不只是补税——小费是社保工资的一部分，报少了将来的社保福利基数也跟着少。

声明：个人学习笔记，不构成法律、税务或医疗建议。

### English

The mechanics live in § 6053(a): an employee who receives tips directly must report them to the employer in writing by the 10th of the following month once tips in a month reach $20; the front-line tool is Form 4070 (Employee's Report of Tips to Employer). Cash tips, charged tips, and pooled shares all count. The report is not a formality — the employer withholds income tax and Social Security and Medicare taxes on reported tips and folds the year's tips into Form W-2: Box 1 (wages, tips, other compensation), Box 5 (Medicare wages and tips), and Box 7 (Social Security tips) all include tips; Box 8 allocated tips are the employer's allocation, read separately from what the employee reported.

Pooled tips are reported at what you actually receive, not the table total. Worked example, matching the case piece: four dealers pool $800 in tokes in a shift and split evenly — each actually receives $200 (800 ÷ 4 = 200), and each reports $200 for that month and year, not $800. Keep a simple tip diary — date, amount, pooling rule — because when your numbers and the employer's records diverge, the diary is your working paper. Underreporting costs more than back tax: tips are Social Security wages, so reporting less also shrinks the base for future benefits.

## 3. 加州口径与动手清单：州税同样计入，记录留满

### 中文主文

加州口径一句话：小费与 toke 同样计入加州所得税的应税收入，跟着联邦定性走——联邦按第 61 条算收入的，加州申报时不要另设一个「赠与」口袋把它藏起来。州表上的工资口径以 W-2 与联邦处理为起点，加州调整另有清单，小费本身不在豁免之列。具体申报位置与当年表格以加州税务局（FTB）当年的说明为准，本篇只钉定性、不钉栏位号。

动手清单四件。第一，每班记账：现金、刷卡、池分三笔分开记，池分写清总池与人数。第二，每月对表：当月小费到 20 美元就交 Form 4070 或雇主的电子报告通道，别攒到年底。第三，年终核 W-2：第 1、5、7 栏与自己日记对一遍，对不上先找雇主更正，再考虑在申报表上按实际数申报并留说明。第四，留档：日记、报告副本、W-2 至少留到申报时效过去。收口句还是 Olk 那条线——冲着服务给的钱就是收入，把报告做在每月，定性之争根本不会轮到您。

### English

State wage reporting starts from the W-2 and the federal treatment; California has its own adjustment list, and tips are not on any exemption list. Exact lines and forms follow the FTB instructions for the year; this piece fixes the characterization, not the box numbers.

Four-item checklist. First, log each shift — cash, charged, and pooled tips separately, noting the pool total and headcount. Second, report monthly: once a month's tips reach $20, file Form 4070 or use the employer's electronic channel instead of saving it up for year-end. Third, reconcile the W-2: check Boxes 1, 5, and 7 against your diary, ask the employer to correct first, and only then consider filing on actual numbers with an explanation attached. Fourth, keep records — diary, report copies, W-2 — at least until the filing limitations period runs. The closing line is still Olk's: money given for service is income, and monthly reporting keeps the characterization fight from ever reaching you.
