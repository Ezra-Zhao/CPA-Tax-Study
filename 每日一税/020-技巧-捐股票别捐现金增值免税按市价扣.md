# 每日一税 第 020 条 · 节税技巧

- 标题：捐涨了的股票别先卖：增值不用交税，按市价扣、上限是 AGI 的 30%
- 类别：节税技巧
- 拟发日期：2026-11-10
- 证据等级：A（核心数字出自 IRC 第 170 条原文、IRS Topic No. 506、IRS Publication 526 与 561、Form 8283 说明书（2025 年 12 月修订版））

## X 版

年底捐赠别先卖股票：持有超 1 年涨了的股票，捐给合格慈善机构，增值不交资本利得税，还按市价扣、上限 AGI 的 30%、超额结转 5 年；先卖再捐，增值先被咬一口税。2026 年起逐项捐赠先过 AGI 0.5% 门槛才扣。本条只作信息分享，不构成税务建议；具体事务请咨询持牌 CPA 或税务律师。

## 仓内完整版

### 省在哪：同一笔捐赠，顺序换一下差一笔税

想捐钱给慈善机构，手里正好有一只涨了的股票，先卖掉再捐现金是最常见的顺序，也是最亏的顺序。卖出那一步先确认资本利得、交一道税，剩下的才轮到捐赠扣除。反过来，把股票原样转给机构：捐赠不是出售，增值部分不确认收入、不交资本利得税；扣除还按捐赠当天的市价算，不按你当年买入的成本算。一笔捐赠，两头都占。

教学演算（数字是假设，不是真实个案）：股票买入 $10,000、现在市值 $50,000、持有超过 1 年，捐赠人 AGI $200,000。直接捐：按市价扣 $50,000，30% 上限是 $200,000 × 30% = $60,000，装得下；增值 $40,000 一分税不用交。先卖再捐：按假设的 15% 长期资本利得税率算，$40,000 × 15% = $6,000 先交给税局（还没算可能适用的 3.8% 净投资收入税和州税），手里只剩 $44,000 能捐。差距就是那 $6,000。

2026 年多一道门槛：逐项慈善扣除先减掉 AGI 的 0.5% 才起扣（OBBBA，2026 税年起）。上面这个例子里，$200,000 × 0.5% = $1,000，若这是全年的全部捐赠，实际能扣的是 $50,000 − $1,000 = $49,000。

### 四个条件，缺一个味道就变了

一是持有超过 1 年。满 1 年的叫资本利得财产，按市价扣；持有 1 年或更短的，按第 170(e)(1) 条只能把增值减掉、基本按成本扣，优势大半没了。

二是给对人。受赠方得是合格组织（教会、学校、医院这类公共慈善机构），捐之前用 IRS 的 Tax Exempt Organization Search 查一眼资格。直接把钱给个人，不算可扣捐赠。捐给私人基金会的限额比 30% 还低，别套这个数。

三是你得走逐项扣除，这笔才有意义。2026 年起不逐项的人新添了一笔线上扣除，但只认现金（单身 $1,000、夫妻合并 $2,000），捐股票不在里面。

四是市价有算法。上市股票的市价是捐赠当日最高成交价与最低成交价的平均数（Publication 561），不是收盘价，也不是你下单那一刻的价格。

### 上限与结转：30% 装不下，分五年慢慢扣

捐资本利得财产给公共慈善机构，当年扣除上限是 AGI 的 30%（第 170(b)(1)(C) 条），比现金捐赠的 60% 低。超出的部分往后结转最多 5 年（第 170(d)(1) 条），结转年份里仍按 30% 上限用。还有一个少用的选举：自愿只按成本扣，可以换到 50% 的上限类别——增值不大的股票、急着当年扣完的人才用得上，一般人别选。

### 纸面功夫：两处最容易翻车

单笔 $250 或以上，报税前要拿到受赠机构的同期书面确认（contemporaneous written acknowledgment，第 170(f)(8) 条），写明机构名、日期和捐赠财产描述；没有这张纸，整笔不能扣，金额再对也没用。

非现金捐赠合计超过 $500，要随税表附 Form 8283。这里有个和网上多数说法不一样的点：上市股票不管金额多大都只填 Section A，不需要找合格评估师做评估——Form 8283 说明书（2025 年 12 月修订版）写得很清楚，超过 $5,000 就要评估那套是给非上市财产准备的。

### 时间：年底前要完成过户，不是年底前下单

捐赠成立看的是股票不可撤销地转进机构的券商账户那一天。想算 2026 税年的，12 月 31 日前必须完成过户；券商之间划转要走几个工作日，拖到 12 月最后一周才动手，很可能划进 2027 年。提前两周发起，留出确认回执的时间。

加州口径一句：加州同样不把捐赠当出售，增值不用交州税；扣除走加州自己的逐项口径，和联邦的门槛、上限不是一套数，报州税时分开算。

本条只作信息分享，不构成税务建议；具体事务请咨询持牌 CPA 或税务律师。

## English

**Daily Tax No. 020 — Donate the appreciated stock, do not sell it first: the gain escapes tax and you deduct full market value, capped at 30% of AGI.** If you plan to give to charity and hold a stock that has gone up, selling first and donating cash is the common order — and the expensive one. The sale recognizes the capital gain and takes a tax bite before the gift. Transfer the shares themselves instead: a donation is not a sale, so the appreciation is never taxed, and the deduction is measured at fair market value on the gift date, not at your cost. Teaching example (hypothetical numbers): stock bought for $10,000, now worth $50,000, held over one year, donor AGI $200,000. Donate directly: deduct $50,000; the 30% cap is $200,000 × 30% = $60,000, so it fits; the $40,000 gain is never taxed. Sell first at an assumed 15% long-term capital-gains rate: $40,000 × 15% = $6,000 goes to the tax authority first (before any 3.8% net investment income tax or state tax), leaving only $44,000 to give. The gap is exactly that $6,000. From 2026 there is one more gate: itemized charitable deductions are reduced by 0.5% of AGI before they start to count (OBBBA, from tax year 2026) — in this example $200,000 × 0.5% = $1,000, so a $50,000 gift that is the year's only giving deducts $50,000 − $1,000 = $49,000. Four conditions carry the benefit. Hold more than one year: capital-gain property deducts at market value, while property held a year or less is cut back under section 170(e)(1) to roughly cost. Give to a qualified organization — a public charity such as a church, school, or hospital; check status in the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search; gifts to individuals never count, and private-foundation limits are lower than 30%. Itemize: from 2026 non-itemizers get a new above-the-line deduction, but it is cash only ($1,000 single, $2,000 joint) — stock gifts are not in it. And market value has a formula: for listed stock it is the average of the day's high and low prices (Publication 561), not the closing price. The cap for capital-gain property given to a public charity is 30% of AGI (section 170(b)(1)(C)), below the 60% cash cap; the excess carries forward up to five years (section 170(d)(1)), still under the 30% cap; an election to deduct only basis trades into the 50% category and suits few people. Paperwork traps: any single gift of $250 or more needs a contemporaneous written acknowledgment from the charity before you file (section 170(f)(8)) — no paper, no deduction. Noncash gifts over $500 in total go on Form 8283, and listed securities of any amount use only Section A with no qualified appraisal — the December 2025 revision of the Form 8283 instructions says so, whatever most online summaries claim. Timing: the gift is complete when the shares irrevocably land in the charity's brokerage account, so for a 2026 gift the transfer must finish by December 31; start two weeks early. California likewise does not treat the donation as a sale, so the gain escapes state tax too, but the state itemized computation is its own set of numbers. For information only, not tax advice; consult a licensed CPA or tax attorney.

## Español (初译待校)

**Impuesto diario n.º 020 — Done las acciones apreciadas, no las venda primero: la ganancia no tributa y deduce el valor de mercado, con tope del 30 % del AGI.** Vender primero y donar el efectivo es el orden común y el caro: la venta reconoce la ganancia y paga impuesto antes de donar. Transfiera las acciones tal cual: la donación no es venta, la apreciación nunca tributa y la deducción se mide al valor de mercado del día del regalo, no a su costo. Ejemplo docente (cifras hipotéticas): acciones compradas por 10.000, hoy 50.000, tenidas más de un año, AGI del donante 200.000. Donación directa: deduce 50.000; el tope del 30 % es 200.000 × 30 % = 60.000, cabe; la ganancia de 40.000 no tributa. Venta previa a una tasa supuesta del 15 %: 40.000 × 15 % = 6.000 se van primero en impuestos y quedan 44.000 para dar. Desde 2026 hay una puerta más: las deducciones benéficas detalladas se reducen primero en el 0,5 % del AGI (OBBBA): 200.000 × 0,5 % = 1.000, y el regalo de 50.000 deduciría 49.000. Cuatro condiciones: tenencia mayor de un año (si es de un año o menos, la sección 170(e)(1) recorta hasta cerca del costo); destinatario calificado (caridad pública; lo dado a personas no cuenta y las fundaciones privadas tienen límites menores); declarar con deducciones detalladas (desde 2026 quien no detalla solo deduce efectivo en línea: 1.000 individual, 2.000 conjunto); y el valor de mercado de acciones cotizadas es el promedio entre el máximo y el mínimo del día (Publication 561), no el cierre. El tope para bienes de ganancia de capital donados a caridad pública es el 30 % del AGI (sección 170(b)(1)(C)); el exceso se arrastra hasta cinco años (sección 170(d)(1)). Papeles: regalos de 250 o más exigen acuse escrito contemporáneo de la institución (sección 170(f)(8)); los no monetarios que sumen más de 500 van en el Form 8283, y los valores cotizados de cualquier monto usan solo la Section A, sin tasación calificada. El regalo se completa cuando las acciones llegan irrevocablemente a la cuenta de la institución: para 2026, la transferencia debe terminar antes del 31 de diciembre. Solo información, no asesoría fiscal. (Traducción inicial pendiente de revisión · 初译待校)

## Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)

**Thuế mỗi ngày số 020 — Hãy tặng cổ phiếu đã tăng giá nguyên trạng, đừng bán trước: phần tăng giá không bị đánh thuế và được khấu trừ theo giá thị trường, trần 30% AGI.** Bán trước rồi tặng tiền mặt là thứ tự phổ biến và cũng là thứ tự tốn kém: bước bán xác nhận lãi vốn và nộp thuế trước. Chuyển thẳng cổ phiếu đi: tặng cho không phải là bán, phần tăng giá không bị đánh thuế, và khấu trừ tính theo giá thị trường ngày tặng, không theo giá vốn. Ví dụ giảng dạy (số giả định): mua 10.000 đô la, nay trị giá 50.000, giữ hơn một năm, AGI người tặng 200.000. Tặng trực tiếp: khấu trừ 50.000; trần 30% là 200.000 × 30% = 60.000 nên vừa; lãi 40.000 không bị đánh thuế. Bán trước với thuế suất giả định 15%: 40.000 × 15% = 6.000 nộp thuế trước, chỉ còn 44.000 để tặng. Từ năm 2026 thêm một ngưỡng: khấu trừ từ thiện theo liệt kê bị giảm trước 0,5% AGI mới bắt đầu tính (OBBBA) — 200.000 × 0,5% = 1.000, nên món quà 50.000 chỉ khấu trừ 49.000. Bốn điều kiện: giữ hơn một năm (từ một năm trở xuống, mục 170(e)(1) cắt phần tăng giá, gần như chỉ tính theo giá vốn); tặng cho tổ chức đủ điều kiện (từ thiện công; tặng cá nhân không được tính và quỹ tư nhân có giới hạn thấp hơn); phải khai theo liệt kê khoản (từ 2026 người không liệt kê chỉ được khấu trừ tiền mặt 1.000/2.000 ở dòng trên); và giá thị trường của cổ phiếu niêm yết là trung bình của giá cao nhất và thấp nhất trong ngày (Publication 561), không phải giá đóng cửa. Trần cho tài sản lãi vốn tặng tổ chức từ thiện công là 30% AGI (mục 170(b)(1)(C)); phần vượt chuyển tiếp tối đa năm năm (mục 170(d)(1)). Bẫy giấy tờ: mỗi món từ 250 trở lên cần văn bản xác nhận đồng thời của tổ chức nhận (mục 170(f)(8)); tặng phi tiền mặt tổng trên 500 phải kèm Form 8283, và chứng khoán niêm yết với bất kỳ số tiền nào cũng chỉ điền Section A, không cần thẩm định đủ điều kiện. Việc tặng hoàn tất khi cổ phiếu vào tài khoản môi giới của tổ chức một cách không thể hủy ngang, nên muốn tính cho năm 2026 thì chuyển khoản phải xong trước 31 tháng 12. Chỉ để chia sẻ thông tin, không phải tư vấn thuế. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính · 初译待校)

## हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)

**रोज़ का कर अंक 020 — बढ़े हुए शेयर पहले बेचें नहीं, वैसे ही दान करें: वृद्धि पर कोई कर नहीं और बाज़ार मूल्य पर कटौती, सीमा AGI की 30%।** पहले बेचकर नकद दान करना सामान्य क्रम है — और महँगा क्रम भी। बिक्री चरण में capital gain पहले निर्धारित होकर कर कटता है। शेयर वैसे ही हस्तांतरित करें: दान बिक्री नहीं है, वृद्धि पर कर नहीं लगता, और कटौती लागत पर नहीं, दान के दिन के बाज़ार मूल्य पर मिलती है। शिक्षण उदाहरण (काल्पनिक अंक): खरीद $10,000, वर्तमान मूल्य $50,000, धारण एक वर्ष से अधिक, दाता का AGI $200,000। सीधा दान: $50,000 कटौती; 30% सीमा $200,000 × 30% = $60,000 है, समा जाती है; $40,000 वृद्धि पर कोई कर नहीं। पहले बेचने पर कल्पित 15% दर से $40,000 × 15% = $6,000 पहले कर में जाता है और दान हेतु $44,000 बचते हैं। 2026 से एक और द्वार है: itemized charitable deductions पहले AGI के 0.5% से घटती हैं (OBBBA) — $200,000 × 0.5% = $1,000, अतः $50,000 का दान वास्तव में $49,000 कटौती देगा। चार शर्तें: एक वर्ष से अधिक धारण (एक वर्ष या कम पर धारा 170(e)(1) वृद्धि काटकर लगभग लागत तक सीमित करती है); योग्य संगठन को दान (सार्वजनिक धर्मार्थ संस्था; व्यक्तियों को देना गिनती में नहीं और private foundations की सीमाएँ और कम हैं); itemize करना आवश्यक (2026 से non-itemizers को केवल नकद की above-the-line कटौती $1,000/$2,000 मिलती है); और सूचीबद्ध शेयर का बाज़ार मूल्य उस दिन के उच्चतम व न्यूनतम का औसत है (Publication 561), closing price नहीं। सार्वजनिक धर्मार्थ संस्था को दिए capital-gain property की सीमा AGI की 30% है (धारा 170(b)(1)(C)); आधिक्य अधिकतम पाँच वर्ष आगे जाता है (धारा 170(d)(1))। कागज़ी फंदे: $250 या अधिक के प्रत्येक दान हेतु संस्था का contemporaneous written acknowledgment चाहिए (धारा 170(f)(8)); कुल $500 से अधिक के noncash दान Form 8283 पर जाते हैं, और सूचीबद्ध प्रतिभूतियाँ किसी भी राशि पर केवल Section A भरती हैं, qualified appraisal नहीं चाहिए। दान तब पूर्ण होता है जब शेयर संस्था के brokerage खाते में अपरिवर्तनीय रूप से पहुँच जाएँ, अतः 2026 के लिए हस्तांतरण 31 दिसंबर से पहले पूरा होना चाहिए। केवल जानकारी के लिए, कर सलाह नहीं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, संशोधन लंबित · 初译待校)
