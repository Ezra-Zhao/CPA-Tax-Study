# 作物保险与农业风险保障怎么管：联邦作物保险 FCIC、RMA 结构与计税口径及加州口径

这篇讲什么：
- 美国的作物保险不是普通商业保险市场自发长出来的，而是《联邦作物保险法》（Federal Crop Insurance Act）搭的公私合营架子：联邦作物保险公司（FCIC）定规则、担再保险与保费补贴，风险管理署（RMA）管日常运营，获批保险商（AIP）把保单卖到农场。
- 保障分两层：巨灾层（CAT）保一半产量、按五成半的价格计，是低门槛的兜底；自购加保层（buy-up）把保障水平往上买，保费补贴随保障水平递减。
- 税务一侧只记可核的口径：保费是农场经营费用、赔款进收入、赔款确认年度有专门的递延选举；公司化农场的资本化规则只作指针。加州农业与旱灾的衔接一句带过，细节以当期口径为准。

## 一、法律骨架：《联邦作物保险法》与三个角色

### 中文
联邦作物保险的根在《联邦作物保险法》（Federal Crop Insurance Act），条文编在《美国法典》第 7 编第 1501 条起（7 U.S.C. §1501 et seq.），1938 年大萧条与尘暴之后立法、1980 年与 1994 年两次大修成今天的样子。三个角色分工写在法里：联邦作物保险公司（Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, FCIC）是农业部内的联邦公司，负责立计划、定条款并为保单提供再保险；风险管理署（Risk Management Agency, RMA）是农业部的运营机构，管费率、产品审批与合规；真正面对农户卖保单、收保费、定损赔付的，是与 FCIC 签约的获批保险商（Approved Insurance Providers, AIP）——私营保险公司。农户买到的是一张由联邦规则定型、联邦补贴保费、联邦再保险兜底的标准化保单。先记住这条链，后面的补贴与计税才有落点。

### English
Federal crop insurance rests on the Federal Crop Insurance Act, codified at 7 U.S.C. section 1501 et seq. — enacted in 1938 after the Depression and the Dust Bowl and reshaped by major reforms in 1980 and 1994. Three roles are written into the statute. The Federal Crop Insurance Corporation (FCIC), a federal corporation inside the Department of Agriculture, designs the program, sets the policy terms, and reinsures the policies. The Risk Management Agency (RMA), the operating agency, handles rates, product approval, and compliance. The policies are actually sold, serviced, and adjusted by Approved Insurance Providers (AIPs) — private insurers under contract with FCIC. What a farmer buys is a standardized policy whose terms are set by federal rules, whose premium is subsidized federally, and whose risk is federally reinsured. Keep that chain in mind; the subsidy and tax treatment below hang on it.

Tres papeles están escritos en la ley. La Corporación Federal de Seguro de Cultivos (Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, FCIC), una corporación federal dentro del Departamento de Agricultura, diseña el programa, fija las condiciones de las pólizas y las reasegura. Quienes venden, atienden y ajustan las pólizas ante el agricultor son los proveedores aprobados (Approved Insurance Providers, AIP), aseguradoras privadas contratadas por la FCIC. Lo que el agricultor compra es una póliza estandarizada cuyas condiciones fija la norma federal, cuya prima está subsidiada federalmente y cuyo riesgo está reasegurado federalmente.

## 二、保障两层：CAT 兜底与 buy-up 加保

### 中文
最基础的一层是巨灾风险保障（Catastrophic Risk Protection, CAT）：按农户实际生产历史产量（APH）的一半给保障，赔付价格取 RMA 公布价格的一定比例（法条口径为 55%），农户基本不付保费、只付一笔行政费，费额以当期 RMA 口径为准。这一层只防「颗粒无收」级别的灾，不保收入的细水长流。往上是自购加保（buy-up）：农户在 CAT 之上选更高的保障水平（coverage level，常见 50% 至 85% 一带、按 5% 递进，以当期 RMA 产品为准），或进一步买产量保障（YP）、收入保障（RP）等产品，把价格风险也包进来。保障水平越高，自己付的保费越多、联邦补贴的比例越低，这个递减结构写在法条第 1508 条 e 款的补贴表里，具体百分比以当期 RMA 口径为准。单元划分（基本单元、选择单元、企业单元）会影响保费与赔付，企业单元把同一作物在全县的地块并成一盘算，保费折扣最大、单块地绝收时却最难触发赔付，这是选单元时真正的取舍。

### English
The base layer is Catastrophic Risk Protection (CAT): coverage at 50 percent of the farm's Actual Production History (APH) yield, paid at a statutory fraction of the RMA-established price — 55 percent under the statute — with the farmer paying essentially no premium, only an administrative fee set by current RMA rules. This layer guards against wipeout-level disaster, not against ordinary income swings. Above it sits buy-up coverage: the farmer elects a higher coverage level (commonly somewhere in the 50-to-85 percent range, in 5-point steps, per current RMA products) or moves to products such as Yield Protection (YP) or Revenue Protection (RP) that also wrap in price risk. The higher the coverage level, the more premium the farmer pays and the lower the federal subsidy share — that declining structure sits in the subsidy schedule of section 1508(e), with exact percentages per current RMA guidance. Unit structure matters as much as the level: enterprise units combine all acreage of a crop in the county into one pot, earning the largest premium discount but making a single-field wipeout hardest to trigger an indemnity — the real trade-off in choosing units.

Esta capa protege contra desastres de aniquilación, no contra oscilaciones ordinarias de ingreso. Encima está la cobertura comprada (buy-up): el agricultor elige un nivel de cobertura mayor (comúnmente en la zona del 50 al 85 por ciento, en pasos de 5, según los productos vigentes de la RMA) o pasa a productos como la protección de rendimiento (YP) o de ingresos (RP), que también envuelven el riesgo de precio. Cuanto mayor el nivel, más prima paga el agricultor y menor es la cuota de subsidio federal: esa estructura decreciente está en la tabla de subsidios de la sección 1508(e), con porcentajes exactos según la guía vigente de la RMA. La estructura de unidades importa tanto como el nivel: las unidades empresariales (enterprise units) combinan toda la superficie de un cultivo en el condado en un solo fondo, con el mayor descuento de prima, pero hacen más difícil que la pérdida total de una sola parcela dispare una indemnización.

## 三、算例：一块玉米地的保障、赔款与保费分担

### 中文
设某农户种玉米 1,000 英亩，APH 产量每英亩 180 蒲式耳，RMA 投影价格每蒲式耳 4.50 美元，选 75% 保障水平的产量保障。第一步，满额产值：180 乘 4.50 等于每英亩 810 美元。第二步，保障额（liability）：810 乘 75% 等于每英亩 607.50 美元，全场 607.50 乘 1,000 等于 607,500 美元。第三步，赔款：若实际收成只有每英亩 100 蒲式耳，触发线是 180 乘 75% 等于每英亩 135 蒲式耳，短缺 135 减 100 等于 35 蒲式耳，每英亩赔款 35 乘 4.50 等于 157.50 美元，全场 157,500 美元（此处用投保价格示意，收入保障的价格口径另有收获价比较，不在此展开）。第四步，保费分担：设该产品全额保费每英亩 30 美元、该保障水平的联邦补贴比例以 55% 示意，则农户自付 30 乘 45% 等于每英亩 13.50 美元、全场 13,500 美元，补贴部分 16.50 美元一英亩。复算：180×4.50＝810，810×75%＝607.50，135－100＝35，35×4.50＝157.50，30×45%＝13.50，两遍一致。补贴比例的现行数以当期 RMA 口径为准，本例的 55% 只是示意。

### English
Take a corn grower with 1,000 acres, an APH yield of 180 bushels per acre, an RMA projected price of $4.50 per bushel, and a 75 percent coverage level under a yield policy. Step one, full value: 180 times 4.50 is $810 per acre. Step two, the liability: 810 times 75 percent is $607.50 per acre, or $607,500 across the farm. Step three, the indemnity: if the actual harvest is only 100 bushels per acre, the trigger is 180 times 75 percent, or 135 bushels per acre; the shortfall is 135 minus 100, or 35 bushels; the indemnity is 35 times 4.50, or $157.50 per acre — $157,500 farm-wide (priced here at the projected price for simplicity; revenue products compare a harvest price as well, outside this example). Step four, the premium split: suppose the full premium is $30 per acre and the federal subsidy share at this coverage level is illustrated at 55 percent; the farmer pays 30 times 45 percent, or $13.50 per acre — $13,500 farm-wide — and the subsidy carries $16.50 per acre. Recheck: 180 × 4.50 = 810; 810 × 75% = 607.50; 135 − 100 = 35; 35 × 4.50 = 157.50; 30 × 45% = 13.50 — verified twice. Current subsidy percentages follow RMA guidance; the 55 percent here is illustrative only.

Sea un productor de maíz con 1.000 acres, un rendimiento APH de 180 bushels por acre, un precio proyectado de la RMA de 4,50 dólares por bushel y un nivel de cobertura del 75 por ciento en una póliza de rendimiento. Paso uno, valor pleno: 180 por 4,50 son 810 dólares por acre. Paso dos, la responsabilidad (liability): 810 por 75 % son 607,50 dólares por acre, o 607.500 dólares en toda la explotación. Paso tres, la indemnización: si la cosecha real es solo 100 bushels por acre, el disparador es 180 por 75 %, es decir, 135 bushels por acre; el déficit es 135 menos 100, o sea, 35 bushels; la indemnización es 35 por 4,50, es decir, 157,50 dólares por acre — 157.500 dólares en total (aquí al precio proyectado por simplicidad; los productos de ingresos comparan además un precio de cosecha, fuera de este ejemplo). Paso cuatro, el reparto de la prima: suponga una prima total de 30 dólares por acre y una cuota de subsidio federal ilustrativa del 55 por ciento en este nivel; el agricultor paga 30 por 45 %, es decir, 13,50 dólares por acre — 13.500 en total — y el subsidio carga con 16,50 por acre. Comprobación: 180 × 4,50 = 810; 810 × 75 % = 607,50; 135 − 100 = 35; 35 × 4,50 = 157,50; 30 × 45 % = 13,50, verificado dos veces. Los porcentajes vigentes de subsidio siguen la guía de la RMA; el 55 por ciento aquí es solo ilustrativo.

## 四、计税口径：保费、赔款与确认年度

### 中文

### English

First, the farmer-paid share of the premium is an ordinary business expense under section 162, deductible in the year paid or accrued; the federal subsidy share never passes through the farmer's hands and is neither income nor a deduction. Fourth, pointers only: incorporated farms face the preproductive-period capitalization rules in the section 447 area, and farming syndicate limits sit around section 464; both are located here, not developed.

## 五、加州衔接（一句带过）与收尾

### 中文
加州没有与联邦作物保险平行的州级作物保险计划，州内农户买的同样是 FCIC／RMA 体系的联邦保单；州的角色更多在旱灾等灾害时的州级救济、用水与农业监管，与联邦赔款在州税上的处理衔接，细节以加州当期口径为准，本篇只带这一句。再补一句全局的：作物保险只兜产量与价格的市场化风险，巨灾救助（如农业部的灾害援助计划）是另一套事后救济，两者不要混为一谈；农户的风险盘子里，保险管可保的那层，留存与对冲管剩下的那层。

### English
California runs no state-level crop insurance program parallel to the federal one: California growers buy the same FCIC/RMA federal policies, and the state's role sits mainly in state-level disaster relief during droughts and in water and agricultural regulation, with the state-tax handling of federal indemnities following current California guidance — one sentence is all this article gives it. One global note to close: crop insurance covers the market-priced layer of yield and price risk, while ad hoc disaster assistance (such as USDA disaster aid programs) is a separate, after-the-fact relief system — do not conflate them. In a farm's risk stack, insurance takes the insurable layer; retention and hedging take the rest.

En la pila de riesgos de una explotación, el seguro toma la capa asegurable; la retención y la cobertura toman el resto.

### 한국어 (初译待校)
캘리포니아에는 연방과 나란히 서는 주 차원의 작물보험 프로그램이 없습니다. 주 안의 농민도 같은 FCIC/RMA 연방 보험을 사고, 주의 역할은 주로 가뭄 같은 재해 때의 주 차원 구제와 물·농업 규제에 있으며, 연방 배상금의 주세 취급은 캘리포니아 당기 기준을 따릅니다. 이 글에서는 이 한 문장만 둡니다. 마무리로 전역 메모 하나: 작물보험은 시장 가격으로 잴 수 있는 수량·가격 위험 층을 덮고, 재난 지원(예: USDA 재난 원조 프로그램)은 사후에 움직이는 별개의 구제 체계이니 둘을 섞지 마십시오. 농장의 위험 층위에서 보험은 보험 가능한 층을, 유보와 헤지는 나머지를 맡습니다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
California không có chương trình bảo hiểm cây trồng cấp bang song song với chương trình liên bang: nông dân trong bang mua cùng các hợp đồng liên bang FCIC/RMA, và vai trò của bang chủ yếu nằm ở cứu trợ cấp bang khi hạn hán và ở quản lý nước và nông nghiệp, với cách xử lý thuế bang đối với tiền bồi thường liên bang theo hướng dẫn hiện hành của California — bài này chỉ dành một câu cho điểm đó. Một ghi chú toàn cục để khép lại: bảo hiểm cây trồng bao phủ lớp rủi ro năng suất và giá được định giá theo thị trường, còn hỗ trợ thảm họa ad hoc (như các chương trình trợ giúp thảm họa của USDA) là một hệ thống cứu trợ khác, chạy sau sự việc — đừng lẫn hai thứ. Trong chồng rủi ro của một nông trại, bảo hiểm nhận lớp có thể bảo hiểm được; phần tự giữ và phòng hộ nhận phần còn lại.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)
California में संघीय कार्यक्रम के समानांतर कोई राज्य-स्तरीय फ़सल बीमा कार्यक्रम नहीं है: राज्य के किसान वही FCIC/RMA संघीय पॉलिसियाँ खरीदते हैं, और राज्य की भूमिका मुख्यतः सूखे जैसी आपदाओं में राज्य-स्तरीय राहत तथा जल व कृषि नियमन में है, संघीय मुआवज़ों का राज्य-कर व्यवहार California के वर्तमान मार्गदर्शन के अनुसार होगा — इस लेख में इस पर बस यही एक वाक्य है। समापन पर एक वैश्विक टिप्पणी: फ़सल बीमा उपज और मूल्य जोखिम की वह परत ढकता है जिसका बाज़ार मूल्य लगता है, जबकि तदर्थ आपदा सहायता (जैसे USDA के आपदा सहायता कार्यक्रम) एक अलग, घटना-पश्चात राहत प्रणाली है — दोनों को एक न समझें। खेत के जोखिम-ढेर में बीमा बीमा-योग्य परत लेता है; धारण और हेजिंग बाकी लेते हैं।
