# HSA 雇主可比供款怎么管：第 4980G 条借用第 4980E 条规则、35% 税额与第 414 条控制集团及加州口径

第 4980G 条是第 43 章里最短的条文之一，全文只有四款，真正的规则全靠借用：它把第 4980E 条为 Archer MSA 写的可比供款规则整体搬给 HSA，再加一条高薪员工例外。这篇走条文逐款：课税触发、税额公式、可比定义、分组规则、豁免结构与申报协调，一款一款对原文。实务判断与发钱清单见本仓常用篇《雇主给员工 HSA 存钱，为什么必须人人一样多》。

---

## 一、本体位置：四款条文与立法沿革

### 中文
第 4980G 条由 2003 年《医疗保险处方药、改进与现代化法》（Pub. L. 108-173）第 1201 条第 (d) 款第 (4) 项第 (A) 目加入，2006 年经 Pub. L. 109-432 第 306 条第 (a) 款修订。四款分工：第 (a) 款立课税本体，雇主在日历年内给任何员工的 HSA 供款而未满足第 (b) 款要求的，对该失败课税；第 (b) 款只有一句话，与第 4980E 条相同或类似的规则与要求适用于本条，税额、定义、豁免都到第 4980E 条里找；第 (c) 款授权财政部长制定条例，包括同时给 Archer MSA 与 HSA 供款的雇主的特别规则，落实为 Reg. 第 54.4980G-1 条至第 54.4980G-7 条一带的可比供款条例与 IRS 问答（如 Notice 2008-52）；第 (d) 款是本条唯一的自有实体规则，高薪员工例外，第四节细说。与邻近条文分工：第 4980E 条管 Archer MSA 本体、第 4980B 条管 COBRA、第 4980D 条管团体健康计划合规，三者与本条同走 Form 8928 申报；员工侧的 HSA 资格与供款上限在第 223 条，雇主供款的员工侧排除在第 106 条第 (d) 款。

### English
Section 4980G was added by section 1201(d)(4)(A) of the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and Modernization Act of 2003 (Pub. L. 108-173) and amended in 2006 by section 306(a) of Pub. L. 109-432. Its four subsections divide the work: (a) states the tax, imposed on an employer that contributes to any employee's HSA during a calendar year and fails the requirements of subsection (b); (b) is a single sentence importing rules and requirements similar to those of section 4980E, so the tax amount, definitions, and waiver all live in section 4980E; (c) authorizes Treasury regulations, including special rules for employers contributing to both Archer MSAs and HSAs, implemented in the comparability regulations at Treas. Reg. section 54.4980G-1 through -7 and IRS guidance such as Notice 2008-52; and (d) is the section's only home-grown substantive rule, the highly compensated employee exception, detailed in Part IV. Neighboring provisions divide the field: section 4980E governs Archer MSAs themselves, section 4980B COBRA, and section 4980D group health plan compliance, all reported with this section on Form 8928; employee-side HSA eligibility and contribution limits sit in section 223, and the employee-side exclusion for employer contributions in section 106(d).

## 二、税额公式：第 4980E 条第 (b) 款的 35%

### 中文
税额公式借第 4980E 条第 (b) 款：税额等于雇主在员工纳税年度落入该日历年内的期间里，给员工账户供款总额的 35%。三个读法要点。一是比例固定 35%，不按违规人数、不按差额大小缩放。二是基数是供款总额：同一雇主当年给全体员工 HSA 的每一美元直给供款都进基数，哪怕只有一组人不可比。三是时间口径按员工纳税年度落点表述，绝大多数员工是日历年纳税人，实务上即当年供款总额。与常用篇同数对读：供款总额 200,000 美元，税额 200,000 乘以 35% 等于 70,000 美元；供款总额 80,000 美元，税额 80,000 乘以 35% 等于 28,000 美元。这笔税是雇主层面的消费税，不得转嫁员工，也不是员工的所得税问题；它与第 4980D 条那种按日计罚的消费税不同，是一年一算的总量罚。

### English
The amount comes from section 4980E(b): the tax equals 35 percent of the aggregate amount contributed by the employer to employees' accounts for taxable years of the employees ending with or within the calendar year. Three reading points. First, the rate is a flat 35 percent, scaled neither by the number of violations nor by the size of any gap. Second, the base is total contributions: every dollar of direct employer HSA contributions for the year enters the base, even if only one group failed comparability. Third, timing is keyed to employees' taxable years, which for most employees are calendar years, so in practice the base is the year's total contributions. Reading with the companion article's numbers: total contributions of $200,000 produce tax of $200,000 times 35 percent, or $70,000; total contributions of $80,000 produce $80,000 times 35 percent, or $28,000. This is an employer-level excise tax, not shiftable to employees and not an employee income tax issue; unlike the per-day excise under section 4980D, it is a once-a-year aggregate charge.

### Español (译稿)
El monto viene del § 4980E(b): el impuesto es el 35 % del total aportado por el empleador a las cuentas de los empleados por años fiscales de los empleados que terminan en ese año civil o dentro de él. Tres claves de lectura. Primero, la tasa es fija, 35 %, sin escalar por el número de incumplimientos ni por el tamaño de la diferencia. Segundo, la base es el total aportado: cada dólar de aportación directa del empleador a HSA en el año entra en la base, aunque solo un grupo haya fallado la comparabilidad. Tercero, el tiempo se mide por los años fiscales de los empleados, que para la mayoría son años civiles, así que en la práctica la base es el total del año. Con los números del artículo práctico: aportaciones totales de 200.000 dólares dan un impuesto de 200.000 por 35 %, es decir 70.000 dólares; totales de 80.000 dólares dan 80.000 por 35 %, es decir 28.000 dólares. Es un impuesto especial a nivel del empleador, no trasladable a los empleados ni un asunto de su impuesto sobre la renta; a diferencia del impuesto por día del § 4980D, es un cargo anual y global.

### 한국어 (초역·待校)
세액 공식은 §4980E(b)에서 빌려옵니다. 세액은 고용주가 직원의 과세연도가 그 역년 안에 끝나거나 그 안에 드는 기간에 직원 계좌에 납입한 총액의 35%입니다. 읽는 법 세 가지. 첫째, 세율은 고정 35%로, 위반 인원수나 차이의 크기로 조정되지 않습니다. 둘째, 기준은 납입 총액입니다. 그해 고용주의 직접 HSA 납입 한 푼까지 모두 기준에 들어가며, 한 그룹만 비교가능성을 깨도 마찬가지입니다. 셋째, 시점은 직원의 과세연도를 기준으로 표현되지만 대다수 직원은 역년 납세자이므로 실무에서는 그해 납입 총액입니다. 실무편과 같은 숫자로 읽으면, 납입 총액 200,000달러이면 세액은 200,000달러 곱하기 35%로 70,000달러, 총액 80,000달러이면 80,000달러 곱하기 35%로 28,000달러입니다. 이 세금은 고용주 층위의 소비세로 직원에게 전가할 수 없고 직원의 소득세 문제도 아니며, §4980D의 일 단위 소비세와 달리 연 1회 총량으로 매겨집니다. (초역·待校)

## 三、可比定义逐款：第 4980E 条第 (d) 款

### 中文
义务本体在第 4980E 条第 (d) 款第 (1) 项：雇主给参与员工供款的，必须给全体可比参与员工做可比供款。第 (2) 项第 (A) 目给可比下定义：供款要么金额相同，要么是覆盖该员工的高免赔健康计划年度免赔额的同一百分比。第 (2) 项第 (B) 目管不满年：在职只有部分年度的员工，供款与全年可比额的比例等于其在职部分与全年的比例时，视为可比。第 (3) 项定义可比参与员工：被雇主任何 HDHP 覆盖的合格个人，且覆盖类别相同；类别只有两档，自付型与家庭型。第 (4) 项把兼职拆开：本项单独适用于兼职员工与非兼职员工，兼职指通常每周工作不到 30 小时的人。第 (e) 款是控制集团规则：按第 414 条第 (b)、(c)、(m)、(o) 款视为一个雇主的全部主体，在本条下算一个雇主，关联公司不能靠拆主体把人分组洗掉。第 (f) 款定义借用：与第 220 条共用的术语按第 220 条含义，HSA 侧经条例转到第 223 条的对应定义。

### English
The duty itself is in section 4980E(d)(1): an employer that contributes for participating employees must make comparable contributions for all comparable participating employees. Paragraph (d)(2)(A) defines comparable contributions: contributions that are either the same amount, or the same percentage of the annual deductible limit under the high deductible health plan covering the employee. Paragraph (d)(2)(B) covers part-year employees: a contribution counts as comparable if it bears the same ratio to the full-year comparable amount as the portion of the year employed bears to the whole year. Paragraph (d)(3) defines comparable participating employees: eligible individuals covered under any HDHP of the employer who share the same category of coverage, the categories being self-only and family coverage. Paragraph (d)(4) applies the rules separately to part-time employees, defined as those customarily employed for fewer than 30 hours a week, and to other employees. Paragraph (e) is the controlled group rule: all persons treated as a single employer under section 414(b), (c), (m), or (o) are treated as one employer, so related entities cannot launder groupings by splitting entities. Paragraph (f) borrows definitions from section 220, which the regulations map to the corresponding section 223 definitions on the HSA side.

## 四、例外与豁免：高薪例外、合理理由与自助餐计划通道

### 中文
例外有三层，来源层级各不相同，要分开记。第一层是条文本体的第 4980G 条第 (d) 款：为适用第 4980E 条之目的，就给非高薪员工的供款而言，高薪员工（第 414 条第 (q) 款定义）不视为可比参与员工。效果是单向的：给高管少给不连累普通员工组；高管组内部若有直给供款，高管之间仍要可比。第二层是第 4980E 条第 (c) 款的部长豁免：失败出于合理理由而非故意疏忽的，财政部长可以在缴税相对于失败程度明显过重时，减免一部分或全部税额，这是裁量减免，要举证、要申请，不自动发生。第三层不在条文里，在条例与 IRS 口径里：通过第 125 条自助餐计划做出的供款（员工工资减额与计划内的雇主供款）不适用可比规则（Publication 969 与 Reg. 第 54.4980G 条口径），因为自助餐计划另有第 125 条的不歧视规则管着。离职员工的口径同在这一层：雇主没有义务给已离职者供款，一旦给了，离职者按覆盖类别自成一组、组内可比（Notice 2008-52 问答口径）。

### English
The exceptions come in three layers from three different sources, and they should be kept apart. Layer one is statutory, section 4980G(d): for purposes of applying section 4980E to a contribution to an employee who is not a highly compensated employee (section 414(q)), highly compensated employees are not treated as comparable participating employees. The effect runs one way: giving executives less does not contaminate the rank-and-file group, while direct contributions among executives themselves must still be comparable within that group. Layer two is the Secretary's waiver in section 4980E(c): where a failure is due to reasonable cause and not willful neglect, the Secretary may waive part or all of the tax to the extent payment would be excessive relative to the failure — discretionary relief that must be evidenced and requested, never automatic. Layer three is regulatory and administrative rather than statutory: contributions made through a section 125 cafeteria plan, both employee salary reductions and employer contributions inside the plan, are outside the comparability rules (Publication 969 and Treas. Reg. section 54.4980G), because cafeteria plans answer to section 125's own nondiscrimination regime. The former-employee rule sits in the same layer: an employer need not contribute for former employees, but if it does, they form their own group by coverage category and must be comparable within it (Notice 2008-52 guidance).

### Español (译稿)
Las excepciones vienen en tres capas de tres fuentes distintas, y conviene no mezclarlas. La primera es legal, el § 4980G(d): a efectos de aplicar el § 4980E a una aportación a un empleado que no sea altamente remunerado (§ 414(q)), los altamente remunerados no se tratan como empleados participantes comparables. El efecto es unidireccional: dar menos a los directivos no contamina al grupo común, mientras que las aportaciones directas entre los propios directivos deben seguir siendo comparables dentro de ese grupo. La segunda es la condonación del Secretario en el § 4980E(c): si el incumplimiento se debe a causa razonable y no a negligencia deliberada, el Secretario puede condonar parte o todo el impuesto en la medida en que el pago resulte excesivo frente al incumplimiento; es un alivio discrecional que hay que probar y solicitar, nunca automático. La tercera es reglamentaria y administrativa, no legal: las aportaciones hechas mediante un plan de cafetería del § 125, tanto las reducciones salariales del empleado como las del empleador dentro del plan, quedan fuera de las reglas de comparabilidad (Publicación 969 y Reg. § 54.4980G), porque los planes de cafetería responden al régimen antidiscriminatorio propio del § 125. La regla de los exempleados está en la misma capa: el empleador no está obligado a aportar por exempleados, pero si lo hace, forman su propio grupo por categoría de cobertura y deben ser comparables dentro de él (guía del Notice 2008-52).

## 五、申报协调与加州口径

### 中文
申报分两条线。消费税线：第 4980G 条的税随第 43 章消费税走 Form 8928 申报，与第 4980B、4980D、4980E 条的税同表分项，具体申报期限以当期 Form 8928 说明为准。工资线：雇主供款在员工 W-2 第 12 栏以代码 W 报告，金额同时是员工侧第 106 条第 (d) 款排除与第 223 条供款上限核算的输入，两条线的数字必须对得上。员工侧提醒一句：雇主供款计入员工个人的 HSA 年度上限，雇主给得多、员工自己再存时要合并算，别双边都顶格存出超额。加州口径：加州不跟随第 223 条的 HSA 体系，雇主供款在加州是员工工资、账户收益在加州计税（FTB 口径，州层证据等级 B），联邦排除与州加回并行，细节见常用篇第五节。本篇为个人学习笔记，不构成税务建议；条文以 26 U.S.C. 第 4980E、4980G 条原文为准，适用前请核当期条例、IRS 公告与 Form 8928 说明，并咨询持牌 CPA 或税务律师。

### English
Reporting runs on two tracks. On the excise track, the section 4980G tax is reported on Form 8928 with the other Chapter 43 excise taxes under sections 4980B, 4980D, and 4980E, itemized on the same form; the filing deadline follows the current Form 8928 instructions. On the wage track, employer contributions are reported in Box 12, Code W, of the employee's W-2, and that figure is also the input for the employee-side section 106(d) exclusion and the section 223 contribution limit, so the two tracks must reconcile. One employee-side caution: employer contributions count toward the employee's own annual HSA limit, so an employee whose employer contributes generously must aggregate before adding personal contributions, or the pair can overshoot into excess contributions. California does not conform to the section 223 HSA regime: employer contributions are California wages and account earnings are taxable by California (FTB guidance; state-level evidence grade B), with the federal exclusion and the state add-back running side by side, as detailed in Part V of the companion article. This article is a personal study note and does not constitute tax advice; the controlling texts are 26 U.S.C. sections 4980E and 4980G, and current regulations, IRS announcements, and the Form 8928 instructions should be checked before applying them, with advice from a licensed CPA or tax attorney.

### Español (译稿)
La declaración va por dos vías. En la vía del impuesto especial, el impuesto del § 4980G se declara en el Formulario 8928 junto con los demás impuestos especiales del Capítulo 43 de los §§ 4980B, 4980D y 4980E, desglosados en el mismo formulario; el plazo sigue las instrucciones vigentes del Formulario 8928. En la vía salarial, las aportaciones del empleador se declaran en la casilla 12, código W, del W-2 del empleado, y esa cifra es también el insumo para la exclusión del § 106(d) y el límite de aportación del § 223 del lado del empleado, así que ambas vías deben cuadrar. Un aviso del lado del empleado: las aportaciones del empleador cuentan para el límite anual de HSA del propio empleado, de modo que quien recibe aportaciones generosas debe sumar antes de aportar por su cuenta, o la suma puede pasarse a exceso. California no se ajusta al régimen HSA del § 223: las aportaciones del empleador son salario en California y los rendimientos de la cuenta tributan en California (criterio de la FTB; evidencia estatal de nivel B), con la exclusión federal y la suma estatal corriendo en paralelo, como se detalla en la parte V del artículo práctico. Este artículo es una nota personal de estudio y no constituye asesoramiento fiscal; los textos que mandan son 26 U.S.C. §§ 4980E y 4980G, y antes de aplicarlos deben revisarse el reglamento vigente, los anuncios del IRS y las instrucciones del Formulario 8928, con asesoría de un CPA con licencia o un abogado fiscal.

### 한국어 (초역·待校)
신고는 두 줄기로 갑니다. 소비세 줄기에서 §4980G의 세금은 §4980B, 4980D, 4980E의 다른 제43장 소비세와 함께 Form 8928에 항목별로 신고하며, 신고 기한은 당기 Form 8928 안내를 따릅니다. 임금 줄기에서 고용주 납입은 직원 W-2의 12번 칸 코드 W로 보고되고, 그 금액은 직원 측 §106(d) 제외와 §223 납입 한도 계산의 입력값이기도 하므로 두 줄기의 숫자가 맞아야 합니다. 직원 측 주의 한 가지: 고용주 납입은 직원 본인의 HSA 연간 한도에 합산되므로, 고용주가 넉넉히 주는 직원은 본인 납입을 더하기 전에 합쳐 계산해야 초과 납입을 피할 수 있습니다. 캘리포니아는 §223의 HSA 체계에 연동하지 않습니다. 고용주 납입은 캘리포니아에서 임금이고 계좌 수익은 캘리포니아에서 과세됩니다(FTB 기준, 주 층위 증거 등급 B). 연방 제외와 주 가산이 나란히 가며, 자세한 내용은 실무편 제5절과 같습니다. 이 글은 개인 학습 노트이며 세무 조언이 아닙니다. 준거 문헌은 26 U.S.C. §4980E와 §4980G이며, 적용 전에 당기 규정, IRS 공고와 Form 8928 안내를 확인하고 면허 CPA나 세무 변호사에게 상담하십시오. (초역·待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)
Việc khai báo đi theo hai đường. Ở đường thuế tiêu thụ đặc biệt, thuế theo Điều 4980G được khai trên Form 8928 cùng các thuế Chương 43 khác theo các Điều 4980B, 4980D và 4980E, liệt kê theo mục trên cùng tờ khai; hạn nộp theo hướng dẫn Form 8928 hiện hành. Ở đường tiền lương, khoản đóng của người sử dụng lao động được khai ở Ô 12, Mã W, trên W-2 của nhân viên, và con số đó cũng là đầu vào cho việc loại trừ theo Điều 106(d) và hạn mức đóng góp Điều 223 phía nhân viên, nên hai đường phải khớp nhau. Một lưu ý phía nhân viên: khoản đóng của người sử dụng lao động được tính vào hạn mức HSA hằng năm của chính nhân viên, nên ai được chủ đóng nhiều phải cộng gộp trước khi tự đóng thêm, kẻo cả hai phía cùng chạm trần thành đóng vượt. California không tương thích với chế độ HSA của Điều 223: khoản đóng của người sử dụng lao động là tiền lương tại California và thu nhập trong tài khoản bị California đánh thuế (hướng dẫn FTB; bằng chứng cấp bang mức B), với loại trừ liên bang và cộng lại cấp bang chạy song song, như Phần V của bài thực hành đã nêu. Bài viết này là ghi chú học tập cá nhân, không cấu thành tư vấn thuế; văn bản chuẩn là 26 U.S.C. các Điều 4980E và 4980G, và trước khi áp dụng cần kiểm tra quy định hiện hành, các công bố của IRS và hướng dẫn Form 8928, đồng thời tham vấn CPA có giấy phép hoặc luật sư thuế. (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
रिपोर्टिंग दो रास्तों पर चलती है। उत्पाद-कर रास्ते पर, §4980G का कर §§4980B, 4980D और 4980E के अन्य Chapter 43 उत्पाद करों के साथ Form 8928 पर मदवार रिपोर्ट होता है; दाखिल समय-सीमा वर्तमान Form 8928 निर्देशों के अनुसार होगी। वेतन रास्ते पर, नियोक्ता योगदान कर्मचारी के W-2 के Box 12, Code W में रिपोर्ट होता है, और वही आँकड़ा कर्मचारी-पक्ष के §106(d) अपवर्जन और §223 योगदान सीमा का इनपुट भी है, इसलिए दोनों रास्तों का मेल ज़रूरी है। कर्मचारी-पक्ष की एक सावधानी: नियोक्ता योगदान कर्मचारी की अपनी वार्षिक HSA सीमा में गिना जाता है, इसलिए जिसे नियोक्ता उदारता से देता हो, उसे अपना योगदान जोड़ने से पहले कुल जोड़ना चाहिए, वरना दोनों ओर से सीमा पार होकर अतिरिक्त योगदान बन सकता है। California §223 की HSA व्यवस्था के अनुरूप नहीं है: नियोक्ता योगदान California में वेतन है और खाते की आय California में करयोग्य है (FTB मार्गदर्शन; राज्य-स्तरीय साक्ष्य श्रेणी B), संघीय अपवर्जन और राज्य की वापस-जोड़ समानांतर चलती हैं, जैसा साथी लेख के भाग V में है। यह लेख व्यक्तिगत अध्ययन नोट है और कर सलाह नहीं है; नियंत्रक पाठ 26 U.S.C. §§4980E और 4980G हैं, और लागू करने से पहले वर्तमान विनियम, IRS घोषणाएँ और Form 8928 निर्देश जाँचें, तथा लाइसेंसधारी CPA या कर वकील से परामर्श करें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
