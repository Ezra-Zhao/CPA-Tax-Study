# 第 4977 条怎么管：超额附加福利 30% 选举税、(b) 款 1% 公式、(c) 款 1984 业务线拟制及加州口径

> 个人学习笔记，梳理《国内收入法典》第 4977 条的条文本体：它是一笔以雇主选举为开关的税，税基是第 132 条 (a) 款第 (1)(2) 项福利价值超过报酬总额 1% 的部分，税率 30%。口径以第 4977 条条文逐款回核。仓内常用财税常识板块有同题实务篇管算账与动作顺序，两篇算例同数对读；CPA备考板块《第 132 条附加福利排除》篇管员工一侧排除，本篇管雇主一侧的税。

## 一、本体定位：选举是开关，(a) 款只对选进来的雇主课税

### 中文

第 4977 条沿革：1984 年《赤字削减法》（Pub. L. 98-369）A 编第 531 条 (e) 款第 (1) 项入典（98 Stat. 885），1986 年 Pub. L. 99-514 第 1853 条 (c) 款改写第 (c) 款第 (2) 项并增设第 (f) 款，1993 年 Pub. L. 103-66 与 1996 年 Pub. L. 104-188 只动第 (c) 款尾段的交叉引用（第 132 条 (g)(2) 到 (i)(2) 再到 (h) 款）。本体骨架六款分工清楚：(a) 款立税，(b) 款定义税基，(c) 款给选举的对价，(d) 款定选举期间，(e) 款合并受控集团，(f) 款限地域。第 (a) 款的句首限定是全条的门：「对本条选举对其适用的雇主」，在该日历年课以等于超额附加福利 30% 的税。没有选举，本条不产生任何税；本条也不改写第 132 条本身的排除规则，它是在排除之上、雇主一侧另起的一笔账。

### English

Section 4977 was added by section 531(e)(1) of division A of Public Law 98-369 in 1984 (98 Stat. 885); Public Law 99-514, section 1853(c), rewrote paragraph (c)(2) and added subsection (f) in 1986, and Public Laws 103-66 (1993) and 104-188 (1996) only moved the cross-reference at the end of subsection (c) — from section 132(g)(2) to (i)(2) and then to (h). The six subsections divide the work cleanly: (a) imposes the tax, (b) defines the base, (c) states what the election buys, (d) sets the election's period, (e) aggregates controlled groups, and (f) limits the territory. The opening limitation of subsection (a) is the gate for the whole section: in the case of an employer to whom an election under this section applies for a calendar year, a tax is imposed for that year equal to 30 percent of the excess fringe benefits. Without an election the section produces no tax at all, and it does not rewrite section 132's exclusion rules — it is a separate employer-side account laid on top of them.

## 二、(b) 款税基：两类福利价值减报酬的 1%

### 中文

第 (b) 款把「超额附加福利」定义成一个差额，由两个分量相减。被减数：雇主在该日历年内提供的附加福利中，依第 132 条 (a) 款第 (1) 项（无额外成本服务）与第 (2) 项（合格员工折扣）没有计入总收入的那部分的合计价值。减数：雇主在该年付给员工的报酬总额的 1%，且该报酬须是为第一章之目的计入总收入的报酬——两个分量都有口径锁：福利一侧只锁第 (1)(2) 项两类，报酬一侧只锁计入总收入的那部分。算例与实务篇同数对读：报酬 10,000,000 美元，1% 为 100,000 美元；两类福利价值 250,000 美元，超额 150,000 美元，(a) 款税额 45,000 美元。报酬 2,000,000 美元时 1% 为 20,000 美元，福利 18,000 美元在减数之内，差额为负，按定义超额为零、无税。本条没有第二级税、没有管理人税、没有封顶：税额就是超额乘 30%，超额多大税多大，这与第 42、43 章那些两级消费税的结构根本不同，答题时不要把纠正、封顶那套搬进来。

### English

Subsection (b) defines excess fringe benefits as a difference of two components. The minuend is the aggregate value of the fringe benefits provided by the employer during the calendar year that were not includible in gross income under paragraphs (1) (no-additional-cost services) and (2) (qualified employee discounts) of section 132(a). The subtrahend is 1 percent of the aggregate compensation paid by the employer to employees during the year that was includible in gross income for purposes of chapter 1. Both components carry a lock: on the benefit side only the paragraph (1) and (2) categories count, and on the compensation side only amounts includible in gross income. The worked figures match the companion practical piece: compensation of $10,000,000 gives $100,000; benefits of $250,000 leave an excess of $150,000 and a subsection (a) tax of $45,000. With compensation of $2,000,000 the 1 percent is $20,000 and benefits of $18,000 fall inside it, so the difference is negative, the excess is zero and there is no tax. The section has no second-tier tax, no manager tax and no cap: the tax is simply the excess times 30 percent, however large the excess is — structurally unlike the two-tier excise taxes of chapters 42 and 43, whose correction and cap machinery must not be imported here.

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

El apartado (b) define los beneficios adicionales en exceso como la diferencia de dos componentes. El minuendo es el valor total de los beneficios proporcionados por el empleador en el año civil que no fueron incluibles en el ingreso bruto según los párrafos (1) (servicios sin costo adicional) y (2) (descuentos calificados) del artículo 132(a). El sustraendo es el 1 por ciento de la remuneración total pagada a los empleados en ese año que fue incluible en el ingreso bruto a efectos del capítulo 1. Ambos componentes tienen cerradura: del lado de los beneficios solo cuentan las categorías de los párrafos (1) y (2), y del lado de la remuneración solo lo incluible. Las cifras coinciden con la pieza práctica: remuneración de 10.000.000, el 1 por ciento es 100.000; beneficios de 250.000 dejan un exceso de 150.000 y un impuesto de 45.000 dólares. Con remuneración de 2.000.000, el 1 por ciento es 20.000 y unos beneficios de 18.000 quedan dentro, con exceso cero y sin impuesto. El artículo no tiene segundo nivel, ni impuesto al gerente, ni tope: el impuesto es simplemente el exceso por el 30 por ciento, a diferencia estructural de los impuestos especiales de dos niveles de los capítulos 42 y 43. (Traducción inicial, pendiente de revisión)

Số bị trừ là tổng giá trị các phúc lợi người sử dụng lao động đã cung cấp trong năm dương lịch mà không bị tính vào tổng thu nhập theo đoạn (1) (dịch vụ không thêm chi phí) và đoạn (2) (giảm giá nhân viên đủ điều kiện) của điều 132(a). ## 三、(c) 款对价与 (d) 款期间：跨线拟制只保 1984 年存量线

### 中文

第 (c) 款的拟制有两道前置：其一，选举在该日历年对雇主有效；其二，自 1984 年 1 月 1 日起至该年结束的全部时间里，雇主的实质上全部员工都有权拿到雇主 1 条业务线里的员工折扣。两道都齐，就第 132 条 (a) 款第 (1)(2) 项而言（条文括注：第 132 条 (h) 款除外），雇主在 1984 年 1 月 1 日已经存在的任何业务线的全部员工，视为那条被指业务线的员工。拟制的射程因此被两个日期锁死：被保的线必须是 1984 年 1 月 1 日已存在的线，1984 年后新设的线进不了这道拟制；被保的员工资格也只往第 (1)(2) 项上贴，不往 (h) 款的业务线定义本身上贴。第 (d) 款只有一句：选举适用于作出选举的日历年和此后全部日历年，除非雇主撤销。本条没有逐年重选、没有自动到期，撤销是唯一的出口，选举的成本收益要按跨年口径算，不能按单年算。

### English

The deeming rule of subsection (c) has two preconditions: an election in effect for the employer for the calendar year, and, at all times on or after January 1, 1984 and before the close of that year, substantially all of the employer's employees entitled to employee discounts on goods or services provided by the employer in one line of business. When both hold, then for purposes of section 132(a)(1) and (2) — the text adds, but not for purposes of section 132(h) — all employees of any line of business of the employer that was in existence on January 1, 1984 are treated as employees of that referenced line. The rule's reach is thus locked by two dates: a protected line must have existed on January 1, 1984, and lines created later cannot enter the deeming; and the preserved employee status attaches only to paragraphs (1) and (2), not to the line-of-business definition in subsection (h) itself. Subsection (d) is one sentence: an election applies to the calendar year for which it is made and all subsequent calendar years unless revoked by the employer. There is no annual re-election and no automatic expiry; revocation is the only exit, so the election's costs and benefits must be reckoned across years, not for a single year.

## 四、(e)(f) 款边界与加州口径：集团合并算、境外雇佣原则上不管

### 中文

第 (e) 款是防拆分规则：依第 414 条 (b) 款（受控公司集团）、(c) 款（共同控制下的行业或企业）或 (m) 款（关联服务集团）被当作受单一雇主雇佣的全部员工，在本条之下也当作受单一雇主雇佣——报酬总额的 1% 线与福利价值合计都按合并后的雇主算，集团内把员工或业务线挪到不同法人名下，不改变这本合并账。第 (f) 款是地域规则：除条例另有规定外，本条只就美国境内的雇佣适用，境外雇佣对应的福利与报酬原则上两边都不进公式。与近邻条文的分工也要分清：第 4976 条罚福利基金把钱用错方向，第 4975 条罚退休计划禁止交易，第 4977 条罚的却是雇主自愿选举之后福利给得过量，三条的开关、税基与对象各不相同。加州口径：加州没有第 4977 条式的雇主选举税，《收入与税法典》不衔接本条，联邦的选举、拟制与税额都不带入加州申报；加州员工福利的排除与计入按加州对联邦条文的衔接口径另行判断（州层为 B 级口径，联邦条文为 A 级）。

### English

Subsection (e) is the anti-splitting rule: all employees treated as employed by a single employer under section 414(b) (controlled group of corporations), (c) (trades or businesses under common control) or (m) (affiliated service groups) are treated as employed by a single employer for purposes of this section — both the 1 percent compensation line and the aggregate benefit value are computed for the combined employer, so moving employees or lines among group entities does not change the combined account. Subsection (f) is the territorial rule: except as otherwise provided in regulations, the section applies only with respect to employment within the United States, so benefits and compensation tied to foreign employment stay out of both sides of the formula in principle. Neighboring sections divide different work: section 4976 taxes welfare benefit funds that pay out in the wrong direction, section 4975 taxes prohibited transactions in retirement plans, while section 4977 taxes an electing employer whose benefits run over the line — different switches, bases and targets. California: the state has no section 4977-style employer election tax and does not conform to the section; the federal election, deeming rule and tax do not carry into a California return, and the exclusion or inclusion of employee benefits in California follows the state's own conformity treatment (state-level point graded B; the federal statute is grade A).

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

El apartado (e) es la regla anti-fragmentación: todos los empleados tratados como empleados de un solo empleador según el artículo 414(b) (grupo controlado de corporaciones), (c) (negocios bajo control común) o (m) (grupos de servicios afiliados) se tratan como empleados de un solo empleador a efectos de este artículo; tanto la línea del 1 por ciento como el valor total de los beneficios se calculan para el empleador combinado, y mover empleados o líneas entre entidades del grupo no cambia la cuenta combinada. El apartado (f) es la regla territorial: salvo que el reglamento disponga otra cosa, el artículo se aplica solo al empleo dentro de Estados Unidos. Los artículos vecinos reparten otro trabajo: el 4976 grava los fondos de beneficios que pagan en dirección indebida, el 4975 las operaciones prohibidas en planes de jubilación, y el 4977 al empleador que eligió y cuyos beneficios se pasan de la línea. California no tiene un impuesto electivo del empleador al estilo del 4977 ni se ajusta a él; la elección, la ficción y el impuesto federales no pasan a la declaración de California (punto estatal de nivel B; el estatuto federal es de nivel A).
