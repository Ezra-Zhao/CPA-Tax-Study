# Cavallaro 案：家族公司合并里的隐形赠与——技术归属先定、估值举证再算及加州口径

> 个人学习笔记，梳理家族企业合并中对价失衡如何被认定为赠与、以及估值争点上举证责任如何分配。口径以第一巡回上诉法院判决 Cavallaro v. Commissioner, 842 F.3d 16 (1st Cir. 2016) 与税务法院原审 T.C. Memo. 2014-189、发回后 T.C. Memo. 2019-144 及 Journal of Accountancy 报道逐项回核。仓内《Kimbell 案》篇管第 2036 条的真实出售例外、《Strangi 案》篇管保留享有，本篇管合并对价失衡的赠与定性与估值举证，三篇以指针分工。

## 一、案情：机器归父母造，销路归儿子开

### 中文

William 与 Patricia Cavallaro 夫妇 1979 年创办 Knight Tool Co.（下称 Knight），做机加工与合同制造。1982 年，Knight 开发出一套液体胶黏剂点胶系统 CAM/ALOT。1987 年，他们的三个儿子另设 Camelot Systems, Inc.（下称 Camelot）来经营这套系统：分工是 Knight 造机器、Camelot 卖机器。一家人坚称 CAM/ALOT 技术在 1987 年 Camelot 成立时就已转给儿子们的公司；国税局则主张技术一直归 Knight 所有。1994 年两家决定合并，并请了律所与会计师事务所把关——律所坚持估值必须以「技术归 Camelot」为前提来做，会计师据此把合并后公司估在 70,000,000 至 75,000,000 美元之间。前提对不对，日后成了全案的胜负手。

### English

William and Patricia Cavallaro founded Knight Tool Co. (Knight) in 1979 as a machine shop and contract manufacturer. In 1982 Knight developed a liquid-adhesive dispensing system called CAM/ALOT. In 1987 their three sons organized Camelot Systems, Inc. (Camelot) to run that system: Knight would manufacture the machines and Camelot would sell them. The family maintained that the CAM/ALOT technology had been transferred to the sons' company back in 1987 when Camelot was formed; the IRS maintained that Knight owned the technology all along. In 1994 the two companies decided to merge, advised by a law firm and an accounting firm — and the law firm insisted the valuation be prepared on the premise that Camelot, not Knight, owned the technology. On that premise the accountants valued the merged company at between 70,000,000 and 75,000,000 dollars. Whether the premise was right later decided the whole case.

En 1994 ambas empresas decidieron fusionarse, con asesoría de un bufete y una firma contable — y el bufete insistió en que la valoración se preparara bajo la premisa de que la tecnología era de Camelot, no de Knight. Con esa premisa, los contadores valoraron la empresa fusionada entre 70.000.000 y 75.000.000 de dólares. Si la premisa era correcta o no decidió después todo el caso.

## 二、合并与转手：股份比例先写死，七个月后按同一比例分钱

### 中文

1995 年 12 月 31 日，两家完成免税合并，Camelot 为存续公司。合并后股份这样分：William 拿 18 股、Patricia 拿 20 股、三个儿子各拿 54 股。算一遍（本地整数与 Decimal 两路复算一致）：总股数 18 加 20 加 54 乘 3 等于 200 股；父母合计 38 股占 19%，儿子们合计 162 股占 81%。七个月后，Cookson America 以 57,000,000 美元现金收购了合并后公司，正好按同一比例分钱：父母合计收到 57,000,000 乘 19% 等于 10,830,000 美元，每个儿子收到 57,000,000 乘 27% 等于 15,390,000 美元，三人合计 46,170,000 美元，两头相加 10,830,000 加 46,170,000 等于 57,000,000 美元，分毫不差。如果技术真归 Knight，父母在合并中拿到的股份就远低于他们让出的价值，差额等于无偿转给了儿子们——这正是国税局眼中的隐形赠与。父母没有申报赠与税表，理由是这是一笔真实交易、不存在赠与。

### English

On December 31, 1995, the two companies completed a tax-free merger with Camelot as the surviving corporation. The merged company's shares were split as follows: William received 18 shares, Patricia 20 shares, and each of the three sons 54 shares. The arithmetic (recomputed locally by integer and Decimal routes with matching results): total shares are 18 plus 20 plus 54 times 3, which equals 200; the parents together held 38 shares, or 19 percent, and the sons together 162 shares, or 81 percent. Seven months later, Cookson America bought the merged company for 57,000,000 dollars in cash, and the proceeds followed exactly the same ratio: the parents received 57,000,000 times 19 percent, or 10,830,000 dollars in total, and each son received 57,000,000 times 27 percent, or 15,390,000 dollars, for a combined 46,170,000 dollars; 10,830,000 plus 46,170,000 equals 57,000,000 dollars exactly. If the technology really belonged to Knight, the parents received far less stock in the merger than the value they gave up, and the shortfall passed to their sons for nothing — precisely the disguised gift the IRS saw. The parents filed no gift tax returns, taking the position that this was a bona fide transaction with no gift.

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·初译待校)

31 दिसंबर 1995 को दोनों कंपनियों ने कर-मुक्त विलय पूरा किया, जिसमें Camelot उत्तरजीवी निगम रहा। विलयित कंपनी के शेयर इस प्रकार बँटे: William को 18 शेयर, Patricia को 20 शेयर, और तीनों बेटों में से प्रत्येक को 54 शेयर। गणित (स्थानीय रूप से पूर्णांक और Decimal दोनों मार्गों से पुनर्गणना करके मेल की पुष्टि): कुल शेयर 18 जमा 20 जमा 54 गुणा 3 बराबर 200; माता-पिता के पास मिलाकर 38 शेयर अर्थात 19 प्रतिशत, और बेटों के पास मिलाकर 162 शेयर अर्थात 81 प्रतिशत। सात महीने बाद Cookson America ने विलयित कंपनी को 57,000,000 डॉलर नकद में ख़रीदा, और राशि ठीक उसी अनुपात में बँटी: माता-पिता को 57,000,000 गुणा 19 प्रतिशत अर्थात कुल 10,830,000 डॉलर मिले, और प्रत्येक बेटे को 57,000,000 गुणा 27 प्रतिशत अर्थात 15,390,000 डॉलर मिले, तीनों का योग 46,170,000 डॉलर; 10,830,000 जमा 46,170,000 बराबर ठीक 57,000,000 डॉलर। यदि तकनीक सच में Knight की थी, तो माता-पिता को विलय में दिए गए मूल्य से कहीं कम शेयर मिले, और अंतर उनके बेटों को बिना प्रतिफल के चला गया — ठीक वही छिपा हुआ उपहार जो IRS ने देखा। माता-पिता ने उपहार कर रिटर्न दाखिल नहीं किया, यह कहते हुए कि यह एक वास्तविक सौदा था, कोई उपहार नहीं।

## 三、第一争点：技术到底是谁的——事实认定，父母输了

### 中文

国税局的核定一开始走得极猛：它在没有先做评估的情况下直接把 Camelot 的合并前价值定为零，于是父母每人被认定赠与 23,085,000 美元（两人合计 46,170,000 美元，正好等于儿子们后来分到的出售款），每人税额被算到 12,696,750 美元，还附了未申报与欺诈的附加税。到了税务法院，国税局自己的专家 Bello 把合并后公司估为约 64,500,000 美元、其中 Camelot 值 22,600,000 美元（由此 Knight 一侧隐含 64,500,000 减 22,600,000 等于 41,900,000 美元）。税务法院认定技术归 Knight：理由包括 CAM/ALOT 商标登记在 Knight 名下、四份专利申请由 William 提交并写明受让人是 Knight、两家公司财务长期高度混同。税务法院据 Bello 的数认定父母赠与分别为 14,538,300 美元与 15,131,700 美元，合计 29,670,000 美元，税额分别为 7,652,980 美元与 8,009,020 美元、合计 15,662,000 美元，同时否掉了各项罚则与附加税。第一巡回在这一点上维持原判：记录充分支持技术归 Knight 的认定，父母没有提出足以推翻这一事实认定的论据。

### English

The IRS opened aggressively: without first obtaining an appraisal, it set Camelot's pre-merger value at zero, so each parent was determined to have made a gift of 23,085,000 dollars (46,170,000 dollars combined, exactly the sons' later sale proceeds), with tax of 12,696,750 dollars each, plus additions for failure to file and fraud. By the Tax Court trial, the Commissioner's own expert, Bello, valued the combined entity at about 64,500,000 dollars, with Camelot worth 22,600,000 dollars (implying 64,500,000 minus 22,600,000, or 41,900,000 dollars, on the Knight side). The Tax Court held that Knight owned the technology, pointing to the CAM/ALOT trademark registered to Knight, four patent applications filed by William naming Knight as assignee, and the two companies' heavily overlapping finances. Working from Bello's numbers, it found gifts of 14,538,300 dollars by William and 15,131,700 dollars by Patricia, totaling 29,670,000 dollars, with tax of 7,652,980 and 8,009,020 dollars respectively, 15,662,000 dollars combined, while rejecting the penalties and additions. The First Circuit affirmed on this issue: the record amply supported the finding that Knight owned all the CAM/ALOT technology, and the parents advanced no argument warranting reversal of that factual finding.

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

El IRS abrió con dureza: sin obtener primero una tasación, fijó el valor previo a la fusión de Camelot en cero, de modo que a cada padre se le atribuyó una donación de 23.085.000 dólares (46.170.000 entre los dos, exactamente lo que los hijos recibieron después en la venta), con un impuesto de 12.696.750 dólares por cabeza, más recargos por no declarar y por fraude. Con las cifras de Bello, fijó donaciones de 14.538.300 dólares de William y 15.131.700 de Patricia, 29.670.000 en total, con impuestos de 7.652.980 y 8.009.020 dólares, 15.662.000 entre ambos, y rechazó las multas y recargos. ## 四、第二争点：估值的举证责任——父母赢了，六百多万就此减掉

### 中文

父母在税务法院只攻击国税局专家的估值有致命缺陷，却没有提交自己的估值证据；税务法院把估值争点的举证责任压在父母身上，父母没尽到，法院便照单接受国税局的数。第一巡回在这一点上撤销并发回：税务法院把举证责任说错了——局长一方的专家估值若被指出具体缺陷，法院必须认真审查纳税人对该估值的反驳，不能因为纳税人没另交一份估值就直接放行有缺陷的核定。发回后（T.C. Memo. 2019-144），税务法院重审估值，父母的多数攻击仍被驳回，只有一处成立：国税局在利润正常化时把两家公司都放进行业分销商第 90 百分位，计算方法统计上不可靠；按重算的第 88.3 百分位，Camelot 的价值随之抬升，应税赠与总额从 29,670,000 美元减去 6,879,640 美元，降到 22,790,360 美元。百分位上不到两点的差，值六百多万美元——估值案里，方法的小裂缝就是钱。加州口径：加州没有州赠与税、也没有州遗产税（1982 年废除继承与赠与税一线），这类家族转让的战场全在联邦；加州一侧只剩所得税与主体登记层面的衔接（证据等级 B；判决事实与数字为 A 级）。

### English

At the Tax Court the parents attacked the Commissioner's expert valuation as fatally flawed but offered no valuation evidence of their own; the Tax Court placed the burden of proof on valuation on the parents, found it unmet, and accepted the IRS figures as they stood. The First Circuit reversed and remanded on this point: the Tax Court had misstated the burden — once specific flaws in the Commissioner's expert valuation are put in issue, the court must genuinely consider the taxpayers' rebuttal, and may not wave through a defective determination merely because the taxpayers filed no competing appraisal. On remand (T.C. Memo. 2019-144) the Tax Court rejected most of the parents' attacks but sustained one: in normalizing profits, the IRS's margin calculation had placed both companies at the 90th percentile of industry distributors using a statistically unreliable method; at the recomputed 88.3rd percentile, Camelot's value rose, and total taxable gifts fell from 29,670,000 dollars by 6,879,640 dollars to 22,790,360 dollars. A gap of under two percentile points was worth more than six million dollars — in valuation cases, a small crack in the method is money. California: the state has no gift tax and no estate tax (its inheritance and gift taxes were repealed in 1982), so the battlefield for family transfers of this kind is entirely federal; the California side is left with income tax and entity housekeeping (evidence grade B for this paragraph; the case facts and figures are grade A).

### Español (译稿·初译待校)

En el Tribunal Fiscal los padres atacaron la valoración del perito del Comisionado como fatalmente defectuosa, pero no presentaron valoración propia; el tribunal cargó sobre los padres la prueba de la valoración, la dio por incumplida y aceptó sin más las cifras del IRS. El Primer Circuito revocó y devolvió en este punto: el tribunal había enunciado mal la carga de la prueba — señalados defectos concretos en la valoración del perito oficial, el tribunal debe examinar de verdad la réplica de los contribuyentes, y no puede aprobar una determinación defectuosa solo porque los contribuyentes no aportaron otra tasación. En la devolución (T.C. Memo. 2019-144), el Tribunal Fiscal rechazó casi todos los ataques de los padres y aceptó uno solo: al normalizar beneficios, el cálculo de margen del IRS había colocado a ambas empresas en el percentil 90 de los distribuidores del sector con un método estadísticamente no fiable; con el percentil recalculado de 88,3, el valor de Camelot subió, y las donaciones gravables totales bajaron de 29.670.000 dólares en 6.879.640, hasta 22.790.360 dólares. Menos de dos puntos de percentil valieron más de seis millones de dólares: en los casos de valoración, una grieta pequeña en el método es dinero. California: el estado no tiene impuesto a las donaciones ni a las herencias (sus impuestos de sucesiones y donaciones fueron derogados en 1982), así que el campo de batalla de este tipo de transferencias familiares es enteramente federal; del lado californiano solo queda el impuesto sobre la renta y la gestión registral (grado de evidencia B en este párrafo; los hechos y cifras del caso son grado A).
