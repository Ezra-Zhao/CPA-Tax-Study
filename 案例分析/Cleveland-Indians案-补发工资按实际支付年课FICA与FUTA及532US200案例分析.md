# Cleveland Indians 案：补发工资按实际支付的那一年课 FICA 与 FUTA

> 案名：United States v. Cleveland Indians Baseball Company, 532 U.S. 200 (2001)｜美国联邦最高法院｜2001 年 4 月 17 日判决，一致意见，Ginsburg 大法官执笔，案号 No. 00-203
> 本篇是《案例分析》栏目的一篇：十年前该付没付的工资，今天补发，工资税按哪一年的税率和工资基数算？球团说按欠薪那年，最高法院说按掏钱那年。一句话的差别，决定了二百多万美元补发工资要不要再过一遍 FICA 与 FUTA 的闸口。

选题说明：INDOPCO 案经 ls＋grep 双查已有本体专篇（《INDOPCO案-友好收购费用资本化案例分析.md》），按预案改选经典税务案例；「Cleveland Indians」经文件名与正文双查全仓零命中、无本体专篇，本篇补之，与本仓雇主工资税篇（计算口径）、第 6656 条缴存罚篇（缴存时点）以指针分工。

## 一、案件事实

### 中文

1980 年代，大联盟球团老板们在自由球员市场上串通压价（即所谓 collusion 事件），球员工会提出申诉后，双方达成和解：各球团合计支付 2.8 亿美元薪资损害赔偿，给有资格索赔的球员。落到本案球团（Cleveland Indians Baseball Company）头上的份额是：欠 8 名球员 1986 年的工资 610,000 美元，欠 14 名球员 1987 年的工资 1,457,848 美元，两年合计 2,067,848 美元（本地复算一致）。球团迟至 1994 年才把这笔钱付清，而且 1994 年时，这些受款球员已经没有一个是球团的雇员了。球团先按 1994 年的税率与工资基数缴纳了雇主份额的 FICA 与 FUTA 税，随后申请退税，主张这笔钱应当按 1986 与 1987 年——也就是工资本应支付的年份——的口径课税。

### English

In the 1980s, Major League Baseball club owners colluded to suppress the free-agent market; after the players' association grieved, the clubs settled for $280 million in salary damages to players with valid claims. The Cleveland Indians Baseball Company's share was $610,000 owed to 8 players for 1986 and $1,457,848 owed to 14 players for 1987 — $2,067,848 in total (recomputed and consistent). The company did not pay until 1994, by which time none of the recipients was still its employee. It paid its employer share of FICA and FUTA taxes using 1994 rates and wage bases, then sued for a refund, arguing the payments should be taxed by reference to 1986 and 1987, the years the wages should have been paid.

## 二、为什么年份就是钱：三组税率与基数

### 中文

FICA 与 FUTA 的税负由两件事决定：税率与工资基数（wage base），两者都按年定。判决书里摆出的数字是：社保税（雇主与雇员各一份）1986 年为 5.7%、基数 42,000 美元；1987 年为 5.7%、基数 43,800 美元；到 1994 年涨到 6.2%、基数 60,600 美元。FUTA 税 1986 与 1987 年为 6.0%、只就每名雇员前 7,000 美元工资课征；1994 年为 6.2%、基数仍是 7,000 美元。医疗保险税（各 1.45%）在 1986 与 1987 年与社保共用同一工资基数，1994 年起则取消了工资上限。关键事实在球员这一侧：他们 1986 与 1987 年的正常工资早就超过了当年的工资基数——也就是说，若把 1994 年的补发回溯塞进 1986 与 1987 年，那两年每人的基数早已被正常工资填满，补发部分新增的 FICA 与 FUTA 税负约等于零；而按 1994 年算，这些人当年在本球团没有其他工资，基数从零开始填，税就实打实地产生了。年份之争，本质上是两百多万美元要不要再过一遍闸口之争。

### English

FICA and FUTA liability turns on two annual dials: the rate and the wage base. The opinion sets out the numbers: the Social Security tax (on employer and employee alike) was 5.7 percent on wages up to $42,000 in 1986 and 5.7 percent up to $43,800 in 1987, rising to 6.2 percent up to $60,600 in 1994. The FUTA tax was 6.0 percent on the first $7,000 of each employee's wages in 1986 and 1987, and 6.2 percent on the same $7,000 base in 1994. The Medicare tax (1.45 percent each) shared the Social Security wage cap in 1986 and 1987 but had no cap at all from 1994 onward. The decisive fact sits on the players' side: their regular 1986 and 1987 salaries had already exceeded those years' wage bases, so allocating the 1994 payments back to 1986 and 1987 would have generated roughly zero additional FICA or FUTA tax — the bases were already full. Taxed in 1994, when none of them had other wages from the club, the bases started empty and the tax was real. The fight over the year was a fight over whether $2 million-plus would pass through the gate a second time.

El impuesto de Medicare (1,45 por ciento cada parte) compartía el tope de la seguridad social en 1986 y 1987, pero desde 1994 ya no tuvo tope. ## 三、争点与下级程序

### 中文

争点只有一句：补发工资的 FICA 与 FUTA，应当按实际支付的年份（1994），还是按本应支付的年份（1986 与 1987）课征？球团的靠山是第六巡回自己的既有判例——该巡回此前认定，和解补发工资不应归到雇主最终付款的期间，而应归到工资未按时支付的期间。地区法院受本巡回判例约束，判球团胜诉、命政府退税；第六巡回维持原判（215 F.3d 1325）。IRS 一方的立场几十年没变过：补发裁决在税务上一律归到实际支付的那一年。最高法院调卷审查，要解决的正是巡回判例与税务局长期口径之间的这道裂缝。

### English

The question presented was one sentence long: are back wages taxed under FICA and FUTA by reference to the year actually paid (1994) or the years they should have been paid (1986 and 1987)? The company leaned on the Sixth Circuit's own precedent, which had held that settlement back wages should be allocated to the periods when the wages went unpaid rather than to the period of final payment. Bound by that precedent, the District Court ruled for the company and ordered a refund; the Sixth Circuit affirmed (215 F.3d 1325). The IRS position had not moved in decades: for tax purposes, backpay awards are attributed to the year actually paid. The Supreme Court took the case to resolve the split between the circuit's rule and the Service's longstanding construction.

### Español (初译待校)

La cuestión era una sola frase: ¿los salarios atrasados se gravan bajo FICA y FUTA con referencia al año efectivamente pagado (1994) o a los años en que debieron pagarse (1986 y 1987)? La empresa se apoyó en el precedente del propio Sexto Circuito, que había sostenido que los salarios atrasados de un acuerdo debían asignarse a los períodos en que no se pagaron y no al período del pago final. Vinculado por ese precedente, el tribunal de distrito falló a favor de la empresa y ordenó la devolución; el Sexto Circuito confirmó (215 F.3d 1325). La posición del IRS no se había movido en décadas: a efectos fiscales, los laudos de salarios atrasados se atribuyen al año efectivamente pagado. La Corte Suprema aceptó el caso para resolver la fractura entre la regla del circuito y la interpretación histórica del fisco. (初译待校)

쟁점은 한 문장입니다. 체불 급여의 FICA와 FUTA를 실제 지급한 연도(1994년)를 기준으로 할 것인가, 지급됐어야 할 연도(1986년과 1987년)를 기준으로 할 것인가. 구단은 제6순회 자체의 기존 판례에 기대었습니다. 그 판례는 합의 체불 급여를 최종 지급 기간이 아니라 급여가 제때 지급되지 않은 기간에 배분해야 한다고 했습니다. 그 판례에 기속된 지방법원은 구단 승소와 환급을 명했고, 제6순회도 이를 유지했습니다(215 F.3d 1325). ### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Vấn đề chỉ gồm một câu: tiền lương truy lĩnh chịu thuế FICA và FUTA theo năm thực trả (1994) hay theo những năm lẽ ra phải trả (1986 và 1987)? ## 四、判决：按实际支付的那一年算

### 中文

2001 年 4 月 17 日，最高法院一致判决撤销第六巡回，Ginsburg 大法官执笔。理由分四层。第一层是文本与结构：第 3101、3111 条（FICA）与第 3301 条（FUTA）的税率表述都系在「支付的工资」上，第 3121 条 (a) 款第 (1) 项的工资基数也是按日历年逐年封顶——法条的整个计税机器都是按支付年组装的。第二层是条例与长期口径：Reg. 第 31.3121(a)-2 条一带长期规定，工资在实际支付时计入、不论何时挣得；法院原话是，对税务局「合理而长期坚持的、对管辖法条及其自身条例的解释」给予应有的尊重（according due respect），据此认定补发工资按实际支付年课税。第三层是拆掉对方的判例支点：球团援引的 Social Security Board v. Nierotko, 327 U.S. 358 (1946)，处理的是社保福利资格计算中工资往哪个期间记——那是福利侧的归属规则，管的是领多少钱，不是税侧的课税时点，不能搬过来控制本案；Rowan Companies v. United States, 452 U.S. 247 (1981) 讲的「工资」定义在 FICA 与所得税预扣之间要一致，解决的是定义问题，同样不回答时点问题。第四层是行政可行性：按支付年统一处理，雇主不必为一笔补发去重开十年前已经封顶的工资基数逐人重算，征纳两边的账都只有一本。

### English

On April 17, 2001, the Supreme Court unanimously reversed the Sixth Circuit, in an opinion by Justice Ginsburg. The reasoning has four layers. First, text and structure: the rate provisions of §§3101 and 3111 (FICA) and §3301 (FUTA) are keyed to wages paid, and the §3121(a)(1) wage base is capped calendar year by calendar year — the whole taxing machine is assembled around the year of payment. Second, regulation and longevity: Reg. §31.3121(a)-2 and its companions had long provided that wages are counted when actually paid, regardless of when earned, and the Court, "according due respect to the Service's reasonable, longstanding construction of the governing statutes and its own regulations," held that back wages are taxed by reference to the year in fact paid. Third, the precedential props were removed: Social Security Board v. Nierotko, 327 U.S. 358 (1946), invoked by the company, governs how wages are allocated in computing Social Security benefit eligibility — a benefits-side allocation rule about how much one receives, not a tax-side timing rule — and does not control; Rowan Companies v. United States, 452 U.S. 247 (1981), which requires a consistent definition of "wages" across FICA and income-tax withholding, answers a definitional question, not a timing one. Fourth, administrability: a single payment-year rule spares employers from reopening decade-old, already-maxed wage bases employee by employee for every backpay award — one set of books for both sides.

## 五、算例：同一笔 50,000 美元，两个年份两个税负

Theo quy tắc của Tòa, đánh thuế vào năm 1994: thuế an sinh xã hội phía người sử dụng lao động = 50.000 × 6,2% = 3.100 đô la (dưới mức căn cứ 60.600); thuế Medicare phía người sử dụng lao động = 50.000 × 1,45% = 725 đô la (năm 1994 không có trần); FUTA gộp = 7.000 × 6,2% = 434 đô la, hoặc 7.000 × 0,8% = 56 đô la sau khi trừ đủ khoản khấu trừ thuế thất nghiệp bang 5,4 phần trăm; tổng gộp phía người sử dụng lao động là 3.100 + 725 + 434 = 4.259 đô la, phía người lao động bị khấu trừ theo cùng thuế suất FICA. ## 六、今日落点与加州口径

### 中文

本案留下的操作口径至今没变：补发工资、和解协议里定性为工资的部分、追溯性调薪，FICA 与 FUTA 都按实际支付那一年的税率与工资基数走，与所得税预扣的支付年口径同向；雇主先按旧年口径少缴的，不能靠「这钱本该早付」免责，补缴与第 6413 条调整、退税时效另按各自规则走。福利侧不要混：社保福利计算中工资记到哪个期间，仍按 Nierotko 一线的福利归属规则处理，与课税时点分家——同一笔补发工资，税表上归支付年，福利记录里可能归应付年，两本账并行不悖。加州口径同向：州失业保险与 ETT 等工资税同样以实际支付时点定归属年度与当年费率基数（EDD 口径，证据等级 B；联邦判决本体为 A 级）。

### English

The operating rule the case leaves behind is unchanged today: backpay, the wage-characterized portion of settlements, and retroactive raises all take the FICA and FUTA rates and wage bases of the year actually paid, in step with the payment-year approach of income-tax withholding; an employer that underpaid under an earned-year theory cannot excuse itself by saying the money should have been paid earlier, and corrections run through §6413 adjustments and the refund limitations periods on their own tracks. Keep the benefits side separate: for Social Security benefit computation, wages are still credited under the Nierotko-line benefits allocation rules, not the tax-timing rule — the same backpay can sit in the payment year on the tax return and in the earned years in the benefits record, two ledgers running in parallel. California points the same way: state unemployment insurance and ETT wages are likewise assigned by when they are actually paid, under that year's rates and bases (EDD guidance, grade B; the federal decision itself is grade A).

## 七、易错点

### 中文

- 把 Nierotko 当课税判例：它管福利资格的工资归属，不管 FICA 的课税时点，本案已明确分家。
- 把 Rowan 的「定义一致」读成「时点一致」：Rowan 统一的是什么算工资，不是什么时候算。
- 以为受款人已离职就不算工资：1994 年受款人无一在职，法院照样按工资课税——工资税看支付的性质，不看支付时是否还在职。
- 把补发回溯到旧年填基数：旧年基数封顶是旧年的事，补发的基数从支付年重新起算，这正是本案 4,259 美元对零的由来。
