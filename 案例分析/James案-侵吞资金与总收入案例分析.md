# James 案：侵吞来的钱算不算收入——从 Wilcox 到权利主张标准

这篇讲什么：- 拆解 James v. United States, 366 U.S. 213 (1961)：一位工会官员在 1951 至 1954 年间侵吞超过 738,000 美元、从未申报，最高法院一方面认定侵吞款在侵吞年度就是总收入、明文推翻 Wilcox，另一方面却撤销了他的逃税定罪并指示撤销起诉——实体上欠税，刑事上不罚，两件事必须分开看。
- 核心区分标准只有一句：纳税人取得一笔款项时，若没有与权利人达成经同意承认的偿还义务（consensual recognition of an obligation to repay），这笔款就不像借款那样被当作非收入；侵吞者取得赃款时恰恰没有这种经同意的承认。
- 本案是现行第 61 条口径的基石之一：非法所得同样属于「来自任何来源的全部收入」，来源合不合法，不改变收入的性质；考试里出现 embezzled funds、illegal income，先想本案。
- 票数结构要单独记牢：推翻 Wilcox 拼得六票，但首席大法官 Warren 的主意见只有三人加入，是相对多数意见（plurality），不是整齐的多数意见；刑事处置上撤销定罪，则是另一条线的结论。

## 一、事实

### 中文
Eugene James 是工会官员。1951 年至 1954 年间，他利用职务之便，从雇主工会以及一家保险公司侵吞资金，合计超过 738,000 美元。这笔钱数额巨大，且跨越多个纳税年度，但他没有在任何一年的申报表上把侵吞款申报为收入。政府随后以故意逃税起诉他：涉及 1939 年法典年份的部分，依据是 1939 年法典第 145(b) 条；涉及 1954 年法典年份的部分，依据是 1954 年法典第 7201 条，也就是现行法典中故意逃避纳税的那一条。他被定罪，下级法院判有罪，第七巡回上诉法院维持定罪，案号为 273 F.2d 5。案件随后上诉到联邦最高法院，1960 年 11 月 17 日听审，1961 年 5 月 15 日宣判。

这里先把两个层面分清楚，因为全案的张力就出在这里。第一个层面是民事的、实体的：侵吞来的钱，在税法上到底算不算侵吞者的收入，算哪一年的收入。第二个层面是刑事的：就算实体上是收入、就算他没有申报，他在行为当时的法律环境下，是否还具备逃税罪所要求的故意（willfulness）。James 本人对侵吞事实本身并无太多可争，他争的正是这两层：先说这笔钱按 Wilcox 不是收入，再说退一万步，在 Wilcox 仍有效的年代行事的人，不可能被认定为故意违反一项明确的纳税义务。

还有一个背景数字值得留意。738,000 美元在 1950 年代初是一笔巨款，远超普通工薪收入。侵吞持续四年、横跨两部法典的生效期间，这也是为什么起诉同时援引 1939 年法典与 1954 年法典两套条文，争点也同时涉及两部法典的总收入定义。

### English
Eugene James was a union official. Between 1951 and 1954, abusing his position, he embezzled funds from his employer union and from an insurance company, in a total amount exceeding $738,000. The sum was large and spread over several taxable years, yet he reported none of it as income on any return. The Government prosecuted him for willful tax evasion: for the years under the 1939 Code, under Section 145(b) of that Code, and for the years under the 1954 Code, under Section 7201 of the 1954 Code, the willful-evasion provision that survives in the present Code. He was convicted, and the United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit affirmed the conviction, 273 F.2d 5. The case then reached the Supreme Court, was argued on November 17, 1960, and was decided on May 15, 1961.

Two levels must be kept apart from the start, because the whole tension of the case lies between them. The first is civil and substantive: whether embezzled money is income to the embezzler for tax purposes, and if so, in which year. The second is criminal: even if the money was income and even if he failed to report it, did he act with the willfulness that the evasion offense requires, given the state of the law at the time he acted? James had little to contest about the embezzlement itself. His fight was on these two levels: first, that under Wilcox the money was not income at all; and second, in the alternative, that a person who acted while Wilcox was still good law could not be found to have willfully violated a known legal duty.

One background figure is worth noting. $738,000 in the early 1950s was an enormous sum, far beyond ordinary wage income. The embezzlement ran for four years and straddled the transition between two revenue codes, which is why the indictment invoked provisions of both the 1939 Code and the 1954 Code, and why the issue implicated the gross-income definitions of both.

## 二、争点

### 中文
本案的争点可以拆成三问，层层递进。第一问，也是实体核心：侵吞取得的款项，是否属于总收入。涉及的条文有两处，措辞几乎同义：1939 年法典第 22(a) 条把总收入定义为包括来自任何来源的收益，1954 年法典第 61(a) 条则写明总收入指来自任何来源的全部收入（all income from whatever source derived）。两条都没有把来源限定为合法来源，问题恰恰是：要不要因为钱来路不正、侵吞者对钱没有合法权利，就把它读出总收入的范围之外。

第二问是先例冲突怎么处理。1946 年的 Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 判过：侵吞款不是侵吞者的收入，理由是侵吞者对款项没有合法的权利主张（claim of right），并且负有无条件的归还义务，情形近似借款。可是 1952 年的 Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 又判勒索所得应税，推理与 Wilcox 已经拉开距离。James 的行为年份里，Wilcox 名义上仍是有效先例，Rutkin 又在侵蚀它。法院必须正面回答：Wilcox 还站不站得住，要留，就得解释 Rutkin；要推翻，就得说清推翻的界限在哪里。

第三问是刑事故意。逃税罪要求故意，即明知有纳税义务而故意违反。James 的行为发生在 1951 至 1954 年，那时 Wilcox 尚未被推翻，一个纳税人完全可能依据当时有效的最高法院先例，真诚地认为侵吞款不是应税收入。即便法院今天改判实体问题，昨天的故意能不能成立，是完全独立的一问。争点的结构因此是：实体结论往前走，刑事责任要不要跟着往前走。

### English
The issue breaks into three questions, each nested in the next. The first, and the substantive core, is whether money acquired by embezzlement is gross income. Two statutory texts are involved, and they are materially identical: Section 22(a) of the 1939 Code defined gross income to include gains from any source whatever, and Section 61(a) of the 1954 Code defines gross income as all income from whatever source derived. Neither text limits income to lawful sources. The precise question is whether money should nevertheless be read out of gross income because it was unlawfully taken and because the embezzler has no lawful right to it.

The second question is what to do with conflicting precedent. Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946), had held that embezzled money is not income to the embezzler, reasoning that the embezzler has no bona fide claim of right to the money and is under an unqualified obligation to repay it, much like a borrower. But Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952), later held extortion proceeds taxable, on reasoning that had already pulled away from Wilcox. During the years of James's conduct, Wilcox was nominally still good law, while Rutkin was undermining it. The Court had to answer squarely whether Wilcox could still stand: if it was kept, Rutkin had to be explained; if it was overruled, the grounds and limits of overruling had to be stated.

The third question is criminal willfulness. Tax evasion requires willfulness — a voluntary, intentional violation of a known legal duty. James acted between 1951 and 1954, when Wilcox had not yet been overruled, and a taxpayer could in good faith have relied on a then-valid Supreme Court precedent for the view that embezzled funds were not taxable income. Even if the Court changed the substantive rule today, whether yesterday's willfulness existed is an entirely separate question. The structure of the case is therefore this: the substantive conclusion moves forward, and the Court must decide separately whether criminal liability follows it forward.

### Español (初译待校)
La cuestión se divide en tres preguntas. La primera, el núcleo sustantivo: si el dinero obtenido por malversación es ingreso bruto. Intervienen dos textos equivalentes: la sección 22(a) del Código de 1939, que incluía las ganancias de cualquier fuente, y la sección 61(a) del Código de 1954, que define el ingreso bruto como todos los ingresos, cualquiera que sea su fuente (all income from whatever source derived). Ninguno limita la fuente a fuentes lícitas; la pregunta es si el dinero debe excluirse por haber sido tomado ilícitamente y porque el malversador no tiene derecho legítimo sobre él. La segunda pregunta es el conflicto de precedentes: Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946), había dicho que el dinero malversado no es ingreso, porque el malversador carece de una pretensión de derecho legítima (claim of right) y está bajo una obligación incondicional de devolverlo, como un prestatario. Pero Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952), declaró gravable el producto de la extorsión, con un razonamiento ya alejado de Wilcox. La tercera pregunta es la intencionalidad penal: la evasión exige willfulness, la violación voluntaria de un deber conocido, y James actuó entre 1951 y 1954, cuando Wilcox aún no había sido revocado.

### 한국어 (初译待校)
쟁점은 세 단계로 나뉜다. 첫째, 실체의 핵심으로, 횡령으로 취득한 돈이 총소득에 해당하는가이다. 관련 조문은 두 개이며 실질적으로 같은 뜻이다. 1939년 법전 제22(a)조는 총소득에 그 출처를 불문한 이익을 포함했고, 1954년 법전 제61(a)조는 총소득을 '그 출처를 불문한 모든 소득'(all income from whatever source derived)으로 정의한다. 어느 조문도 출처를 합법적인 출처로 한정하지 않는다. 문제는 돈이 불법적으로 취득되었고 횡령자에게 합법적 권리가 없다는 이유로 이를 총소득에서 빼서 읽어야 하는가이다. 둘째, 선례 충돌을 어떻게 처리할 것인가이다. Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946)는 횡령자가 그 돈에 대해 정당한 권리주장(claim of right)이 없고 무조건적인 반환의무를 지므로 차입과 비슷하여 횡령금은 소득이 아니라고 했다. 그러나 Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952)는 공갈로 얻은 돈을 과세소득으로 보아 이미 Wilcox와 거리를 두었다. 셋째, 형사상 고의이다. 탈세는 알려진 법적 의무를 자발적·의도적으로 위반하는 고의(willfulness)를 요구하는데, James는 Wilcox가 아직 뒤집히지 않았던 1951년부터 1954년 사이에 행동했다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Vấn đề được chia thành ba câu hỏi. Thứ nhất, cốt lõi thực chất: tiền thu được bằng biển thủ có phải là tổng thu nhập hay không. Có hai văn bản liên quan với nội dung tương đương: Điều 22(a) của Bộ luật 1939 bao gồm các khoản lợi từ bất kỳ nguồn nào, và Điều 61(a) của Bộ luật 1954 định nghĩa tổng thu nhập là mọi thu nhập từ bất kỳ nguồn nào (all income from whatever source derived). Cả hai đều không giới hạn nguồn ở nguồn hợp pháp; câu hỏi là liệu tiền có bị loại ra chỉ vì bị lấy bất hợp pháp và người biển thủ không có quyền hợp pháp đối với nó. Thứ hai là xung đột tiền lệ: Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946), từng phán rằng tiền biển thủ không phải thu nhập, vì người biển thủ không có yêu sách quyền chính đáng (claim of right) và có nghĩa vụ hoàn trả vô điều kiện, giống như người vay. Nhưng Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952), lại phán tiền tống tiền phải chịu thuế, với lập luận đã tách khỏi Wilcox. Thứ ba là sự cố ý trong hình sự: tội trốn thuế đòi hỏi willfulness, tức cố ý vi phạm một nghĩa vụ pháp lý đã biết, trong khi James hành động từ 1951 đến 1954, khi Wilcox chưa bị lật bỏ.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

## 三、判决

### 中文
判决必须分两半读，混在一起就会把本案读反。

先说实体结论。法院认定：侵吞取得的款项，在侵吞发生的年度，构成侵吞者的总收入。侵吞者不能因为这笔钱来路非法、自己终究负有归还义务，就把它排除在总收入之外。Commissioner v. Wilcox 被明文推翻（overruled），不再有效。这一结论同时覆盖 1939 年法典第 22(a) 条与 1954 年法典第 61(a) 条两个时期，口径统一：总收入的定义不区分合法与非法来源。

再说意见结构，这一点考试和实务都容易写错。主意见由首席大法官 Warren 撰写，Brennan 与 Stewart 两位大法官加入，连同 Warren 本人共三人，因此这是一份相对多数意见（plurality opinion），不是通常意义上五票以上的整齐多数意见。推翻 Wilcox 这件事本身实际拼得了六票：Warren 三人组之外，Harlan 大法官另写协同意见、Frankfurter 大法官加入，主张推翻 Wilcox；Clark 大法官也单独协同，同意推翻 Wilcox。三路加起来，同意推翻 Wilcox 的共六位。另有 Whittaker 大法官的意见，Black 与 Douglas 加入：这一路同意撤销对 James 的起诉，但明确反对推翻 Wilcox。所以准确的表述是：推翻 Wilcox 有六票支持，但阐述实体理由的主意见只有三票，是一份 plurality；写报告或答题时，不要把 Warren 意见写成整齐的多数意见。

最后说刑事处置，它与实体结论方向相反。因 James 行为时 Wilcox 仍是有效的最高法院先例，他完全可能依据该先例认为侵吞款不是应税收入，逃税罪所要求的故意因此无法成立。最高法院据此撤销定罪，并发回下级法院、指示撤销起诉（reversed and remanded with directions to dismiss the indictment）。注意这不是发回重审、换个陪审团再判一次，而是连起诉一并撤销。本案的最终图景因此很醒目：实体上，侵吞款是收入、欠税成立、Wilcox 被推翻；刑事上，因为行为时的法律状态，定罪不成立、起诉撤销。欠税与坐牢，在本案里被干净地切成了两件事。

### English
The judgment must be read in two halves. Read together, the case comes out backwards.

First, the substantive holding. The Court held that money acquired by embezzlement constitutes gross income to the embezzler in the year of the embezzlement. The embezzler cannot exclude the money from gross income merely because it was unlawfully taken or because he is ultimately obliged to repay it. Commissioner v. Wilcox was expressly overruled and is no longer good law. The holding covers both statutory periods, Section 22(a) of the 1939 Code and Section 61(a) of the 1954 Code, under a single approach: the definition of gross income does not distinguish between lawful and unlawful sources.

Second, the structure of the opinions, a point that is easy to get wrong in exams and in practice. The lead opinion was written by Chief Justice Warren, joined by Justices Brennan and Stewart — three justices in total, counting Warren himself. It is therefore a plurality opinion, not a tidy five-vote majority opinion. The overruling of Wilcox itself in fact commanded six votes: beyond the Warren group of three, Justice Harlan wrote separately concurring in the overruling, joined by Justice Frankfurter, and Justice Clark also concurred separately in overruling Wilcox. Added together, six justices agreed to overrule Wilcox. Justice Whittaker, joined by Justices Black and Douglas, took a different path: that group agreed that the indictment against James should be dismissed, but expressly declined to overrule Wilcox. The accurate statement is therefore this: overruling Wilcox had six votes, but the lead opinion explaining the substantive reasoning had only three, and is a plurality. Do not describe the Warren opinion as a straightforward majority opinion.

Finally, the criminal disposition, which points in the opposite direction from the substantive holding. Because Wilcox was still valid Supreme Court precedent when James acted, he could well have believed, on the strength of that precedent, that embezzled funds were not taxable income, and the willfulness required for tax evasion therefore could not be established. The Supreme Court accordingly reversed the conviction and remanded the case with directions to dismiss the indictment. This was not a remand for a new trial before a different jury; the indictment itself was to be dismissed. The final picture of the case is striking: substantively, embezzled money is income, the tax is owed, and Wilcox is overruled; criminally, because of the state of the law at the time of the conduct, the conviction fails and the indictment is dismissed. Owing the tax and going to prison were, in this case, cleanly separated into two different things.

## 四、推理：从 Wilcox 到 James

### 中文
推理的起点是宪法与条文。第十六修正案授权国会对来自任何来源的收入征税，第 22(a) 条与第 61(a) 条的措辞与之呼应，都用了最宽的表达，且都没有在总收入定义里加入合法二字。国会若想只对合法来源征税，完全可以在条文中写明；它没有写。法院因此拒绝把合法性读成总收入的构成要件：一个人实际取得了经济上的好处、对这笔钱形成了现实的支配，税法先问的是有没有所得，而不是这笔所得在民法或刑法上干不干净。

真正的技术活儿，是处理 Wilcox 留下的那套区分。Wilcox 的逻辑是：借款不是收入，因为借款人负有归还义务，并且是经借贷双方同意、被承认的归还义务；Wilcox 把侵吞者也放进了这一格，说侵吞者对赃款没有合法的权利主张，又负有无条件的归还义务，所以不构成收入。James 的相对多数意见接受了借款这一格的前提，却把侵吞者从这一格里移了出来，区分点提炼得很锋利：借款人取得款项的当下，就存在一项经同意承认的偿还义务（consensual recognition of an obligation to repay）——出借人同意把钱交给他、双方共同承认这笔钱将来要还。侵吞则完全不同：没有任何权利人同意把钱交给侵吞者，更不存在双方共同承认的偿还安排；侵吞者负有的归还义务，是法律在他违背权利人意志取得款项之后，单方面强加给他的，而不是取得款项时经同意承认的义务。

于是标准成形了，后来被称为权利主张（claim of right）思路在非法所得领域的表达：纳税人在没有限制条件地支配一笔款项、且取得时不存在经同意承认的偿还义务时，这笔款项在取得年度构成收入，哪怕取得方式非法、哪怕日后可能被追索、被判归还。归还的风险不否定当下的所得，就像合法收入日后因纠纷被退回，也不倒推当初没有取得收入一样。Wilcox 错就错在把法律事后强加的归还义务，与借款中事前经同意承认的归还义务混为一谈；一旦这层混淆被拆开，Wilcox 就没有继续存在的理由，于是被推翻。

推理里还有半步要交代清楚：为什么这套新标准不回头惩罚 James 本人。法律标准的更替解决的是从今往后怎么算收入的问题，而刑事故意问的是行为当时行为人对义务的认识。行为时 Wilcox 尚在，James 对义务的认识有最高法院先例可依，故意不成立。实体标准向前适用、刑事责任不溯及地跟随，这正是第三节里两半判决在推理上的接合点。

### English
The reasoning starts with the Constitution and the statutory text. The Sixteenth Amendment empowers Congress to tax incomes from whatever source derived, and both Section 22(a) and Section 61(a) use the broadest corresponding language; neither writes the word lawful into the definition of gross income. If Congress had wanted to tax only lawful sources, it could have said so in the statute; it did not. The Court therefore refused to read lawfulness into gross income as an element: when a person has actually received an economic benefit and exercises real dominion over the money, tax law asks first whether there has been an accession to wealth, not whether that accession was clean under property or criminal law.

The real technical work was dismantling the distinction Wilcox had built. Wilcox reasoned that loan proceeds are not income because the borrower is under an obligation to repay — an obligation the lender and borrower have consensually recognized. Wilcox then placed the embezzler in the same box: the embezzler, it said, has no bona fide claim of right to the money and is under an unqualified duty to repay, so there is no income. The James plurality accepted the loan box itself but moved the embezzler out of it, and the line it drew is sharp. When a borrower receives money, there exists at the very moment of receipt a consensual recognition of an obligation to repay: the lender agrees to hand the money over, and both sides jointly acknowledge that it is to be repaid. Embezzlement is the opposite. No rightful owner agreed to hand money to the embezzler, and there is no mutually acknowledged repayment arrangement at all. The embezzler's duty to repay is imposed on him by law, unilaterally, after he took the money against the owner's will; it is not an obligation consensually recognized at the moment of receipt.

The standard that emerges — the claim of right way of thinking, applied to unlawful gains — is this: where a taxpayer receives money under no restriction as to its disposition, and there is no consensual recognition of an obligation to repay at the time of receipt, the money is income in the year received, even if it was unlawfully taken and even if it may later be recovered from him or ordered repaid. The risk of having to give it back does not negate the present accession, just as lawfully received income later returned after a dispute does not retroactively show that no income was ever received. Wilcox's error was to conflate a repayment duty imposed by law after the fact with a repayment obligation consensually recognized in advance, as in a loan. Once that conflation is exposed, Wilcox has no remaining support, and it was overruled.

One further half-step completes the reasoning: why the new standard does not reach back to punish James himself. Replacing the legal standard answers how income is to be computed from now on; criminal willfulness asks what the actor understood his duty to be at the time he acted. When James acted, Wilcox was still on the books, and his understanding of his duty had Supreme Court precedent behind it, so willfulness fails. The substantive standard moves forward while criminal liability does not follow it backward — and that is exactly how the two halves of the judgment fit together.

### Español (初译待校)
El razonamiento parte de la Constitución y del texto legal. La Decimosexta Enmienda faculta al Congreso para gravar ingresos de cualquier fuente, y tanto la sección 22(a) como la 61(a) usan la fórmula más amplia, sin incluir la palabra lícito en la definición de ingreso bruto. Si el Congreso hubiera querido gravar solo las fuentes lícitas, lo habría dicho. Por eso el Tribunal se negó a convertir la licitud en un elemento del ingreso: cuando una persona recibe efectivamente un beneficio económico y ejerce dominio real sobre el dinero, el derecho fiscal pregunta primero si hubo un incremento patrimonial. El trabajo técnico consistió en desmontar la distinción de Wilcox. Wilcox había razonado que el préstamo no es ingreso porque el prestatario está obligado a devolverlo, con una obligación reconocida de común acuerdo; y colocó al malversador en la misma casilla, por carecer de una pretensión de derecho legítima y estar bajo un deber incondicional de devolución. La pluralidad de James aceptó la casilla del préstamo, pero sacó de ella al malversador: al recibir un préstamo existe, en el mismo momento de la recepción, un reconocimiento consensuado de la obligación de devolver (consensual recognition of an obligation to repay); en la malversación, en cambio, ningún titular consintió entregar el dinero ni hubo reconocimiento mutuo de devolución, y el deber de devolver lo impone la ley unilateralmente, después de la apropiación contra la voluntad del dueño. La regla resultante, en clave de claim of right, es que quien recibe dinero sin restricciones sobre su uso y sin ese reconocimiento consensuado obtiene ingreso en el año de la recepción, aunque la obtención sea ilícita y aunque luego deba devolverlo. Y esa regla nueva no castiga retroactivamente a James: la intencionalidad penal se juzga según el derecho vigente cuando actuó, y entonces Wilcox aún regía.

## 五、与 Rutkin、Glenshaw Glass 的谱系和现行第 61 条口径

### 中文
把本案放回谱系里，位置就清楚了。第一环是 Wilcox（1946）：它在总收入与非法所得之间划了一条例外线，侵吞款被挡在收入之外。第二环是 Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952)：勒索者以威胁手段取得的钱款被判应税。Rutkin 的事实与 Wilcox 很难在原则上区分——勒索者同样对钱没有合法权利、同样可能被追索——法院实际上已经离开了 Wilcox 的逻辑，只是当时还没有明说推翻。第三环是 Commissioner v. Glenshaw Glass Co., 348 U.S. 426 (1955)：最高法院给出总收入的经典工作定义——对财富的明确实现的增加、完全支配、纳税人可以清楚认识到的所得（clearly realized gains, complete dominion, undeniably accessions to wealth），并强调第 61 条的措辞表明国会要在宪法允许的范围内完整行使征税权。Glenshaw Glass 处理的是惩罚性赔偿，详见本仓同板块文章，但它的定义是一般性的。第四环才是 James（1961）：把 Glenshaw Glass 的宽定义与 Rutkin 的结果合流，正面处理 Wilcox，宣布侵吞款应税、Wilcox 推翻。四环连起来，是一条从例外到通例的线：非法性不是排除收入的理由，控制与受益才是。

现行口径因此可以一句话说清：现行第 61(a) 条下，非法所得——侵吞、勒索、盗窃、贿赂、非法经营利润等——与合法所得一样计入总收入，在取得并形成无限制支配的年度入账；财政条例第 1.61-14(a) 条也把杂项收入的口径写得很宽，实务申报中，非法活动所得有其既定的申报位置。反过来，真正的借款不入收入，靠的正是 James 划出的那条线：取得时存在经同意承认的偿还义务。同样，代他人保管、明确受限制不得动用的资金，与侵吞者那种把钱当自己钱花的支配状态不同，判断时仍回到支配是否完整、取得时有无经同意的偿还安排这两个问题。

最后留一个尾巴，只点方向、不下死结论。James 本人即使刑事上脱身，民事上仍站在双重义务之下：他要把侵吞款偿还给工会与保险公司，同时要就这笔款缴纳所得税，这是判决后果的一部分，不能漏写。那么，如果他在以后年度实际归还了这笔钱，税上怎么处理？现行法下可能的方向包括：在归还年度主张扣除，或者在符合条件时援引第 1341 条的权利主张救济（claim of right relief）来缓和同一笔钱先全额入税、后全额归还造成的税率与年度错位。但具体适用条件、金额门槛与计算方法必须按现行法条与最新指引逐项核对，本篇只提示方向，不写死结论。考试若问到归还年度处理，先答扣除与第 1341 条两个方向，再注明以现行法核对，是稳妥的答法。

### English
Placed back in its line of cases, the position of James becomes clear. The first link is Wilcox (1946), which drew an exception line between gross income and unlawful gains and kept embezzled money outside income. The second link is Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952), where money extracted by threats was held taxable. Rutkin is hard to distinguish from Wilcox in principle — the extortionist likewise had no lawful right to the money and was likewise exposed to recovery — and the Court had in substance already left Wilcox's logic behind without yet saying so. The third link is Commissioner v. Glenshaw Glass Co., 348 U.S. 426 (1955), which supplied the classic working definition of gross income: instances of undeniable accessions to wealth, clearly realized, over which the taxpayer has complete dominion, together with the point that the language of Section 61 shows Congress intending to exercise its taxing power to the full extent the Constitution allows. Glenshaw Glass itself concerned punitive damages, treated in this repository's companion article, but its definition is general. The fourth link is James (1961), which brought the broad Glenshaw Glass definition and the Rutkin result together, confronted Wilcox directly, held embezzled funds taxable, and overruled Wilcox. The four links form a single line from exception to general rule: illegality is not a ground for excluding income; dominion and benefit are what count.

The current position under Section 61(a) can therefore be stated in one sentence: unlawful gains — embezzlement, extortion, theft, bribes, profits of illegal enterprises, and the like — are included in gross income on the same footing as lawful gains, in the year they are received under unrestricted dominion, and Treas. Reg. Section 1.61-14(a) states the miscellaneous-income rule broadly, with established return positions for income from illegal activities in practice. Conversely, a genuine loan stays out of income precisely because of the line James drew: at receipt there is a consensual recognition of an obligation to repay. Funds held for another under real restrictions on use likewise differ from the embezzler's treatment of the money as his own, and the analysis still returns to the same two questions: is dominion complete, and was there a consensually recognized repayment arrangement at receipt?

One tail remains, flagged as direction only and not as a settled conclusion. Even though James escaped criminally, civilly he stood under a double obligation: he had to repay the embezzled funds to the union and the insurance company, and he owed income tax on the same money. That consequence is part of the case and should not be omitted. If he in fact repaid the money in a later year, how is the repayment treated for tax? Possible directions under current law include claiming a deduction in the year of repayment or, where the conditions are met, invoking the claim of right relief of Section 1341 to soften the mismatch created when the same money is taxed in full in one year and repaid in full in another. The specific conditions, thresholds, and computations must be checked item by item against the current statute and guidance; this article points to the directions only and states no fixed conclusion. On an exam, naming the deduction and Section 1341 as the two directions, with a note to verify under current law, is the safe answer.

본안을 선례의 흐름 안에 놓으면 위치가 분명해진다. 첫 고리는 Wilcox(1946)로, 총소득과 불법소득 사이에 예외선을 그어 횡령금을 소득에서 제외했다. 둘째 고리는 Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952)로, 협박으로 뜯어낸 돈이 과세소득으로 인정되었다. 공갈범 역시 그 돈에 합법적 권리가 없고 반환 청구에 노출되어 있다는 점에서 Wilcox와 원칙적으로 구별하기 어렵고, 법원은 실질적으로 이미 Wilcox의 논리를 떠난 상태였다. 셋째 고리는 Commissioner v. Glenshaw Glass Co., 348 U.S. 426 (1955)로, 총소득의 고전적 정의를 제시했다. 부인할 수 없는 재산의 증가이고, 명확히 실현되었으며, 납세자가 완전히 지배하는 것, 그리고 제61조의 문언은 의회가 헌법이 허용하는 범위에서 과세권을 온전히 행사하려 했음을 보여 준다는 것이다. 넷째 고리가 James(1961)로, Glenshaw Glass의 넓은 정의와 Rutkin의 결과를 합쳐 Wilcox를 정면으로 다루어 횡령금 과세를 선언하고 Wilcox를 폐기했다. 현행 제61(a)조 아래에서는 횡령, 공갈, 절도, 뇌물, 불법 영업의 이익 같은 불법소득도 합법소득과 마찬가지로, 제한 없는 지배 아래 취득한 연도에 총소득에 포함되며, 재무부 규정 제1.61-14(a)조도 기타소득의 기준을 넓게 규정한다. 반대로 진정한 차입금이 소득에서 빠지는 것은 바로 James가 그은 선, 즉 취득 시점에 합의된 반환의무의 인정이 있기 때문이다. 마지막으로 방향만 제시하고 결론을 못 박지 않는 꼬리가 하나 남는다. James는 형사적으로 벗어났어도 민사적으로는 이중 의무 아래 있었다. 횡령금을 노조와 보험회사에 반환해야 했고, 같은 돈에 대해 소득세도 내야 했다. 이후 연도에 실제로 반환했다면 세법상 처리는 어떻게 되는가. 현행법상 가능한 방향으로는 반환 연도에 공제를 주장하는 방법과, 요건을 갖추면 제1341조의 권리주장 구제(claim of right relief)를 원용하는 방법이 있으나, 구체적 요건과 계산은 현행 법령으로 항목별로 확인해야 하며 본 글은 방향만 제시한다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Đặt vụ án trở lại dòng tiền lệ, vị trí của James trở nên rõ ràng. Mắt xích đầu tiên là Wilcox (1946), vụ án vẽ ra một đường ngoại lệ và để tiền biển thủ nằm ngoài thu nhập. Mắt xích thứ hai là Rutkin v. United States, 343 U.S. 130 (1952): tiền chiếm đoạt bằng đe dọa bị phán là phải chịu thuế, trong tình tiết khó phân biệt về nguyên tắc với Wilcox, vì kẻ tống tiền cũng không có quyền hợp pháp đối với số tiền. Mắt xích thứ ba là Commissioner v. Glenshaw Glass Co., 348 U.S. 426 (1955), đưa ra định nghĩa kinh điển về tổng thu nhập: những sự gia tăng tài sản không thể phủ nhận, được thực hiện rõ ràng, và nằm dưới quyền chi phối hoàn toàn của người nộp thuế, đồng thời nhấn mạnh ngôn từ của Điều 61 cho thấy Quốc hội muốn thực thi quyền đánh thuế đến mức tối đa Hiến pháp cho phép. Mắt xích thứ tư là James (1961), kết hợp định nghĩa rộng của Glenshaw Glass với kết quả của Rutkin, đối mặt trực tiếp với Wilcox, tuyên bố tiền biển thủ phải chịu thuế và lật bỏ Wilcox. Theo luật hiện hành, Điều 61(a) đối xử với các khoản lợi bất hợp pháp — biển thủ, tống tiền, trộm cắp, hối lộ, lợi nhuận từ hoạt động phi pháp — giống như các khoản lợi hợp pháp: chúng thuộc tổng thu nhập của năm được nhận dưới quyền chi phối không hạn chế, và Quy định § 1.61-14(a) cũng phát biểu quy tắc thu nhập khác theo nghĩa rộng. Ngược lại, một khoản vay thật sự nằm ngoài thu nhập chính vì đường ranh mà James đã vẽ: tại thời điểm nhận, có sự công nhận đồng thuận về nghĩa vụ hoàn trả. Còn một phần đuôi chỉ nêu hướng, không chốt kết luận: dù thoát trách nhiệm hình sự, về dân sự James vẫn chịu nghĩa vụ kép — phải hoàn trả tiền biển thủ cho công đoàn và công ty bảo hiểm, đồng thời phải nộp thuế thu nhập trên chính số tiền đó. Nếu ông hoàn trả vào một năm sau, các hướng xử lý theo luật hiện hành có thể gồm khấu trừ trong năm hoàn trả hoặc, khi đủ điều kiện, viện dẫn cơ chế cứu trợ claim of right tại Điều 1341; các điều kiện cụ thể phải đối chiếu với luật hiện hành, và bài này không đưa ra kết luận cố định.

Glenshaw Glass Co., 348 U.S. 426 (1955) है, जिसने सकल आय की शास्त्रीय परिभाषा दी: संपत्ति में निर्विवाद वृद्धि, जो स्पष्ट रूप से साकार हो और करदाता के पूर्ण नियंत्रण में हो, साथ ही यह बात कि धारा 61 की भाषा कांग्रेस की संविधान-सीमा तक कर-शक्ति प्रयोग करने की मंशा दिखाती है। चौथी कड़ी James (1961) है, जिसने Glenshaw Glass की व्यापक परिभाषा और Rutkin के परिणाम को जोड़कर Wilcox का सामना किया, गबन किए धन को करयोग्य घोषित किया और Wilcox को उलट दिया। वर्तमान धारा 61(a) के अंतर्गत अवैध लाभ — गबन, रंगदारी, चोरी, रिश्वत, अवैध कारोबार का मुनाफ़ा — वैध लाभ की तरह ही, अप्रतिबंधित नियंत्रण में प्राप्ति के वर्ष में सकल आय में शामिल होते हैं, और विनियम § 1.61-14(a) भी विविध आय का नियम व्यापक रूप से कहता है। इसके विपरीत, वास्तविक ऋण आय से बाहर रहता है ठीक James की खींची रेखा के कारण: प्राप्ति के समय लौटाने के दायित्व की सहमतिपूर्ण स्वीकृति मौजूद होती है। अंत में एक दुमछल्ला केवल दिशा के रूप में रहता है, पक्का निष्कर्ष नहीं: आपराधिक रूप से छूटने पर भी James पर दीवानी दोहरा दायित्व रहा — गबन किया धन यूनियन और बीमा कंपनी को लौटाना था, और उसी धन पर आयकर भी देना था। यदि वह बाद के किसी वर्ष में धन लौटाए, तो वर्तमान कानून में संभावित दिशाओं में लौटाने के वर्ष में कटौती का दावा, या शर्तें पूरी होने पर धारा 1341 के claim of right relief का सहारा शामिल है; विशिष्ट शर्तें वर्तमान कानून से जाँचनी होंगी, और यह लेख कोई निश्चित निष्कर्ष नहीं देता।

## 六、实务与备考要点

### 中文
实务上，先把本案拆成两本账，永远不要混着报。第一本是实体税账：客户取得的钱，只要是他实际支配、像自己的钱一样能花，且取得时没有与付款方经同意承认的偿还安排，先按第 61 条推定计入总收入，来路是否合法不影响定性；侵吞、盗窃、贿赂、非法经营所得概莫能外，入账年度是取得并支配的当年，不是被发现、被起诉或被判归还的那一年。第二本是责任账：客户要不要坐牢、要不要被罚故意，看的是行为当时的法律状态与他的主观认识，不能拿今天的实体标准倒推当年的故意。本案中 James 的结局正是两本账的标本：税要补，罪不成立。

给客户做判断时，问三个问题就够了。一，这笔钱他能不能自由动用、是否按自己的钱在支配。二，取得的那一刻，有没有出借人、雇主或权利人与他共同承认这笔钱要还——有书面借款、明确还款安排的，才往借款那一格放；单方面心里想着以后会还，不算经同意承认。三，这笔钱后来是否被实际追回或归还——这影响的是以后年度的扣除或第 1341 条方向，不影响取得年度的收入定性，按现行法核对后处理。三个问题答完，定性基本不会错。

备考要点收成五条。一，案名与坐标：James v. United States, 366 U.S. 213 (1961)，1961 年 5 月 15 日宣判，推翻 Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946)。二，规则一句话：侵吞款在侵吞年度构成总收入，非法所得不因非法而免税。三，区分标准背原词：consensual recognition of an obligation to repay——有，是借款，不入收入；无，是所得，入收入，这是本案最爱考的一刀。四，意见结构别写错：Warren 意见是三人相对多数（plurality），推翻 Wilcox 共六票，Harlan（Frankfurter 加入）与 Clark 分别协同，Whittaker（Black、Douglas 加入）只同意撤销起诉、反对推翻 Wilcox。五，刑事尾巴单独记：因行为时 Wilcox 有效，故意不成立，定罪撤销并指示撤销起诉——题干若同时问 tax liability 与 criminal liability，两问的答案方向相反，正是出题人埋的点。题干里出现 embezzled funds、embezzlement income、illegal income 该不该报，答案方向基本就是本案。

### English
In practice, keep this case in two separate ledgers and never report them as one. The first is the substantive tax ledger: money a client actually received and could spend as his own, where at receipt there was no consensually recognized repayment arrangement with the payor, is presumptively gross income under Section 61, and the unlawfulness of the source does not change the characterization. Embezzlement, theft, bribes, and profits of illegal activity are all included, and the year of inclusion is the year of receipt and dominion, not the year the conduct is discovered, prosecuted, or ordered repaid. The second is the liability ledger: whether the client faces criminal punishment or a willfulness-based exposure turns on the state of the law and his understanding at the time he acted, and today's substantive standard cannot be projected backward to create yesterday's willfulness. James himself is the specimen of the two ledgers: the tax was owed, the crime was not made out.

Three questions are enough for client work. First, could he use the money freely, exercising dominion as if it were his own? Second, at the moment of receipt, did a lender, employer, or rightful owner jointly acknowledge with him that the money was to be repaid — only a documented loan or a clear repayment arrangement belongs in the loan box, while a private intention someday to repay is not a consensual recognition. Third, was the money later actually recovered or repaid — which affects a possible deduction or the Section 1341 direction in the later year, not the income characterization in the year of receipt, and is handled only after checking current law. Answer those three and the characterization will rarely go wrong.

For REG, five points are worth fixing in memory. (1) Case and citation: James v. United States, 366 U.S. 213 (1961), decided May 15, 1961, overruling Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946). (2) The rule in one sentence: embezzled funds are gross income in the year of embezzlement, and unlawful gains are not exempt merely because they are unlawful. (3) Memorize the distinguishing phrase: consensual recognition of an obligation to repay — if present, the item is a loan and not income; if absent, it is income, and this is the cut examiners love to test. (4) Get the opinion structure right: the Warren opinion is a three-justice plurality; six justices in total voted to overrule Wilcox, with Harlan (joined by Frankfurter) and Clark concurring separately, while Whittaker (joined by Black and Douglas) agreed only to dismiss the indictment and refused to overrule Wilcox. (5) Keep the criminal tail separate: because Wilcox was good law when James acted, willfulness failed, and the conviction was reversed with directions to dismiss the indictment — when a stem asks about both tax liability and criminal liability, the two answers point in opposite directions, which is exactly the trap being set. When a REG stem mentions embezzled funds, embezzlement income, or whether illegal income must be reported, this case supplies the direction of the answer.

### Español (初译待校)
En la práctica, este caso se lleva en dos libros separados. El primero es el libro fiscal sustantivo: el dinero que el cliente recibió y pudo gastar como propio, sin un acuerdo de devolución reconocido consensuadamente con quien lo entregó en el momento de la recepción, se presume ingreso bruto según la sección 61, y la ilicitud de la fuente no cambia su carácter. Malversación, robo, sobornos y beneficios de actividades ilegales se incluyen, y el año de inclusión es el de la recepción y el dominio, no el del descubrimiento ni el de la orden de devolución. El segundo es el libro de responsabilidad: el castigo penal depende del estado del derecho y del conocimiento del autor cuando actuó, y el estándar sustantivo de hoy no puede proyectarse hacia atrás. Para el trabajo con clientes bastan tres preguntas: si pudo usar el dinero libremente como propio; si al recibirlo existía un reconocimiento conjunto de que debía devolverse —solo un préstamo documentado entra en la casilla del préstamo, y la intención privada de devolver algún día no es reconocimiento consensuado—; y si el dinero fue después recuperado o devuelto, lo que afecta a una posible deducción o a la vía de la sección 1341 en el año posterior, verificada en la ley vigente, no a la calificación del año de recepción. Para REG, cinco puntos: (1) James v. United States, 366 U.S. 213 (1961), decidido el 15 de mayo de 1961, que revocó Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946); (2) la regla: los fondos malversados son ingreso bruto en el año de la malversación; (3) la frase distintiva, consensual recognition of an obligation to repay: si existe, es préstamo y no ingreso; si no existe, es ingreso; (4) la estructura de opiniones: la de Warren es una pluralidad de tres, la revocación de Wilcox reunió seis votos, con las concurrencias separadas de Harlan (con Frankfurter) y Clark, mientras Whittaker (con Black y Douglas) solo aceptó desestimar la acusación y se negó a revocar Wilcox; y (5) la cola penal: al estar Wilcox vigente cuando James actuó, no hubo intencionalidad, y la condena fue revocada con instrucciones de desestimar la acusación.

### 한국어 (初译待校)
실무에서는 이 사건을 두 개의 장부로 나누어 다뤄야 한다. 첫째는 실체적 세무 장부이다. 고객이 실제로 받아 자기 돈처럼 쓸 수 있었던 돈으로, 받는 시점에 지급자와의 사이에 합의된 반환 약정이 없었다면, 제61조 아래 총소득으로 추정되며 출처가 불법이라는 사정은 성격을 바꾸지 않는다. 횡령, 절도, 뇌물, 불법 영업의 이익이 모두 포함되고, 귀속 연도는 적발이나 기소, 반환 명령의 해가 아니라 취득하여 지배한 해이다. 둘째는 책임 장부이다. 형사처벌 여부는 행위 당시의 법 상태와 행위자의 인식으로 판단하며, 오늘의 실체 기준을 거슬러 적용해 어제의 고의를 만들 수는 없다. 고객 판단에는 세 가지 질문이면 충분하다. 그 돈을 자기 돈처럼 자유롭게 쓸 수 있었는가. 받는 순간, 대여자·고용주·권리자가 그와 함께 반환해야 할 돈임을 인정했는가 — 문서화된 차입만이 차입의 칸에 들어가고, 언젠가 갚겠다는 일방적 속생각은 합의된 인정이 아니다. 그 돈이 나중에 실제로 회수되거나 반환되었는가 — 이는 취득 연도의 소득 성격이 아니라 이후 연도의 공제나 제1341조 방향에 영향을 주며, 현행법을 확인한 뒤 처리한다. (5) 형사적 꼬리: 행위 당시 Wilcox가 유효했으므로 고의가 성립하지 않아 유죄가 파기되고 기소 기각 지시가 내려졌다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)
Trong thực hành, hãy giữ vụ án này trong hai sổ riêng. Sổ thứ nhất là sổ thuế thực chất: khoản tiền khách hàng thực sự nhận được và có thể tiêu như tiền của mình, mà tại thời điểm nhận không có thỏa thuận hoàn trả được đồng thuận công nhận với bên giao tiền, thì được suy đoán là tổng thu nhập theo Điều 61, và nguồn bất hợp pháp không làm thay đổi tính chất. Biển thủ, trộm cắp, hối lộ và lợi nhuận từ hoạt động phi pháp đều thuộc diện này, và năm tính thu nhập là năm nhận và chi phối, không phải năm bị phát hiện, truy tố hay bị buộc hoàn trả. Sổ thứ hai là sổ trách nhiệm: hình phạt hình sự phụ thuộc vào tình trạng pháp luật và nhận thức của người hành động tại thời điểm hành động, và tiêu chuẩn thực chất hôm nay không thể chiếu ngược về quá khứ. Khi tư vấn, ba câu hỏi là đủ: ông ta có thể tự do dùng số tiền như tiền của mình không; tại khoảnh khắc nhận, có người cho vay, chủ lao động hay chủ sở hữu nào cùng ông ta công nhận rằng số tiền phải hoàn trả không — chỉ khoản vay có giấy tờ mới vào ô tiền vay, còn ý định riêng sẽ trả một ngày nào đó không phải là sự công nhận đồng thuận; và số tiền sau đó có thực tế bị thu hồi hay được hoàn trả không — điều này ảnh hưởng tới khả năng khấu trừ hoặc hướng Điều 1341 ở năm sau, sau khi đối chiếu luật hiện hành, chứ không ảnh hưởng tới việc xác định thu nhập ở năm nhận. Để ôn REG, ghi nhớ năm điểm: (1) James v. United States, 366 U.S. 213 (1961), phán quyết ngày 15 tháng 5 năm 1961, lật bỏ Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946); (2) quy tắc: tiền biển thủ là tổng thu nhập trong năm biển thủ; (3) cụm từ phân biệt cần thuộc nguyên văn: consensual recognition of an obligation to repay — nếu có, đó là khoản vay và không phải thu nhập; nếu không, đó là thu nhập; (4) cấu trúc ý kiến: ý kiến của Warren là plurality gồm ba thẩm phán, việc lật bỏ Wilcox có tổng cộng sáu phiếu, với Harlan (Frankfurter tham gia) và Clark đồng tình riêng, trong khi Whittaker (Black và Douglas tham gia) chỉ đồng ý bác bỏ cáo trạng và phản đối lật bỏ Wilcox; và (5) phần đuôi hình sự: vì Wilcox còn hiệu lực khi James hành động nên yếu tố cố ý không thành lập, bản án kết tội bị hủy kèm chỉ thị bác bỏ cáo trạng.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)
व्यवहार में इस मामले को दो अलग बहियों में रखना चाहिए। पहली मूल कर-बही है: जो धन ग्राहक ने वास्तव में प्राप्त किया और अपने धन की तरह खर्च कर सका, और जिसकी प्राप्ति के समय देने वाले के साथ लौटाने का कोई सहमतिपूर्ण स्वीकृत प्रबंध नहीं था, वह धारा 61 के अंतर्गत सकल आय माना जाएगा, और स्रोत की अवैधता उसका चरित्र नहीं बदलती। गबन, चोरी, रिश्वत और अवैध गतिविधियों का लाभ सभी शामिल हैं, और समावेश का वर्ष प्राप्ति और नियंत्रण का वर्ष है, पकड़े जाने या लौटाने के आदेश का वर्ष नहीं। दूसरी उत्तरदायित्व-बही है: आपराधिक दंड कार्य के समय के कानून और कर्ता की समझ पर निर्भर करता है; आज का मूल मानदंड पीछे लगाकर कल का जानबूझपन नहीं बनाया जा सकता। ग्राहक के लिए तीन प्रश्न पर्याप्त हैं: क्या वह धन को अपने धन की तरह स्वतंत्र रूप से उपयोग कर सकता था; प्राप्ति के क्षण क्या ऋणदाता, नियोक्ता या स्वामी ने उसके साथ यह स्वीकार किया था कि धन लौटाना है — केवल दस्तावेज़ी ऋण ही ऋण वाले ख़ाने में आता है, और किसी दिन लौटाने का निजी इरादा सहमतिपूर्ण स्वीकृति नहीं है; और क्या धन बाद में वास्तव में वसूला या लौटाया गया — यह प्राप्ति के वर्ष की आय-प्रकृति को नहीं, बाद के वर्ष में संभावित कटौती या धारा 1341 की दिशा को प्रभावित करता है, और वर्तमान कानून जाँचकर ही निपटाया जाता है। REG के लिए पाँच बिंदु याद रखें: (1) James v. United States, 366 U.S. 213 (1961), 15 मई 1961 को निर्णीत, जिसने Commissioner v. Wilcox, 327 U.S. 404 (1946) को उलट दिया; (2) नियम: गबन किया धन गबन के वर्ष की सकल आय है; (3) भेदक वाक्यांश मूल शब्दों में याद करें: consensual recognition of an obligation to repay — यदि यह मौजूद है तो मद ऋण है और आय नहीं, यदि नहीं है तो आय है; (4) रायों की संरचना: Warren की राय तीन न्यायाधीशों की plurality है, Wilcox को उलटने के पक्ष में कुल छह मत थे, Harlan (Frankfurter सहित) और Clark अलग-अलग सहमत हुए, जबकि Whittaker (Black और Douglas सहित) केवल अभियोग खारिज करने पर सहमत हुए और Wilcox उलटने का विरोध किया; और (5) आपराधिक दुमछल्ला: जब James ने कार्य किया, Wilcox लागू था, इसलिए जानबूझपन नहीं बना, दोषसिद्धि उलटी गई और अभियोग खारिज करने के निर्देश दिए गए।
