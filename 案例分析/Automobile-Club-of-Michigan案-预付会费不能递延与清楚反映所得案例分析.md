# Automobile Club of Michigan v. Commissioner, 353 U.S. 180 (1957) 案例分析：预付的会费，为什么不能摊着慢慢算收入

> 板块：案例分析｜案名：Automobile Club of Michigan v. Commissioner, 353 U.S. 180 (1957)，美国联邦最高法院，1957 年判决。本篇经 ls 文件名加 grep 正文双查：Automobile Club 在案例板块零命中、无本体专篇；与 Schlude 案、Artnell 案同属预付收入递延家族，本篇管源头那一格——服务义务没有固定履约表时，递延连门都进不去。

## 中文主文

### 一、案情：会费先收了，俱乐部想按月份把收入摊开

密歇根汽车俱乐部向会员预收会费，会员交一笔钱，换的是会籍期间内随时可用的道路救援等服务。俱乐部在账上把预收会费按会籍月份分摊、逐月转收入，报税也跟着这样报：今年只报今年摊到的那部分，剩下的算递延。国税局不认，要求会费在收到年度全额计入收入。争议的骨架很干净：钱已经实实在在收到手、支配权在俱乐部一边，唯一的争点是服务还没提供完，能不能把税法上的收入确认也跟着服务进度往后推。俱乐部的主张本质上是把会计上的配比搬进税法：收入跟着义务走，义务没尽，收入未完。

### 二、判决：没有固定履约安排，递延不被接受，收到即所得

最高法院支持国税局。推理分两步。第一步是请求权规则（claim of right）：纳税人收到款项、对其有完全支配、没有返还义务的刚性限制时，收到年度即构成所得，不因日后可能要提供服务而改变定性。第二步是方法关门：税法第 446 条要求会计方法清楚反映所得，俱乐部这套按月分摊的问题在于，会员何时用服务、用多少，全凭会员决定，俱乐部对每位会员的未来服务义务既没有固定时间表，也没有可按年切分的确定进度——分摊的比例是人为估出来的，不是义务本身长出来的。用算例把差别摆明（自编教学数字，本地复算一致）：某会员一次预付 12 个月会费 120 美元，俱乐部账面每月转 120 除以 12 等于 10 美元收入；税法口径下，收到年度先认全额 120 美元，往后各月不再重复计。两种口径全年总数相同，差的只是时间——而时间正是本案的全部争点。其后 1961 年的 American Automobile Association v. United States, 367 U.S. 687 (1961) 沿同一逻辑再确认：全国性汽车协会的预付会费同样不能这样递延。

### 三、在家族里的位置：与 Schlude、Artnell 怎么分工

预付收入这一家族要连起来记。Schlude v. Commissioner, 372 U.S. 128 (1963) 管舞蹈教室预收学费，服务次数同样由学员决定，递延被否。Artnell Co. v. Commissioner, 400 F.2d 981 (7th Cir. 1968) 是家族里的例外口子：棒球队预售下一年度季票，比赛日期在收款时已经固定排定，法院允许把收入递延到比赛实际举行的年度——义务的时间表是死的，递延才有落点。三案并排，规则的正反面就齐了：履约时间与内容在收款时已确定到可按期切分的，递延有讨论空间；全凭客户日后决定用不用、何时用的，收到即所得。再往后，国会用第 451 条第 (c) 款给权责发生制纳税人开了一道成文窄门：预收款原则上收到即计，但可选择把与财务报表收入确认挂钩的部分递延一小段期间——这是立法后来补的缓和，不改变本案在普通法一侧的源头地位。考试给到预付事实，先问一句履约表定没定死，再决定往哪一格放。

### 四、加州口径

加州所得税对收入确认大体跟随联邦框架，预付收入的州层处理以《收入与税法典》与 FTB 当期口径逐项核对，不另设一套独立的递延时钟。本案的实务落点在日常一侧：收会员费、订阅费、服务包预付款的小企业，账面递延与税法确认是两套口径，报税时要做书税差异调整，别把账上的递延收入直接抄进申报表。

## English

Automobile Club of Michigan v. Commissioner, 353 U.S. 180 (1957). The club collected membership dues in advance; members paid once for road services available on demand throughout the membership period. On its books the club spread the dues over the membership months and reported income the same way. The Supreme Court sided with the Commissioner on two steps. First, the claim-of-right rule: money received under a claim of right, with unrestricted dominion and no fixed obligation to repay, is income in the year received even though services remain to be performed. Second, the method failed section 446's clear-reflection test because when and how much each member would use the services was entirely up to the member — the club had no fixed schedule of performance to which a ratable allocation could attach, so the monthly spread was an estimate, not the shape of the obligation itself. A teaching example shows the only real difference is timing (verified locally): a 120-dollar prepaid twelve-month membership is 120 divided by 12 equals 10 dollars a month on the books, but the full 120 dollars is income in the year of receipt for tax, with nothing counted again later. American Automobile Association v. United States, 367 U.S. 687 (1961), confirmed the same result for a national club. Within the prepaid-income family: Schlude v. Commissioner, 372 U.S. 128 (1963), denied deferral for dance-studio fees for the same reason, while Artnell Co. v. Commissioner, 400 F.2d 981 (7th Cir. 1968), allowed deferral where baseball season tickets were sold for games whose dates were already fixed — performance pinned to a schedule is what makes deferral arguable. Congress later added a narrow statutory easing in section 451(c) for accrual-method taxpayers, letting part of an advance payment follow financial-statement recognition for a limited period, without displacing this case as the common-law source. California note: California largely follows the federal income-recognition framework, so a small business collecting dues, subscriptions, or service prepayments must keep book deferral and tax recognition apart and adjust for the difference on the return.

## Español (traducción de estudio, 初译待校)

Automobile Club of Michigan v. Commissioner, 353 U.S. 180 (1957). El club cobraba por anticipado las cuotas de membresía: el socio pagaba una vez por servicios de asistencia en carretera disponibles a demanda durante todo el período. En su contabilidad el club repartía las cuotas entre los meses de membresía y declaraba el ingreso igual. La Corte Suprema dio la razón al Comisionado en dos pasos. Primero, la regla del claim of right: el dinero recibido con dominio pleno y sin obligación fija de devolución es ingreso en el año de cobro, aunque queden servicios por prestar. Segundo, el método no superaba la prueba de reflejo claro del artículo 446, porque cuándo y cuánto usaría cada socio los servicios dependía por entero del socio: el club no tenía un calendario fijo de prestación al que anclar el reparto mensual, de modo que el reparto era una estimación, no la forma de la propia obligación. Un ejemplo didáctico muestra que la única diferencia real es el momento (verificado localmente): una membresía prepagada de 120 dólares por doce meses son 120 entre 12 igual a 10 dólares al mes en libros, pero fiscalmente los 120 dólares son ingreso en el año de cobro y nada se cuenta otra vez después. American Automobile Association v. United States, 367 U.S. 687 (1961), confirmó el mismo resultado para un club nacional. En la familia del ingreso anticipado: Schlude v. Commissioner, 372 U.S. 128 (1963), negó el diferimiento de cuotas de una academia de baile por la misma razón, mientras Artnell Co. v. Commissioner, 400 F.2d 981 (7th Cir. 1968), lo permitió cuando los abonos de béisbol se vendían para partidos con fechas ya fijadas: una prestación atada a un calendario es lo que hace defendible el diferimiento. El Congreso añadió después una atenuación legal estrecha en el artículo 451(c) para contribuyentes en base devengada. Nota de California: California sigue en lo esencial el marco federal de reconocimiento de ingresos, así que quien cobra cuotas, suscripciones o anticipos de servicios debe separar el diferimiento contable del reconocimiento fiscal y ajustar la diferencia en la declaración.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

- Ghép nguyên tắc phù hợp vào luật thuế: lập luận của câu lạc bộ về bản chất là mang nguyên tắc phù hợp (matching) của kế toán vào luật thuế — thu nhập đi theo nghĩa vụ, nghĩa vụ chưa xong thì thu nhập chưa xong — và tòa đã không chấp nhận cách ghép này.
- Chi tiết cửa hẹp Điều 451(c): cửa hẹp luật định này là một lựa chọn; người nộp thuế theo cơ sở dồn tích chỉ có thể hoãn phần gắn với ghi nhận doanh thu trên báo cáo tài chính áp dụng, và chỉ trong một khoảng thời gian ngắn. Đây là nới lỏng do luật về sau bổ sung, không thay đổi vị thế nguồn gốc thông luật của vụ án. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, đang chờ hiệu đính)

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

- मिलान सिद्धांत का कर कानून में प्रत्यारोपण: क्लब का तर्क मूलतः लेखांकन के मिलान (matching) सिद्धांत को कर कानून में लाना था — आय दायित्व के पीछे चलती है, दायित्व अधूरा तो आय भी अधूरी — और न्यायालय ने यह प्रत्यारोपण स्वीकार नहीं किया।
- धारा 451(c) के संकीर्ण द्वार का विवरण: यह वैधानिक संकीर्ण द्वार एक चुनाव है; उपार्जन-आधार करदाता केवल वही भाग स्थगित कर सकता है जो लागू वित्तीय विवरण की आय-पहचान से जुड़ा हो, और वह भी केवल छोटी अवधि के लिए। यह बाद में आई वैधानिक राहत है और मामले की सामान्य-विधि स्रोत स्थिति को नहीं बदलती। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा प्रतीक्षित है)
