# Tyler 案：欠税卖房，多卖出来的钱政府不能自己留——598 U.S. 631 (2023) 征收条款案例分析

案例：Tyler v. Hennepin County, Minnesota, 598 U.S. 631 (2023)，美国联邦最高法院，首席大法官 Roberts 执笔、全体一致，2023 年 5 月 25 日判决，推翻第八巡回 26 F.4th 789 (2022)。

## 一、中文主文

### 1. 这篇讲什么

政府可以为欠税卖掉您的房子，但只能拿走欠它的那一份；卖多了，多出来的钱（超额款，surplus equity）还是原房主的财产。明尼苏达州的旧制度偏偏规定：县把欠税房产没收出售后，超出税债的售房款归县、镇与学区分掉，原房主一分拿不回。Tyler 案里，94 岁的 Geraldine Tyler 欠的房产税连同利息、罚则合计约 15,000 美元，县把她的公寓卖了 40,000 美元，把多出的 25,000 美元全部留下。最高法院全体一致认定：她至少已经可信地主张（plausibly alleges）县留置这 25,000 美元违反了联邦宪法第五修正案的征收条款（Takings Clause）——政府可以卖房抵债，但不能借税债之名没收比债务更多的财产。本篇管判例本体；卖掉之后超额款具体怎么申领，见本仓常用财税常识同题一篇。

### 2. 案情

Tyler 1999 年在 Minneapolis 买下一套一居室公寓并独居十多年，2010 年搬进老年公寓后停缴该公寓的房产税。欠税本金约 2,300 美元，连同利息与罚则滚到约 15,000 美元。明尼苏达的程序是：欠税满一年转逾期，之后有三年赎回期，期满州取得绝对产权，可自用或出售；出售所得超出税债与出售成本的部分，按 Minn. Stat. 第 282.08 条由县等地方主体留存，原房主没有任何追回通道。县于 2015 年没收公寓并以 40,000 美元售出，15,000 美元税债就此消灭，剩下的 25,000 美元县里全部留下自用。

Tyler 以集体诉讼起诉，主张两条：留置超额款违反第五修正案征收条款；没收远超债务的价值构成第八修正案的过度罚金（Excessive Fines）。地区法院驳回起诉（505 F. Supp. 3d 879），第八巡回维持（26 F.4th 789 (2022)），理由是州法既然不承认税款拍卖超额款上的财产利益，就谈不上征收。最高法院发调卷令后推翻第八巡回。

### 3. 裁判理由

第一，超额款是「财产」。一项价值算不算征收条款保护的财产，要看州法、传统财产法原则、历史实践与本院先例合起来怎么说。历史一线很硬：政府不得从纳税人身上拿走超过其所欠的数额，这一原则可溯至《大宪章》以来的英国法传统；今天绝大多数州与联邦政府在卖房抵税后都把超额款退还纳税人。明尼苏达自家法律在许多其他场合也承认债务人对超额价值的权利。州不能靠在自家法典里写一句「超额款不算房主的」，就把宪法上的财产定义掉。

第二，拿走超额款是典型的直接征收。县有权卖房收回 15,000 美元税债，这一点法院从未否认；越界的是后一步——用税债作杠杆，把不属于债务的 25,000 美元也据为己有。这是政府直接把私人财产拨归自用的经典征收形态，没有公正补偿就不成立。

第三，两个抗辩都被挡回。其一，「放弃」抗辩：县说 Tyler 停缴税款、搬离，等于建设性放弃房屋。法院说，明尼苏达的没收制度关心的是欠没欠税、不是房屋有没有被使用，欠税不等于放弃财产，不能把不缴税重新包装成放弃来绕开征收条款。其二，先例 Nelson v. City of New York, 352 U.S. 103 (1956) 被区分开：纽约那套制度给房主留了在期限内申领超额款的机会，房主自己没去领才丧失；明尼苏达制度根本不给任何追回机会，两案不是一回事。

第四，过度罚金一条本案没有裁断。法院只用征收条款就解决了案件，第八修正案问题留待下级法院；Gorsuch 大法官的协同意见（Jackson 大法官加入）则主张那条线也应认真对待。程序口径也要读准：本案处在驳回起诉的复审阶段，判决用语是 Tyler「可信地主张」了征收，案件发回继续审理，不是当庭判县赔 25,000 美元。

### 4. 算例：三笔钱怎么分（本案数字）

售房款 40,000 美元，税债（欠税加利息、罚则）约 15,000 美元，超额款 = 40,000 − 15,000 = 25,000 美元。按本案规则：15,000 美元县里拿得正当；25,000 美元是 Tyler 的财产，全部留置即构成征收争议的标的。再换一组教学数字对读：若售房款 60,000 美元、税债与合法成本合计 18,000 美元，超额款 = 60,000 − 18,000 = 42,000 美元，政府可留的仍只有债务那一份。两组数字已用整数与 Decimal 两路复算，结果一致。

### 5. 备考与实务启示

备考抓手：第一，征收条款的门不是「政府能不能征税」，而是「能不能拿超所欠」——征税权与征收的界线就画在债务数额上。第二，「财产」定义是州法加传统原则加历史的合成题，州立法不能单方面定义掉联邦宪法权利。第三，Nelson 的区分点是「有没有给申领机会」：给了机会、自己过期没领，与压根不给机会，宪法评价相反——这也是各州在 Tyler 之后修法时普遍改走「超额款申领制」的原因。

实务上，本案不免除任何一分税债，也不挡县卖房；它挡的是卖房之后的留置。家里有长辈欠房产税、房屋进入 tax-defaulted 程序的，先走赎回与缴清，再盯出售后的超额款申领期限（各州规则不同，加州口径见同题常识篇，B 级）。另注意射程：本案针对的是政府卖房抵债后留置超额款，私人抵押权止赎的超额款本来就归债务人，不在本案射程内。

## 二、English

Case: Tyler v. Hennepin County, Minnesota, 598 U.S. 631 (2023) (unanimous; Roberts, C.J.; decided May 25, 2023), reversing 26 F.4th 789 (8th Cir. 2022).

Geraldine Tyler, 94, bought a Minneapolis condominium in 1999 and stopped paying its property taxes after moving to a senior residence in 2010. The unpaid taxes were about $2,300; with interest and penalties the debt came to about $15,000. Under Minnesota's scheme (Minn. Stat. sections 281.18, 282.07, 282.08), the county seized the condo, sold it in 2015 for $40,000, extinguished the $15,000 debt — and kept the remaining $25,000 for itself, with no mechanism for the former owner to recover any surplus. Tyler sued under the Takings Clause of the Fifth Amendment and the Excessive Fines Clause of the Eighth Amendment. The district court dismissed (505 F. Supp. 3d 879) and the Eighth Circuit affirmed, reasoning that state law recognized no property interest in the surplus.

The Supreme Court reversed. The surplus equity is property: whether remaining value from a tax sale is protected property is answered by state law together with traditional property-law principles, historical practice, and precedent, and history — traceable to Magna Carta — dictates that a government may not take from a taxpayer more than she owes; most states and the federal government return the excess. A state cannot define the property interest away by statute. Retaining the $25,000 was a classic taking in which the government directly appropriates private property for its own use: the county could sell the home to recover the $15,000, but it could not use the tax debt to confiscate more. The Court rejected the county's constructive-abandonment argument (nonpayment is not abandonment) and distinguished Nelson v. City of New York, 352 U.S. 103 (1956), where the scheme gave owners an opportunity to recover the surplus and the owners simply failed to claim it. Because the Takings Clause resolved the case, the Court did not reach the Excessive Fines claim; Justice Gorsuch, joined by Justice Jackson, concurred and would have taken that claim seriously. Note the posture: reviewing a dismissal, the Court held Tyler plausibly alleges a taking and remanded.

Worked figures: $40,000 sale price − $15,000 tax debt = $25,000 surplus. A second teaching example: $60,000 − $18,000 of debt and lawful costs = $42,000 surplus, of which the government may keep only the debt portion. Both computations were verified two ways.

## 三、Español (初译待校)

Caso: Tyler v. Hennepin County, Minnesota, 598 U.S. 631 (2023) (unanime; ponente el Presidente Roberts; 25 de mayo de 2023), que revoca 26 F.4th 789 (8.º Cir. 2022).

Geraldine Tyler, de 94 años, dejó de pagar el impuesto predial de su condominio de Minneapolis; la deuda —unos 2.300 dólares de impuesto, cerca de 15.000 con intereses y sanciones— llevó al condado a incautar y vender la vivienda por 40.000 dólares en 2015, quedándose con los 25.000 dólares excedentes sin ningún mecanismo de devolución. El Tribunal Supremo sostuvo que el excedente es propiedad protegida por la Cláusula de Expropiación de la Quinta Enmienda: el gobierno puede vender la casa para cobrar lo debido, pero no puede confiscar más de lo que se le debe; un Estado no puede eliminar por ley esa propiedad, y no pagar impuestos no equivale a abandonar la vivienda. Se distinguió Nelson v. City of New York, 352 U.S. 103 (1956), donde sí existía una oportunidad de reclamar el excedente. La reclamación por multas excesivas no se resolvió. Cifras: 40.000 − 15.000 = 25.000 de excedente; en un ejemplo docente, 60.000 − 18.000 = 42.000.

## 五、Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Án lệ: Tyler v. Hennepin County, Minnesota, 598 U.S. 631 (2023) (Tòa án Tối cao Hoa Kỳ, nhất trí toàn thể, Chánh án Roberts, tuyên ngày 25 tháng 5 năm 2023), đảo ngược 26 F.4th 789 (Tòa Phúc thẩm Vòng 8, 2022).

Bà Geraldine Tyler, 94 tuổi, ngừng nộp thuế bất động sản cho căn hộ chung cư ở Minneapolis; khoản thuế khoảng 2.300 đô la, cộng lãi và phạt thành khoảng 15.000 đô la. Quận tịch thu và bán căn hộ năm 2015 với giá 40.000 đô la, giữ toàn bộ 25.000 đô la thặng dư mà không có bất kỳ cơ chế hoàn trả nào. Tòa Tối cao phán rằng phần thặng dư là tài sản được Tu chính án thứ Năm, điều khoản trưng dụng, bảo vệ: chính quyền có thể bán nhà để thu khoản nợ, nhưng không thể tịch thu nhiều hơn số nợ; một bang không thể dùng luật để định nghĩa mất quyền tài sản đó, và việc không nộp thuế không phải là từ bỏ tài sản. ## 六、हिन्दी (初译待校)

मामला: Tyler v. Hennepin County, Minnesota, 598 U.S. 631 (2023) (अमेरिकी सर्वोच्च न्यायालय, सर्वसम्मत, मुख्य न्यायाधीश Roberts, 25 मई 2023), 26 F.4th 789 (8th Cir. 2022) को उलटते हुए।

94 वर्षीय Geraldine Tyler ने Minneapolis के अपने condominium का संपत्ति कर देना बंद कर दिया; कर लगभग 2,300 डॉलर था, ब्याज व दंड सहित ऋण लगभग 15,000 डॉलर हो गया। काउंटी ने 2015 में मकान ज़ब्त कर 40,000 डॉलर में बेचा और 25,000 डॉलर अधिशेष बिना किसी वापसी-प्रक्रिया के रख लिया। सर्वोच्च न्यायालय ने कहा कि यह अधिशेष पाँचवें संशोधन के Takings Clause द्वारा संरक्षित संपत्ति है: सरकार ऋण वसूलने के लिए घर बेच सकती है, परंतु देय से अधिक ज़ब्त नहीं कर सकती; कोई राज्य कानून बनाकर उस संपत्ति-हित को समाप्त नहीं कर सकता, और कर न चुकाना संपत्ति का परित्याग नहीं है। Nelson v. City of New York, 352 U.S. 103 (1956) से भेद किया गया क्योंकि वहाँ अधिशेष माँगने का अवसर मौजूद था। अत्यधिक जुर्माने का दावा निर्णीत नहीं हुआ। गणना: 40,000 − 15,000 = 25,000 डॉलर अधिशेष; शिक्षण उदाहरण: 60,000 − 18,000 = 42,000 डॉलर।
